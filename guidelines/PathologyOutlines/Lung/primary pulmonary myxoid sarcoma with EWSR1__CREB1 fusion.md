Definition / general

  * Low grade tumor 
  * Typically in (or close to) large airways 
  * Lobulated tumor with spindle and stellate cells arranged in lace-like strands and cords within prominent myxoid stroma 

Essential features

  * Endobronchial nodular mass with reticular / lace-like growth pattern and abundant myxoid stroma 
  * Expresses EMA and vimentin, negative for most other markers 
  * FISH positive for _EWSR1_ rearrangement (85%) 

Terminology

  * Primary pulmonary myxoid sarcoma 

ICD coding

  * ICD-O: [8842/3](https://seer.cancer.gov/archive/icd-o-3/errata.sitetype.20180117.pdf#page=2) \- primary pulmonary myxoid sarcoma 
  * ICD-10: [C34.9](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/C00-D49/C30-C39/C34-/C34.9) \- malignant primary bronchiogenic / bronchogenic neoplasm 

Epidemiology

  * Mean age = 46.3 years; F:M = 1.4:1 (24 cases) 
  * 40% with cough, hemoptysis, chest pain; 20% present as incidental lung mass ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1722](21997693)) 

Sites

  * Bilateral lungs, 76% within or close to a large bronchus ([Int J Surg Pathol 2017;25:518](28449608)) 

Pathophysiology

  * Probably originates from primitive mesenchymal cells with myofibroblastic or fibroblastic differentiation ([Virchows Arch 2014;465:453](25134518)) 
  * Controversial relationship to myxoid [angiomatoid fibrous histiocytoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/softtissuefhangiomatoid.html) with similar _EWSR1_ fusion genes ([Histopathology 2014;65:144](24372335)) 
  * Primary pulmonary myxoid sarcoma (PPMS) and myxoid variant of angiomatoid fibrous histiocytoma may represent a continuum with overlapping histologic, immunohistochemical and genetic features ([Histopathology 2014;65:144](24372335), [Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:1535](32773530)) 

Etiology

  * Smoking history in 80% ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1722](21997693)) 

Diagnosis

  * Relies on histology, immunohistochemistry and FISH in biopsy or surgical resection 

Radiology description

  * Mass related to bronchus, predominantly endobronchial

Prognostic factors

  * Patients with _EWSR1_ rearrangement have favorable prognosis; wild type _EWSR1_ portends poor clinical outcome ([Pol J Pathol 2017;68:261](29363919)) 

Case reports

  * 28 year old men and 66 year old woman with features overlapping with angiomatoid fibrous histiocytoma ([Histopathology 2014;65:144](24372335)) 
  * 29 year old woman with mass in a major fissure of the left lung without parenchymal invasion ([Thorac Cancer 2017;8:535](28722216)) 
  * 32 year old woman with tumor adjacent to the right main bronchus ([Pathology 2017;49:792](29110880)) 
  * 44 and 49 year old men with both components of PPMS and angiomatoid fibrous histiocytoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:1535](32773530)) 
  * 48 year old man with a huge mass extending into the right main bronchus ([Pol J Pathol 2017;68:261](29363919)) 
  * 80 year old woman with an endobronchial mass in the left main bronchus ([Int J Surg Pathol 2017;25:518](28449608)) 

Treatment

  * Surgical resection ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1722](21997693)) 

Gross description

  * Generally < 4 cm, well circumscribed or nodular 
  * Glistening or gelatinous cut surface, white-gray to yellow ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1722](21997693)) 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Lobulated architecture, reticular or lace-like pattern with anastomosing cords and strands, abundant myxoid stroma, often lightly basophilic ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1722](21997693)) 
  * Oval, spindle to polygonal cells with minimal to moderate atypia, infrequent mitotic figures with the majority < 5/10 high power fields 
  * Admixed lymphoplasmacytic infiltrate 
  * Some cells are chondrocyte-like or mimicking physaliferous cells in chordoma ([Diagn Pathol 2020;15:15](32039736))

Electron microscopy description

  * Focal dense plaques present in the plasma membrane, external lamina and intermediate junctions are rarely observed ([Virchows Arch 2014;465:453](25134518)) 

Molecular / cytogenetics description

  * ~85% of tumors showed t(2;22)(q33;q12) with _EWSR1::CREB1_ fusion gene detected by FISH or PCR ([Int J Surg Pathol 2017;25:518](28449608)) 
  * First report of a case with _EWSR1::ATF1_ fusion gene ([Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:1535](32773530)) 
    * Second case ([Int J Surg Pathol 2022 Apr 24 [Epub ahead of print]](35466725)) 

Sample pathology report

  * Lung, right lower lobe, lobectomy: 
    * Primary pulmonary myxoid sarcoma (see comment) 
    * Comment: H&E sections demonstrate a well circumscribed endobronchial tumor composed of lobules of spindled and stellate cells arranged in a reticular pattern within a myxoid background. A chronic inflammatory infiltrate is seen throughout the tumor. Immunohistochemically, the tumor cells are positive for EMA and negative for AE1 / AE3, CAM 5.2, pancytokeratin, p40, desmin, SMA, CD34, TTF1, KIT, vimentin, CD1a and S100. A FISH test for _EWSR1_ was positive for rearrangement in the tumor cells. The morphology in combination with the FISH test result confirm the above diagnosis.

Practice question #1

Which of the following is a good prognostic factor for this primary pulmonary lung lesion?  
  
  1. Brisk lymphoplasmacytic infiltrate 
  2. FISH positive for _EWSR1_ translocation 
  3. Lack of tumor necrosis 
  4. Middle age onset 
  5. Size > 5 cm 

Practice answer #1

**B.** FISH positive for _EWSR1_ translocation. Primary pulmonary myxoid sarcoma tumors (shown in photo) with _EWSR1_ rearrangement have a relatively good prognosis.
