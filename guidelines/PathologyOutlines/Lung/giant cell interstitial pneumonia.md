## Interstitial lung diseases

### Giant cell interstitial pneumonia

**PubMed search** : [giant cell interstitial pneumonia [title]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/?term=giant+cell+interstitial+pneumonia+%5Btitle%5D)

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lungnontumorgiantcellpneumonia.html#definitiongeneral42143) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lungnontumorgiantcellpneumonia.html#microscopichistologicdescription42144)

Definition / general

  * Rare; currently not considered a form of idiopathic interstitial pneumonia 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Intra-alveolar multinucleated giant cells and other inflammatory cells 
  * Giant cells often phagocytose other histiocytes
