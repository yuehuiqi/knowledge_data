Definition / general

  * Podoplanin is a 40 kDa, transmembrane, oncofetal, O linked sialoglycoprotein (mucin type) found on lymphatic endothelium, mesothelium and fetal testis (also other cells as indicated [below](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsD240.html#stainsD240positivestaining)) 
  * D2-40 is a monoclonal antibody that reacts to podoplanin 
  * Podoplanin expression is encoded by the _PDPN_ gene (1p36.21) and regulated by the _PROX1_ gene 

Essential features

  * D2-40 is a monoclonal antibody that reacts to podoplanin showing membranous staining 
  * Mostly used to show lymphatics (e.g. lymphovascular invasion) and lymphatic differentiation in vascular tumors 
  * Also useful as a part of IHC panel in mesothelioma versus adenocarcinoma and seminoma versus nonseminoma differentiation 

Terminology

  * Podoplanin is also known as Aggrus, OTS8, gp36, M2A and T1A2 

Pathophysiology

  * Increases endothelial cell adhesion, migration and tube formation 
  * Platelet aggregation (Aggrus) 
  * Barrier function in high endothelial venules 
  * Maintenance of physical elasticity of lymph nodes 
  * Reference: [OMIM: Podoplanin; PDPN [Accessed 28 October 2021]](https://omim.org/entry/608863)

Interpretation

  * Membranous staining ([Adv Anat Pathol 2006;13:83](16670463)) 
  * Dot-like (e.g. ependymoma) ([Clin Neuropathol 2009;28:373](19788053)) 

Uses by pathologists

  * Limited use because of lack of specificity; most efficient in IHC when used in an IHC panel 
  * To determine presence of lymphatics in various conditions ([Am J Surg Pathol 2015;39:1511](26371781)) 
  * Confirmation of lymphovascular invasion ([Ann Diagn Pathol 2009;13:168](19433295), [Mod Pathol 2007;20:183](17206106), [Mod Pathol 2009;22:216](18820667), [Int J Colorectal Dis 2009;24:1069](19387662), [J Cutan Pathol 2009;36:1157](19222695), [Arch Dermatol 2008;144:462](18427039), [Hum Pathol 2008;39:901](18440591), [Am J Surg Pathol 1989;13:177](2919716)) 
  * Dermatopathology, bone and soft tissue pathology: 
    * Unlike other vascular markers (CD34, CD31, ERG, FLI1), D2-40 expression is limited to lymphatic differentiation 
    * To identify lymphatic differentiation in evaluation of vascular tumors 
    * Also expressed in a subset of angiosarcomas and Kaposi sarcomas, reflecting lymphatic differentiation in some of these tumors ([Histopathology 2005;46:396](15810951), [Mod Pathol 2002;15:434](11950918)) 
    * Dermatofibroma (+) versus dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans (DFSP) (-) ([Mod Pathol 2010;23:434](20062007)) 
    * Primary skin adnexal tumors (+) versus metastatic adenocarcinoma (-) ([Am J Dermatopathol 2018;40:389](29781860), [J Cutan Pathol 2010;37:403](20377670)) 
    * Chondrosarcoma (+) versus chordoma (-) ([Acta Neuropathol 2007;113:87](17021752))
  * Uropathology: 
    * Seminoma (+) versus nonseminoma (-) 
    * Note that some embryonal carcinomas may also express D2-40 up to 30% ([Virchows Arch 2006;449:200](16736189), [Mod Pathol 2007;20:320](17277761)) 
    * In situ germ cell neoplasm (+) 
    * Adrenocortical neoplasms (+) versus renal cell carcinoma (-) ([J Clin Pathol 2008;61:293](17660332)) 
    * Utility in distinguishing intraductal spread of urothelial carcinoma from prostatic stromal invasion ([Anticancer Res 2008;28:2997](19031946))
  * Thoracic pathology: 
    * Mesothelioma (+), even in effusions, versus adenocarcinoma (-) ([Diagn Cytopathol 2007;35:342](17497664), [Cancer 2007;109:933](17279584)) 
    * Note the presence of expression in significant proportion of ovarian, lung and breast carcinomas (30 - 50%) ([Am J Surg Pathol 2006;30:878](16819331))
  * Hemangioblastoma (+) versus clear cell renal cell carcinoma metastasis 

Prognostic factors

  * Controversial results of prognostic significance in detecting lymphovascular invasion with D2-40 compared to H&E in breast and other cancers ([Mod Pathol 2009;22:216](18820667), [Mod Pathol 2007;20:183](17206106), [Hum Pathol 2018;77:98](29601841), [Cancer Med 2016;5:3121](27748061), [Anticancer Res 2008;28:2997](19031946), [Cancer Lett 2007;246:167](16574316)) 
  * D2-40 expression in cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (SCC) is associated with metastasis and poor prognosis ([J Histotechnol 2020;43:147](32496961), [J Cutan Pathol 2017;44:144](27859466), [J Am Acad Dermatol 2012;67:1310](22521203))

Positive staining - normal

  * Lymphatic endothelium ([Am J Pathol 1999;154:385](10027397)) 
  * Adrenal cortical cells, normal and neoplastic ([J Clin Pathol 2008;61:293](17660332)) 
  * Breast myoepithelium, patchy, in normal and neoplastic breast ([Hum Pathol 2008;39:175](18206495)) 
  * Follicular dendritic cells ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2009;17:102](18838918)) 
  * Granulosa cells ([Am J Pathol 2005;166:913](15743802)) 
  * Mesothelium, normal and reactive ([Mod Pathol 2005;18:105](15389250)) 
  * Prostatic basal cells ([Anticancer Res 2008;28:2997](19031946)) 
  * Odontogenic epithelial cells ([J Oral Pathol Med 2017;46:618](27859616)) 
  * Skin, basal cells of hair follicle outer root sheath ([J Cutan Pathol 2008;35:926](18537863)) 
  * Developing testis ([Virchows Arch 2006;449:200](16736189)) 
  * Developing brain ([Mod Pathol 2006;19:974](16648867)) 
  * Basal cells of squamous mucosal epithelium (tonsil, esophagus) 

Positive staining - disease

  * Soft tissue tumors 
    * Vascular tumors / lesions 
      * Angiosarcoma (40 - 80%) ([Am J Pathol 1999;154:385](10027397), [Histopathology 2005;46:396](15810951), [Mod Pathol 2002;15:434](11950918)) 
      * Kaposi sarcoma (100%) ([Am J Pathol 1999;154:385](10027397), [Histopathology 2005;46:396](15810951), [Mod Pathol 2002;15:434](11950918)) 
      * Kaposiform hemangioendothelioma (95 - 100%) ([J Cutan Pathol 2006;33:492](16872472), [Mod Pathol 2005;18:1454](15920541)) 
      * Atypical vascular proliferations of breast postradiation ([Cancer 2007;109:1584](17357996)) 
      * Epithelioid hemangioendothelioma of liver (80%) ([Mod Pathol 2008;21:125](18084256)) 
      * Lymphatic malformations ([Histopathology 2005;46:396](15810951))
    * Dermatofibroma, including cellular variant ([Mod Pathol 2010;23:434](20062007)) 
    * Sinonasal hemangiopericytoma ([Virchows Arch 2006;448:459](16362413)) 
    * Solitary fibrous tumor (some, [Pathol Int 2007;57:618](17685935) but see [Virchows Arch 2006;448:459](16362413)) 
    * Myxoinflammatory fibroblastic sarcoma (80 - 90%) ([Am J Surg Pathol 2014;38:1](24121178))
  * CNS tumors 
    * Myoxid / chondromyxoid lesions of CNS: 
      * Chondroid and chordoid tumors: skeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma (100%), enchondroma (96%), low grade chondrosarcoma (95%), chordoid meningioma (80%) and chordoid glioma (75%) but not chordoma or extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma ([Mod Pathol 2006;19:746](16547467), [Acta Neuropathol 2007;113:87](17021752))
    * Hemangioblastoma ([Acta Neuropathol 2005;109:497](15864611)) 
    * Schwannoma, most of epithelioid malignant peripheral nerve sheath tumors (MPNSTs), occasional neurofibromas or MPNSTs ([Am J Clin Pathol 2008;129:886](18480004)) 
    * Ependymoma but also widely expressed in other CNS tumors, such as meningioma, glioblastoma, choroid plexus tumors, pilocytic astrocytoma ([Clin Neuropathol 2009;28:373](19788053), [Virchows Arch 2006;448:493](16411134))
  * Follicular dendritic cell tumor ([Int J Clin Exp Pathol 2008;1:276](18784810)) 
  * Malignant mesothelioma, including sarcomatoid subtype ([Mod Pathol 2006;19:34](16056246), [Mod Pathol 2005;18:105](15389250), [Am J Surg Pathol 2008;32:123](18162779)) 
  * Germ cell tumors 
    * Gonadal / extragonadal seminoma (dysgerminoma) ([Am J Clin Pathol 2007;128:767](17951198), [Int J Gynecol Pathol 2009;28:347](19483629)) 
    * Some (30%) embryonal carcinomas of testis ([Mod Pathol 2007;20:320](17277761))
  * Epithelial neoplasia / carcinomas 
    * Serous carcinoma of gynecologic tract (ovarian 23%, endometrial 43%) ([Mod Pathol 2008;21:1147](18567994)) 
    * In effusion cytology, ovarian (58%), lung (33%), breast (30%), adenocarcinomas of unknown origin (42%), usually focal staining in contrast to mesothelioma ([Mod Pathol 2005;18:105](15389250)) 
    * Squamous cell carcinoma (80%) ([Am J Pathol 2005;166:913](15743802)) 
    * Lung, pleomorphic carcinomas (33%) ([Pathol Int 2008;58:771](19067851)) 
    * Skin, adnexal carcinoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2007;31:304](17255777)) 
    * Adrenocortical carcinomas ([J Clin Pathol 2008;61:293](17660332)) 
    * Ameloblastic tumors (most) ([J Oral Pathol Med 2017;46:618](27859616))
  * Renal angiomyolipoma, lymphatics are prominent; not in liver angiomyolipoma ([Mod Pathol 2006;19:669](16528375), [Hum Pathol 2009;40:374](18973924), [Mod Pathol 2008;21:125](18084256)) 
  * Crohn's disease due to increased lymphatics ([Virchows Arch 2008;452:57](18040712)) 

Negative staining

  * Capillary endothelium 
  * Glomus tumor ([Histopathology 2005;46:396](15810951)) 
  * Retiform hemangioendothelioma, usually ([Am J Dermatopathol 2008;30:31](18212541)) 
  * Dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans (DFSP) ([Mod Pathol 2010;23:434](20062007)) 
  * Chordoma ([Acta Neuropathol 2007;113:87](17021752)) 
  * Clear cell renal cell carcinoma ([Acta Neuropathol 2005;109:497](15864611))
