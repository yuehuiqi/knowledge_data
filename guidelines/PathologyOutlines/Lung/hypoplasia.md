## Cystic disease / congenital anomalies

### Hypoplasia

**PubMed search** : [pulmonary hypoplasia [title]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/?term=pulmonary+hypoplasia+%5Btitle%5D)

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lungnontumorhypoplasia.html#definitiongeneral209140) | [Gross images](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lungnontumorhypoplasia.html#grossimages209141)

Definition / general

  * Incomplete development of lung; lung usually weighs less than normal with fewer alveoli than expected for gestational age 
  * Bilateral hypoplastic disease is fatal 
  * **Causes** : oligohydramnios (renal agenesis, fetal membrane rupture), decreased intrathoracic space (renal cystic disease, diaphragmatic hernia), reduced breathing (anencephaly, musculoskeletal disorders, brainstem hypodevelopment, [Virchows Arch 2009;454:451](19288128)) 
  * **References** : [eMedicine](http://emedicine.medscape.com/article/1005696-overview)
