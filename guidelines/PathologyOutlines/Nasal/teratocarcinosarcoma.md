Definition / general

  * Teratocarcinosarcoma (TCS) is a malignant sinonasal tract neoplasm containing mixed epithelial, mesenchymal and primitive neuroepithelial elements 

Essential features

  * Aggressive neoplasm with predilection for anterior skull base location 
  * Disease of middle age that commonly affects men 
  * Characteristic fetal-like clear cell appearance in squamous epithelial component with teratoid and sarcomatoid elements 
  * Loss of BRG1 (SMARCA4) nuclear expression in majority of the cases ([Turk Neurosurg 2017;27:468](27438614)) 
  * Frequent local recurrence in first 2 years ([Rare Tumors 2023;15:20363613231204047](37743905)) 

Terminology

  * Teratocarcinosarcoma (current WHO 5th edition; recommended) 
  * Teratoid carcinosarcoma (not recommended) 

ICD coding

  * ICD-O: [9081/3](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/96612/9789241548496_eng.pdf#page=85) \- teratocarcinosarcoma 

Epidemiology

  * Rare malignancy (< 150 cases reported to date) 
  * Primarily affects middle aged adults 
  * Wide age range (10 - 80 years); median: 54.7 years 
  * Exceedingly rare under 20 years 
  * Strong male predominance ([J Int Med Res 2020;48:300060520971488](33342340)) 

Sites

  * Nasal cavity and paranasal sinuses (anterior skull base cancer) 
    * Commonly involves ethmoid sinus (50%) followed by maxillary sinus ([BMJ Case Rep 2022;15:e252429](36428033))
  * In rare instances, involvement of orbit, cribriform plate, nasopharynx and oral cavity ([Int J Surg Pathol 2013;21:37](22923779)) 

Pathophysiology

  * SWI / SNF complex subunit deficiency (_SMARCA4_) 
    * _SMARCA4_ (chromatin remodeling SWI / SNF complex component) inactivation leads to cellular lineage differentiation and proliferation ([Cells 2023;13:81](38201285))
  * Dysregulation in Wnt / beta catenin signaling pathway 
    * Activating pS45F mutation in _CTNNB1_ (beta catenin) leads to cell proliferation (particularly mesenchymal component) ([J Neurol Surg B Skull Base 2017;78:346](28725522))

Etiology

  * No risk factor or tumor predisposition syndrome associations are currently known 

Diagrams / tables

  * See [Molecular / cytogenetics images](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/nasalteratoidcarcinosarcoma.html#molecytogeneticsimagessection) section 

Clinical features

  * Nasal obstruction and epistaxis (most common) due to mass effect ([Am J Rhinol Allergy 2021;35:132](32954838)) 
  * Headache, nausea and vomiting also due to mass effect 
  * Visual impairment, abnormal behavior and confusion (orbital and cranial extension / involvement) ([Ear Nose Throat J 2022 Oct 18 [Epub ahead of print]](36257626)) 
  * Hyposmia / anosmia, facial swelling and proptosis 

Diagnosis

  * Imaging 
    * PET CT scan and MRI of head and neck to assess the local destruction and extension ([J Int Med Res 2020;48:300060520971488](33342340))
  * Nasal endoscopy 
    * To visualize the mass and lesional tissue sampling for histologic diagnosis

Radiology description

  * PET / CT: an expansile, hypermetabolic mass ([Turk Neurosurg 2017;27:468](27438614)) 
  * MRI (fat suppressed) 
    * Hypointense (T1 weighted) isointense (T2 weighted) heterogenous mass with homogenous postcontrast enhancement ([Turk Neurosurg 2017;27:468](27438614))

Prognostic factors

  * Size, cranial / brain extension and distant metastases at the time of presentation 
  * Residual disease (postoperative) ([Am J Rhinol Allergy 2021;35:132](32954838)) 

Case reports

  * 28 year old man with right sided nasal obstruction for 3 months and recurrent epistaxis ([J Oral Maxillofac Pathol 2016;20:147](27194879)) 
  * 46 year old woman presented with a growth on the inner side of the cheek that had grown rapidly in 3 weeks ([J Surg Case Rep 2022;2022:rjac169](35423000)) 
  * 48 year old man presented with left sided nasal obstruction for 3 years along with discharge, hyposmia and visual impairment ([Ear Nose Throat J 2022 Oct 18 [Online ahead of print]](36257626)) 
  * 50 year old man presented in ear, nose, throat (ENT) outpatient department with intermittent left sided epistaxis and dizziness for last 1 year ([J Nutr 1987;117:758](3585527)) 
  * 52 year old woman presented with progressive maxillofacial swelling and blurred vision in both eyes, previously treated for sinusitis, suspicion of invasive fungal sinusitis ([Ann R Coll Surg Engl 2021;103:e193](33852367)) 

Treatment

  * Small sized lesions by endoscopic sinus surgery ([Pathologica 2021;113:469](34974554)) 
  * Lesions with orbital extension and cribriform plate involvement require radical surgery followed by radiation ([Am J Otolaryngol 2014;35:5](23731851))

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Tumor comprises an admixture of epithelial, mesenchymal and neuroepithelial elements 
    * Epithelial component: squamous epithelium (keratinized or nonkeratinized) with peculiar fetal-like clear cell appearance or glandular structures (mucinous or ciliated) 
    * Mesenchymal component: hypercellular fascicles of spindle cells that can show smooth muscle, rhabdomyoblastic, chondroid / osteoblastic (common) or adipocytic differentiation 
    * Neuroepithelial component: sheet and nests of primitive epithelioid cells with enlarged hyperchromatic nuclei, fibrillary background and rosette formation
  * Cytologic atypia is variable, ranging from bland to overtly malignant in both epithelial and mesenchymal components ([J Int Med Res 2020;48:300060520971488](33342340)) 
  * Neuronal maturation can be seen as posttherapy changes

Sample pathology report

  * Mass left nasopharynx, biopsy: 
    * Sinonasal teratocarcinosarcoma (see comment) 
    * Comment: The biopsy shows mixture of epithelial, mesenchymal and neuroepithelial components. The presence of fetal-like clear cell appearance in the epithelial islands and loss of _SMARCA4 (BRG1)_ stain in the tumor are consistent with the above diagnosis. The tumor has propensity for lung metastases. 

Differential diagnosis

  * Limited biopsy may not contain all 3 elements and poses a diagnostic challenge

Practice question #1

A middle aged man presented in ear, nose, throat (ENT) clinic with a history of off and on nasal obstruction for last 6 months. Nasal endoscopy shows a polypoidal tumor in the nasal cavity. The histopathology examination shows an infiltrating tumor displaying malignant looking squamous epithelial areas with clear cell change, intermixed nests of immature rounded cells and cellular spindle cell areas. The tumor also shows loss of SMARCA4 nuclear expression. The tumor can be differentiated from the SMARCA4 deficient carcinoma by the presence of which of the following features?   
  
  1. Anaplasia 
  2. Brisk mitotic activity 
  3. Positivity of pancytokeratins 
  4. Positivity of synaptophysin 
  5. Teratoid elements 

Practice answer #1

**E**. Teratoid elements. The histologic findings described above are characteristic of teratocarcinosarcoma, displaying all 3 elements. Answers C and D are incorrect because both tumors can show reactivity to pancytokeratins and synaptophysin. Answers A and B are also incorrect because anaplasia and mitoses are present in both tumor types.

Practice question #2

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/nasalteratoidcarcinosarcomaUdDin04.jpg

A 40 year old man presented with anosmia and nasal obstruction. MRI shows an expansile mass involving the cribriform plate. The biopsy shows a tumor in the submucosa that comprises nests and lobules of primitive neuroepithelial cells. There are admixed epithelial elements that comprise squamous differentiation. SMARCA4 (BRG1) stain shows loss of nuclear expression. What is the likely diagnosis?   
  
  1. Craniopharyngioma 
  2. Ewing sarcoma 
  3. Lymphoepithelioma-like carcinoma 
  4. Olfactory neuroblastoma (ONB) 
  5. Teratocarcinosarcoma (TCS) 

Practice answer #2

**E**. Teratocarcinosarcoma (TCS). The histologic findings are consistent with teratocarcinosarcoma (TCS). Answer B is incorrect because Ewing sarcoma is primarily a tumor of young age and it does not show epithelial differentiation. Answer D is incorrect because ONB usually does not show teratoid differentiation and has retained nuclear expression of BRG1 stain. Answers A and C are also incorrect because neither of these tumors contain neuroepithelial components.
