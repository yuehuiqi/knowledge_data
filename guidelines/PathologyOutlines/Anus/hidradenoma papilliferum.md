Definition / general

  * Hidradenoma papilliferum is a rare benign tumor that originates from mammary-like anogenital glands and occurs almost exclusively in middle aged women 

Essential features

  * Hidradenoma papilliferum is a rare, benign adnexal tumor that arises almost exclusively in the anogenital region of middle aged women and originates from mammary-like (apocrine type) glands 
  * Presents as a small, well circumscribed, asymptomatic dermal nodule, most commonly in the anogenital areas 
  * Histologically, it features cystic and papillary architecture lined by 2 epithelial layers: an inner columnar layer with apocrine decapitation secretion and an outer myoepithelial layer 
  * Immunohistochemistry supports its apocrine differentiation with markers like CK7, GCDFP-15, AR and p63; local excision is curative, with no risk of malignant transformation 

Terminology

  * Also known as papillary hidradenoma, mammary-like glandular adenoma, anogenital hidradenoma papilliferum and apocrine papillary cystadenoma 

ICD coding

  * ICD-O: [8405/0](https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/f63d7652-1254-47f4-8ab7-3c65ad60d57d/content#page=72) \- papillary hidradenoma / hidradenoma papilliferum 
  * ICD-10 
    * [D12.9](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/C00-D49/D10-D36/D12-/D12.9) \- benign neoplasm of anus and anal canal 
    * [D28.0](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/C00-D49/D10-D36/D28-/D28.0) \- benign neoplasm of vulva 

Epidemiology

  * Occurs most often in middle aged White women, ages ranging from 25 to 66 years; mean age of 45 years ([Am J Dermatopathol 2016;38:598](26863059), [Cureus 2021;13:e13061](33680603)) 
  * Only rare isolated cases have been reported in men ([Am Surg 2016;82:E43](26874122), [Ir J Med Sci 1998;167:26](9540295)) 

Sites

  * Occurs most commonly in vulva (71.6%), followed by perianal (14%) and perineal (3.8%) areas ([Am J Dermatopathol 2016;38:598](26863059)) 
  * Rarely in nonanogenital areas such as the head and neck; also known as ectopic hidradenoma papilliferum ([Ann Coloproctol 2019;35:361](31937076)) 

Pathophysiology

  * It was considered to arise from apocrine sweat glands 
  * Anogenital mammary-like gland (AGMLG) is a normal component of the anogenital area; recent studies suggest that hidradenoma papilliferum is derived from AGMLGs ([Am J Dermatopathol 2016;38:598](26863059), [BMJ Case Rep 2018;2018:bcr2017220354](30061121), [Arch Pathol Lab Med 2013;137:1237](23991738)) 
  * Hidradenoma papilliferum is regarded as analogous to intraductal papilloma of the breast ([Hum Pathol 2016;55:57](27184479)) 
  * Human papillomavirus (HPV) does not appear to be involved in pathogenesis ([Genes Chromosomes Cancer 2016;55:113](26493284)) 

Etiology

  * Etiology remains elusive 
  * Some studies suggest hormones, including estrogen, progesterone and prolactin, may play a role in the pathogenesis ([J Obstet Gynaecol 2023;43:2186781](36912189), [Cureus 2021;13:e13061](33680603)) 

Clinical features

  * Usually a solitary, painless, firm, freely mobile nodular or cystic lesion, size ranging from 2 to 20 mm; > 20 mm in rare cases 
  * Some cases may present with ulceration and symptoms such as itching, pain, burning, discharge and bleeding ([Dermatol Surg 2003;29:790](12828711)) 

Diagnosis

  * Diagnosis is established by histology 
  * Common clinical differential diagnoses include cyst, fibroma, hemorrhoid, lipoma and wart 

Prognostic factors

  * Benign lesion with good prognosis 
  * No local recurrence if excision margins are clear 
  * Malignant transformation is extremely rare ([Genes Chromosomes Cancer 2016;55:113](26493284)) 

Case reports

  * 22 year old woman with a perianal nodule ([Dermatol Surg 2003;29:790](12828711)) 
  * 23 year old woman with a perineal mass lesion ([J Obstet Gynaecol 2023;43:2186781](36912189)) 
  * 31 year old woman with a perianal mass ([BMJ Case Rep 2018;2018:bcr2017220354](30061121)) 
  * 35 year old woman with a perineal mass lesion ([Cureus 2021;13:e13061](33680603)) 
  * 35 and 39 year old women with perianal masses ([Ann Coloproctol 2019;35:361](31937076)) 
  * 74 year old man with a dermal cystic lesion in the region between the anus and scrotum ([Diagn Pathol 2010;5:42](20576161)) 

Treatment

  * Surgical removal by excision is enough and no follow up is required 

Gross description

  * Generally a solitary, well circumscribed, firm, pink, bluish or skin colored nodular / polypoid lesion, usually < 2 cm in size 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Usually a solitary, intradermal, well circumscribed nodular or cystic lesion ([J Clin Pathol 2017;70:424](27742746), [Pathology 2019;51:362](31010589)) 
  * Focal attachment to the overlying epidermis in rare cases 
  * Often exhibits complex branching and anastomosing tubules in a labyrinthine manner ([Am J Dermatopathol 2016;38:598](26863059)) 
  * Focal glandular, papillary and small cystic formation ([Genes Chromosomes Cancer 2016;55:113](26493284), [Pathology 2019;51:362](31010589)) 
  * 2 layers of lining cells: luminal eosinophilic columnar epithelial cells and basal myoepithelial cells ([J Clin Pathol 2017;70:424](27742746)) 
  * Epithelial cells may present decapitation secretion 
  * Oxyphilic metaplasia is the most common type of metaplasia 
    * Usually multifocal, with relatively more cellular atypia and conspicuous nucleoli ([Am J Dermatopathol 2016;38:598](26863059)) 
  * Squamous and mucinous metaplasia and clear cell changes of the epithelium are seen in rare cases ([Am J Dermatopathol 2016;38:598](26863059)) 
  * Ductal hyperplasia and sclerosing adenosis-like changes may occur, resembling the changes of breast tissue 
  * Focal clear cell changes in the myoepithelial cells are not uncommon 
  * Foamy macrophages are commonly identified within the lumina of the tubules 
  * Mitotic activity is variable and may be elevated but lacks clinical significance

Negative stains

Molecular / cytogenetics description

  * PI3K / AKT pathway plays an essential tumorigenic role in hidradenoma papilliferum (60 - 70%) ([Ann Diagn Pathol 2017;28:12](28648934), [J Clin Pathol 2017;70:424](27742746), [Pathology 2019;51:362](31010589), [Hum Pathol 2016;55:57](27184479), [Genes Chromosomes Cancer 2016;55:113](26493284)) 
  * _PIK3CA_ and _AKT1_ oncogenic mutations are most common 
  * _BRAF, APC_ and _ERBB4_ mutations are rarely identified ([Pathology 2019;51:362](31010589)) 
  * No _RAS, TP53_ or _CTNNB1_ mutations are found 

Sample pathology report

  * Anus, nodule, excision: 
    * Hidradenoma papilliferum (see comment) 
    * Comment: The excision specimen demonstrates a well circumscribed dermal adnexal lesion with complex papillary fronds lined by secretory columnar cells and myoepithelial cells, consistent with hidradenoma papilliferum. No dysplasia or malignancy is identified.

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/anushidradenomapapilliferumPQHao1.jpg

A 26 year old woman presented with a perianal lesion associated with itching and bleeding. Examination revealed a pedunculated, reddish nodule in the anal area. The nodule was totally excised and histologic features of the lesion are shown in the image above. What is the most likely diagnosis?   
  
  1. Clear cell hidradenoma 
  2. Hidradenoma papilliferum 
  3. Syringocystadenoma papilliferum 
  4. Thrombosed hemorrhoids with intravascular papillary endothelial hyperplasia (IPEH) 

Practice answer #1

**B.** Hidradenoma papilliferum. The image shows classical morphologic features of hidradenoma papilliferum with complex branching and anastomosing tubules and focal papillary architecture. Answer A is incorrect because clear cell hidradenoma usually presents with solid nests of polyhedral or round cells with basophilic or clear cytoplasm. Answer C is incorrect because features of syringocystadenoma papilliferum are not identified in this figure, like connection to the epidermis and fibrovascular core with plasma cells. In addition, syringocystadenoma papilliferum more commonly occurs in the head and neck area. Answer D is incorrect because although thrombosed hemorrhoids can be associated with papillary endothelial hyperplasia, it is lined by a single layer of plump endothelial cells. An adjacent organizing thrombus may be present.

Practice question #2

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/anushidradenomapapilliferumPQHao2.jpg

Which of the following statements is correct about the perianal lesion shown above?   
  
  1. Estrogen receptor is expressed in this tumor 
  2. Human papillomavirus (HPV) infection is a risk factor for this tumor 
  3. Male and female patients have a similar chance of having this tumor 
  4. Microscopically, attachment to the epidermis is never identified in this tumor 

Practice answer #2

**A.** Estrogen receptor is expressed in this tumor. Estrogen receptor has been found in 90% of hidradenoma papilliferum cases. Some studies have suggested that hormonal stimulation may be involved in the pathogenesis of this tumor. Answer B is incorrect because HPV is detected only in a minority of hidradenoma papilliferum cases. Answer C is incorrect because hidradenoma papilliferum occurs almost exclusively in female patients. Answer D is incorrect because some hidradenoma papilliferum can have focal connection to the epidermis, resembling syringocystadenoma papilliferum. However, real involvement like epidermal invagination identified in syringocystadenoma papilliferum is not seen in hidradenoma papilliferum.
