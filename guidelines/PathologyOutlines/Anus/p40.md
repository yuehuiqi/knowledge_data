Definition / general

  * Truncated, nontransactivating p63 isoform ([Mod Pathol 2012;25:405](22056955)) 
  * May stimulate cell proliferation, block apoptosis and favor unrestrained tumor growth ([Int J Surg Pathol 2013;21:229](23486764)) 

Essential features

  * Nuclear marker with expression in squamous, urothelial, myoepithelial / basal cell differentiation 
  * More specific for squamous cell differentiation than p63 in lung carcinoma ([Mod Pathol 2012;25:405](22056955)) 
  * Recommended for subtyping non small cell carcinoma ([J Thorac Oncol 2019;14:377](30572031)) 

Terminology

  * Also called ΔNp63 
  * There are other molecules that are also called p40: 
    * Eukaryotic initiation factor 3 (eIF3-p40) ([J Virol 2007;81:11569](17686838)) 
    * Interleukin 12 / cytokine p40 (or its antibodies used to treat psoriasis) ([J Biol Chem 2011;286:29806](21730055), [Semin Cutan Med Surg 2010;29:48](20430307)) 
    * Lysosomal membrane p40 ([Biochem J 2008;414:431](18479248)) 
    * Phagocyte NADPH oxidase subunit p40 phox ([Blood 2009;114:3309](19692703)) 
    * _Klebsiella pneumoniae_ toll-like receptor2 ligand p40 ([J Immunother 2009;32:875](19752746)) 
    * _Lactobacillus casei_ BL23 ([J Mol Microbiol Biotechnol 2010;19:231](21178363)) 
    * _Mycoplasma_ p40 ([Microbiology (Reading) 2011;157:473](20966086)) 
    * _Saccharomyces cerevisiae_ p40 / ARPC1 ([J Biol Chem 2010;285:8481](20071330))

Clinical features

  * Mutations cause ectrodactyly ectodermal dysplasia cleft syndrome, ankyloblepharon ectodermal dysplasia clefting syndrome, nonsyndromic split hand / split foot malformation ([J Cell Sci 2011;124:2200](21652629)) 

Interpretation

  * Nuclear stain 

Uses by pathologists

  * Usually positive in squamous, urothelial and myoepithelial / basal cell differentiation 
  * Part of recommended panel for subtyping non small cell lung carcinoma ([J Thorac Oncol 2019;14:377](30572031))

Lung squamous cell carcinoma

Positive staining - normal

  * Squamous cells ([Mod Pathol 2012;25:405](22056955)) 
  * Myoepithelial / basal cells in breast, salivary gland and prostate ([Am J Surg Pathol 2001;25:1054](11474290)) 
  * Cytotrophoblasts ([Am J Surg Pathol 2004;28:1177](15316317)) 

Positive staining - disease

  * Generally positive in squamous, basal / myoepithelial and urothelial cell differentiation 
  * Squamous cell carcinoma of any site 
  * Bone / head and neck / soft tissue: adamantinoma-like Ewing family tumor ([Am J Surg Pathol 2013;37:772](23552387), [Am J Surg Pathol 2019;43:187](30285997), [Head Neck Pathol 2022 Jan 13 [Epub ahead of print]](35025056)) 
  * Breast: myoepithelial carcinoma, metaplastic carcinoma 
  * Gynecological: 
    * Choriocarcinoma ([Malays J Pathol 2017;39:175](28866701)) 
    * Stratified mucin producing intraepithelial lesion (SMILE) ([Cells 2021;10:2039](34440808)) 
  * Lung: 
    * Squamous cell carcinoma (p40 [strong] and > 50% of cells, TTF1-) 
    * Adenosquamous (p40 strong and < 50%) ([J Thorac Oncol 2012;7:281](22071786), [Am J Surg Pathol 2012;36:895](22367298)) 
    * Adenocarcinoma (5% are positive but in < 5% of cells) ([Mod Pathol 2012;25:405](22056955)) 
    * Metastatic urothelial or squamous cell carcinoma, undifferentiated thymic carcinomas and choriocarcinomas may also be positive for p40 ([Malays J Pathol 2017;39:175](28866701)) 
    * Bronchiolar adenoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2018;42:1010](29846186)) 
  * Head and neck: squamous cell / sarcomatoid carcinomas ([Pathol Res Pract 2022;229:153733](34929597)) 
    * Nasal cavity, paranasal sinuses, nasopharynx ([Head Neck Pathol 2014;8:141](24114197)): 
      * Ewing / PNET (25%, 1/4 cases) ([Head Neck Pathol 2014;8:141](24114197)) 
      * HPV related multiphenotypic sinonasal carcinoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:1690](28877065)) 
      * Nasopharyngeal carcinoma (close to 100%) ([Head Neck Pathol 2014;8:141](24114197)) 
      * NUT carcinoma (75%) ([Head Neck Pathol 2014;8:141](24114197)) 
      * Olfactory neuroblastoma (28%, 13/46 cases; if positive, often focal) ([Head Neck Pathol 2014;8:141](24114197)) 
      * Sinonasal undifferentiated carcinoma (55%) ([Head Neck Pathol 2014;8:141](24114197)) 
      * Small cell carcinoma (50%, 1/2 cases) ([Head Neck Pathol 2014;8:141](24114197)) 
      * SMARCB1 deficient sinonasal carcinoma (44%) ([Am J Surg Pathol 2014;38:1282](25007146)) 
    * Salivary gland ([Oral Surg Oral Med Oral Pathol Oral Radiol 2022;133:189](34518135)): 
      * Adenoid cystic carcinoma (83%) 
      * Myoepithelial carcinoma (~100%) 
      * Basal cell adenoma and basal cell carcinoma (100%) 
      * Oncocytoma (100%, 7/7 cases) 
      * Myoepithelioma (100%) 
      * Epithelial myoepithelial carcinoma (~100%) 
      * Oncocytoma (100%) 
      * Pleomorphic adenoma 
      * Mucoepidermoid adenoma 
  * Thymic neoplasms ([Ann Diagn Pathol 2015;19:216](25979154)): 
    * Thymoma (97%) 
    * Undifferentiated thymic carcinomas that lack squamous features (50%) 
  * Skin: 
    * Primary cutaneous mucinous carcinoma (25%) ([Am J Dermatopathol 2021;43:e175](33989221)) 
    * Basal cell carcinoma 
    * Skin adnexal tumors such as hidradenoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:711](31876586)) 
  * Urothelial carcinoma (up to 87%, with or without squamous differentiation) ([Pathology 2016;48:543](27594510), [Adv Anat Pathol 2020;27:114](32205473)) 

Negative staining

  * Lung: adenocarcinoma (5% are positive but in < 5% of cells) ([Mod Pathol 2012;25:405](22056955)) 
  * Sarcomatoid mesothelioma (6% are positive) ([Int J Surg Pathol 2021;29:820](33939493)) 

Sample pathology report

  * Cervix, biopsy: 
    * Invasive squamous cell carcinoma, poorly differentiated 
    * Ancillary studies: The malignant cells are diffusely positive for cytokeratin 5/6, p40 and p16 and are negative for cytokeratin 7, supporting the diagnosis. 

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/lungtumorscc_Bychkov02.jpg

A 55 year old male construction worker with a 20 pack year smoking history and no prior diagnosis of malignancy, presents with a lung mass, which is biopsied. The biopsy demonstrates a poorly differentiated malignant neoplasm. An immunohistochemical panel comprising CK7, CK20, CK5/6, TTF1, p40, napsin, calrentin and BerEP4 is performed.   
  
Which profile supports the diagnosis of squamous cell carcinoma?   
  
  1. CK7+, TTF1+, napsin+, other stains negative 
  2. CK5/6+, p40+ in more than 50% of cells, strong, other stains negative 
  3. CK7+, CK5/6+, TTF1+, p40+ in less than 50% of cells strong, other stains negative 
  4. CK5/6+, calretinin+, others negative 
  5. CK20+, others negative 

Practice answer #1

**B.** CK5/6+, p40+ in more than 50% of cells, strong, other stains negative. Strong p40 staining in greater than 50% of cells is expected in squamous cell carcinoma. Profile A is seen in lung adenocarcinoma. Profile C is supportive of lung adenosquamous carcinoma. Profile D is consistent with mesothelioma. Metastatic colorectal carcinoma is a possibility with profile E. A diagnosis of primary squamous cell carcinoma of the lung needs to be made in the appropriate clinical context, as metastatic squamous cell carcinoma or direct extension from a thymic carcinoma may have the same profile. PAX8 and CD117 are often positive in thymic carcinomas and will be negative in primary lung squamous cell carcinoma.
