Definition / general

  * Nodules of fetal skin, hair and fat found on the amnion 

Essential features

  * Deposition of fetal squames as nodules of surface of amnion 
  * Associated with severe prolonged oligohydramnios, anhydramnios 

Terminology

  * Vernix granuloma 

Sites

  * Amnion of the chorionic disc, placental membranes or umbilical cord 

Pathophysiology

  * From vernix that has been rubbed into defects of the amniotic surface 
    * Most likely first step is reactive amnionic epithelium due to decreased / absent amniotic fluid 
    * Vernix is then able to attach to the defect ([Baergen: Benirschke's Pathology of the Human Placenta, 7th Edition, 2021](https://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/3030847241/pathologyoutl-20)) 

Etiology

  * Result of deficient amniotic fluid over a prolonged period of time 

Clinical features

  * Pregnancies complicated by severe oligohydramnios, including 
    * Cases of fetal renal agenesis ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2007;131:1829](18081444)) 
    * Placenta of the donor twin ("suck twin") in twin to twin transfusion syndrome 
    * Cases of fetal sirenomelia ([Arch Dis Child 1960;35:250](13795887)) 

Diagnosis

  * Gross and histopathologic examination 

Case reports

  * Baby with bilateral renal agenesis and oligohydramnios ([Proc R Soc Med 1958;51:512](13567717)) 
  * 19 year old woman with no prenatal care who gave birth to a fetus with sirenomelia and renal agenesis ([Arch Dis Child 1960;35:250](13795887)) 
  * 33 year old woman with missed abortion and fetus with congenital ichthyosis ([Am J Clin Pathol 1977;67:567](559408)) 

Gross description

  * Multiple firm, circumscribed, round to ovoid, raised, shiny, yellow nodules visible on the amniotic surface ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2007;131:1829](18081444)) 
  * 1 - 5 mm in diameter 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Composed of varying proportions of squamous cells (occasionally keratinized), fat and hair embedded in degenerative amorphous acidophilic debris ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2007;131:1829](18081444))

Fetal squamous cells in amnion

Positive stains

  * Fetal skin cells within the amnion nodosum will stain positive for [keratins](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsckgeneral.html)

Electron microscopy description

  * Nodules are composed mostly of closely packed bundles of fibrillary material of high electron density and cellular elements of various kinds ([Arch Pathol 1974;98:39](4829774)) 
  * Basement membrane of amnion epithelium is usually present under the nodules 

Sample pathology report

  * Placenta, delivered: 
    * Third trimester placenta 330 grams (tenth to twenty fifth percentile) 
    * Umbilical cord: unremarkable 3 vessel cord 
    * Fetal membranes and maternal decidua: amnion nodosum 
    * Placental disc: villous maturation appropriate for gestational age; intervillous thrombus 

Differential diagnosis

  * **Squamous metaplasia** : 
    * Grossly typically more plaque-like, patchy and hydrophobic 
    * Microscopically keratinizing metaplasia of the amnion 
  * **Chorion nodosum** ([Pediatr Dev Pathol 2006;9:353](16953683)): 
    * Similar nodules 
    * Develop on the chorion 
    * Associated with early amnion rupture / amniotic bands
