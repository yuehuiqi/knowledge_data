## Esophagitis

### Esophagitis-overview

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagusesophagitisgen.html#definitiongeneral131590) | [Epidemiology](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagusesophagitisgen.html#epidemiology131591) | [Etiology](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagusesophagitisgen.html#etiology131592) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagusesophagitisgen.html#microscopichistologicdescription131593)

Definition / general

  * Defined as epithelial damage and inflammation ([eMedicine: Esophagitis [Accessed 12 February 2019]](http://emedicine.medscape.com/article/174223-overview)) 

Epidemiology

  * 5% to 11% incidence in US, although reflux symptoms in 33 - 44% of general population 
  * Higher incidence in northern Iran and China 

Etiology

  * Most common cause is gastroesophageal reflux (reflux of gastric contents into lower esophagus); infectious causes are much less common 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Histologic changes may be severe (erosion, ulcer, exudates) or subtle
