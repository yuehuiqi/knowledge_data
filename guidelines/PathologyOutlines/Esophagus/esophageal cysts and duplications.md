# Esophagus

## Congenital anomalies

### Esophageal cysts and duplications

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophaguscysts.html#definitiongeneral132124) | [Bronchogenic cysts](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophaguscysts.html#bronchogeniccysts132125) | [Duplication cysts](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophaguscysts.html#duplicationcysts132126) | [Inclusion cysts](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophaguscysts.html#inclusioncysts132127) | [Retention cysts](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophaguscysts.html#retentioncysts465078)

Definition / general

  * Rare; mostly developmental unless due to cystic degeneration of tumor 
  * Simple cysts are epithelial lined; duplication cysts have 2 muscle layers ([eMedicine: Esophageal Cysts [Accessed 14 February 2019]](http://emedicine.medscape.com/article/426589-overview), [Arch Pathol Lab Med 1977;101:136](576577)) 
  * Either intramural or attached to outer layers of esophagus 
  * Often asymptomatic, even if large; may cause obstructive symptoms 
  * See [Retention cysts](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophaguscysts.html#retention)

Bronchogenic cysts

Definition / general

  * Often young women with dysphagia or chest pain during exercise ([Clin Imaging 2006;30:309](16919550)) 
  * Developmental cysts arise from anomalous budding of foregut bronchial structures; contains cartilage and mucus glands, smooth muscle and ciliated columnar epithelium 

Case reports

  * 26 year old man with cystic lesion at lower esophagus ([Dig Surg 2006;23:209](16874000))

Duplication cysts

Definition / general

  * Also called gastroenteric cyst, foregut cyst 
  * Congenital anomaly, usually lower esophagus 
  * Most are intramural; usually isolated anomaly; however, duplications external to esophageal wall may be associated with vertebral anomalies 
  * 90% do not communicate with esophagus 
  * Usually symptomatic causing dysphagia or respiratory difficulty 

Case reports

  * 22 day old boy ([Indian J Pathol Microbiol 2006;49:396](17001896)) 
  * 2 infants with respiratory distress ([Pediatr Emerg Care 2005;21:854](16340764)) 
  * 14 year old boy with fistula to lung ([Eur J Cardiothorac Surg 2000;18:117](10869950)) 
  * 52 year old woman ([Yonsei Med J 2005;46:859](16385665)) 
  * 61 year old woman with squamous cell carcinoma ([Br J Radiol 2003;76:343](12763952)) 

Treatment

  * Surgery (if symptoms) or possibly observation ([Endoscopy 2005;37:870](16116541))

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Mucosa, submucosa and muscular layers similar to GI tract; lined by either esophageal squamous, gastric, primitive, ciliated columnar or small intestinal epithelium

Inclusion cysts

Definition / general

  * Lined by squamocolumnar epithelium, may be ciliated 

Retention cysts

Definition / general

  * Also called mucocele 
  * Derive from obstructed submucosal gland ducts 
  * Small, usually in lower esophagus 
  * May cause intramural pseudodiverticulosis, with multiple flask-like invaginations into esophageal wall ([Am J Clin Pathol 1976;65:314](816192)) 
  * Associated with chronic esophagitis and fibrosis; also surgically isolated segments of esophagus ([Dis Esophagus 2002;15:96](12060052)) 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Saccular or flask shaped dilation of submucosal gland excretory ducts; rarely reaches muscularis propria 
  * In large lesions, muscularis does not accompany the lesion so are not true diverticula
