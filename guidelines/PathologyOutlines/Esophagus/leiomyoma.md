Definition / general

  * Most common benign tumor of esophagus ([eMedicine: Esophageal Leiomyoma [Accessed 15 February 2019]](http://emedicine.medscape.com/article/174599-overview), [Surg Clin North Am 1983;63:625](6310805)), 8% incidence in autopsy studies ([Hum Pathol 1981;12:1006](7319487)) 

Clinical features

  * Median age 35 years, 2/3 men, usually single, 24% multiple (seedling tumors) 
  * Usually arises from inner circular muscle; most common in distal esophagus, rarely polypoid 
  * Benign behavior ([Ann Thorac Surg 2005;79:1122](15797036)) 
  * Minute (1 - 2 mm "seedling") tumors are often near the gastroesophageal junction and are asymptomatic 
  * Multiple tumors are associated with MEN1 syndrome ([Am J Pathol 2001;159:1121](11549605)) 
  * Large tumors may cause obstructive symptoms 

Case reports

  * 26 year old woman with multinodular growth pattern simulating carcinoma ([Dis Esophagus 2007;20:187](17439606)) 
  * 64 year old man with coexisting early squamous cell carcinoma ([Jpn J Clin Oncol 2004;34:751](15640507)) 
  * 75 year old man with coexisting leiomyosarcoma ([J Exp Clin Cancer Res 2005;24:487](16270537)) 

Treatment

  * Excise if significant symptoms ([Ann Thorac Cardiovasc Surg 2007;13:78](17505413)), endoscopic enucleation for small tumors ([Singapore Med J 2006;47:901](16990968)), esophagectomy for large tumors ([World J Gastroenterol 2005;11:4258](16015702))

Gross description

  * Circumscribed, mural, solitary mass, 2 - 5 cm (surgical specimens), bulges into lumen, may be polypoid 
  * Pinkish gray white with whorled cut surface; mucosal surface is only rarely ulcerated

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Similar to classic endometrial leiomyoma; circumscribed lesion of circular muscularis propria or muscularis mucosae composed of intersecting fascicles of bland spindle cells with abundant cytoplasm 
  * Variable fibrosis in center of large leiomyomas 
  * Occasional calcification; no / rare mitotic figures; no atypia, no cellular foci
