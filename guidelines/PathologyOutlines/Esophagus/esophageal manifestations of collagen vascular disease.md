### Esophageal manifestations of collagen vascular disease

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagusscleroderma.html#definitiongeneral132228) | [Case reports](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagusscleroderma.html#casereports132229) | [Treatment](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagusscleroderma.html#treatment132230) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagusscleroderma.html#microscopichistologicdescription132231) | [Microscopic (histologic) images](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagusscleroderma.html#microscopichistologicimages132232) | [Electron microscopy images](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagusscleroderma.html#electronmicroscopyimages132233) | [Differential diagnosis](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagusscleroderma.html#differentialdiagnosis132234)

Definition / general

  * Characterized by fibrosis, inflammation, increased collagen and vasculitis 
  * Can be part of CREST syndrome (**C** alcinosis, **R** aynaud phenomenon, **E** sophageal involvement, **S** clerodactyly, **T** elangiectasia) 
  * Involves esophagus in 75%+ patients, usually distal 2/3, with aperistalsis and reduced tone of lower esophageal sphincter 
  * Commonly causes reflux esophagitis ([Semin Arthritis Rheum 2006;36:173](17045629)), strictures, _Candida_ esophagitis, Barrett esophagus ([J Clin Gastroenterol 2006;40:769](17016130) but similar prevalence of Barrett as other reflux esophagitis patients, [Arthritis Rheum 2005;52:2882](16142744)) 

Case reports

  * 38 year old man with achalasia ([Dtsch Med Wochenschr 2006;131:1799](16902902)) 
  * 51 year old woman with CMV esophagitis ([J Clin Rheumatol 2001;7:384](17039180)) 

Treatment

  * No treatment for underlying disease; proton pump inhibitors for reflux esophagitis, dilation for strictures 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * **Resection specimens:** atrophy and replacement fibrosis of inner circular layer of muscularis propria and resulting stenosis; longitudinal layer is usually involved; also submucosal fibrosis, mild inflammation, intimal proliferation of arterioles ([Gut 2006;55:1697](16527835)) 
  * **Biopsies:** ulcers or erosions resembling reflux esophagitis, _Candida_ or Barrett
