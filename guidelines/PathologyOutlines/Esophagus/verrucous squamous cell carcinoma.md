Definition / general

  * Highly differentiated variant of squamous cell carcinoma that is locally invasive with a pushing, as opposed to an infiltrative border, that is identical to lesions of the same name in the upper aerodigestive tract 
  * Metastases are uncommon 

Epidemiology

  * Extremely rare 
  * Male predominance 
  * Ages 36 - 76 years 

Pathophysiology / etiology

  * Cases related to chronic mucosal irritation, lye ingestion, diverticular disease, reflux esophagitis ([Gastroenterology 1996;110:904](8608902)) 
  * Reported case with positive HPV stain ([Gastroenterol Hepatol 2013;36:311](23195756)) 

Clinical features

  * Slow growing, nodal metastases are uncommon and distant metastases are not reported but still has high mortality because of advanced local disease with the development of fistulas ([J Gastroenterol Hepatol 1993;8:107](8439656), [J Clin Gastroenterol 1991;13:102](2007728), [Minerva Chir 2005;60:61](15902055)) 

Diagnosis

  * Tissue biopsy, knowledge of clinical and endoscopic findings may be essential to prevent misdiagnosis

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Similar to [oral cavity](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/oralcavityverrucous.html) counterpart 
  * Well differentiated with papillary fronds covered by squamous epithelium 
  * Bland cytologic features with mild atypia 
  * Variable parakeratosis and hyperkeratosis 
  * Pushing type of invasion with blunt epithelial projections

Differential diagnosis

  * **Carcinoma cuniculatum:** deeply penetrating and burrowing growth pattern ([Ann Diagn Pathol 2005;9:134](15944954)) 
  * Superficial biopsies may be diagnosed as [squamous papillomas](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagussquamouspapilloma.html) or other benign lesions 
    * Papillomas are generally under 3 cm in dimension, generally do not demonstrate circumferential growth, lack ulceration, do not cause stricture and do not invade submucosa or muscularis, which are common findings in verrucous carcinoma ([Can J Gastroenterol 2004;18:459](15229749), [Am J Gastroenterol 1996;91:1031](8633546))
