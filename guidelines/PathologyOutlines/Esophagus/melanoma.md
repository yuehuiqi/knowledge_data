Definition / general

  * Primary malignant melanoma developing from the esophageal mucosa / melanocyte 

Essential features

  * Middle aged to elderly patients, with male predominance 
  * Protruding tumor with pigmentation / black discoloration in the middle or lower esophagus 
  * Epithelioid or spindle melanoma cells with junctional melanocytic activity / junctional melanocytic component 
  * Aggressive tumor 

Terminology

  * Primary malignant melanoma of the esophagus 

ICD coding

  * ICD-10: [C15.9](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/C00-D49/C15-C26/C15-/C15.9) \- malignant neoplasm of the esophagus, unspecified 

Epidemiology

  * ~0.3 - 0.8% of all esophageal tumors ([Esophagus 2021;18:1](33047261), [Histopathology 2021;78:240](32654197)) 
  * Middle aged to elderly patients, with male predominance ([Mod Pathol 2019;32:957](30760858), [Thorac Cancer 2019;10:950](30864295), [Histopathology 2021;78:240](32654197)) 

Sites

  * Most common in the distal third of the esophagus ([Mod Pathol 2019;32:957](30760858), [Dis Esophagus 2019 Oct 30 [Epub ahead of print]](31665346)) 
  * Lower esophagus (58% [44/76 cases] to 60% [12/20 cases]); middle esophagus (34% [26/76 cases] to 35% [7/20 cases]) ([Thorac Cancer 2019;10:950](30864295), [Medicine (Baltimore) 2020;99:e20957](32664098)) 

Pathophysiology

  * See [Molecular / cytogenetics description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagusmelanoma.html#melanomamolecular)

Etiology

  * See [Molecular / cytogenetics description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/esophagusmelanoma.html#melanomamolecular)

Clinical features

  * Middle aged to elderly patients, with male predominance ([Mod Pathol 2019;32:957](30760858), [Thorac Cancer 2019;10:950](30864295), [Histopathology 2021;78:240](32654197)) 
  * Progressive dysphagia, weight loss and chest pain ([Mod Pathol 2019;32:957](30760858), [Dis Esophagus 2019 Oct 30 [Epub ahead of print]](31665346)) 

Diagnosis

  * Biopsy with immunohistochemistry 

Radiology description

  * Bulky, polypoid intraluminal masses ([Radiology 1998;209:455](9807573))

Prognostic factors

  * TNM staging ([Medicine (Baltimore) 2020;99:e20957](32664098)) 

Case reports

  * 68 year old man with a 20 mm amelanotic malignant melanoma of the esophagus ([Oncol Lett 2018;15:9087](29844820)) 
  * 74 year old man with an amelanotic malignant melanoma of the esophagus treated with immunotherapy ([J Surg Case Rep 2021;2021:rjab393](34659737)) 
  * 81 year old woman with a 30 mm primary malignant melanoma treated with esophagectomy and nivolumab ([J Med Case Rep 2021;15:237](33947459)) 
  * 86 year old woman with a 58 mm amelanotic malignant melanoma of the esophagus ([Surg Case Rep 2019;5:4](30635729)) 

Treatment

  * Surgical resection 
  * Endoscopic resection 
  * Immunotherapy (PD-1 inhibitor systemic treatment) ([Thorac Cancer 2019;10:950](30864295)) 
  * Chemotherapy 
  * Targeted therapy

Melanoma

Positive stains

  * Melanoma markers ([S100](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainss100.html), [HMB45](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainshmb45.html), [MelanA / MART1](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsmart1.html), [MITF](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsmitf.html), [SOX10](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainssox10.html), [tyrosinase](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainstyrosinase.html)) 

Negative stains

Molecular / cytogenetics description

  * Contrary to cutaneous melanoma, no (0% [0/22]) or few (up to 6.6%) _BRAF_ mutations identified ([Mod Pathol 2019;32:957](30760858), [Thorac Cancer 2019;10:950](30864295), [Virchows Arch 2009;454:513](19319568)) 
  * _NRAS_ mutations: 33 - 35% ([Mod Pathol 2019;32:957](30760858), [Virchows Arch 2009;454:513](19319568)) 
  * Relatively common _KIT_ mutations (10 - 50%) by next generation sequencing (NGS) analysis ([Histopathology 2021;78:240](32654197) [Mod Pathol 2019;32:957](30760858)) 
  * NGS analysis revealed _NF1_ (30%), _SF3B1_ (20%), _KRAS_ (10%), _BRCA2_ (10%), _KIT_ (10%) and _TP53_ (10%) mutations; no _BRAF_ mutations were detected ([Histopathology 2021;78:240](32654197)) 

Sample pathology report

  * Esophagus, mass, biopsy: 
    * Malignant melanoma (see comment) 
    * Comment: The infiltrating epithelioid tumor cells are positive for S100, HMB45, MelanA and negative for AE1 / AE3, CAM5.2, synaptophysin and chromogranin. The morphology and immunoprofile are consistent with malignant melanoma.
