Cytokeratin 20 (CK20, K20)  

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsck20.html#definitiongeneral234350) | [Uses by pathologists](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsck20.html#usesbypathologists234351) | [Microscopic (histologic) images](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsck20.html#microscopichistologicimages234352) | [Positive staining - normal](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsck20.html#positivestainingnormal234353) | [Positive staining - not malignant](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsck20.html#positivestainingnotmalignant234354) | [Positive staining - malignant](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsck20.html#positivestainingmalignant234355) | [Negative staining](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsck20.html#negativestaining234356)

Definition / general

  * Epithelial marker (MW 46 kDa) with restricted expression compared to CK7 ([OMIM #608218](http://omim.org/entry/608218)) 

Uses by pathologists

  * **General patterns (specificity varies)**  
  
  * CK7+ / CK20+ in carcinomas of bile duct (extrahepatic / gallbladder, often, [Pathol Res Pract 2003;199:65](12747467)), lung-mucinous bronchioloalveolar ([Am J Clin Pathol 2004;122:421](15362373)), pancreas ([Cancer 2006;106:693](16362976), but see [Arch Pathol Lab Med 2000;124:1196](10923083)) 
    * Urothelium (often, [Arch Pathol Lab Med 2001;125:921](11419977), [Hum Pathol 2002;33:1136](12454820)) 
    * Also primary mucinous tumors of ovary (74%), upper GI tract (78%) and endocervix (88%, [Am J Surg Pathol 2006;30:1130](16931958))
  
  * CK7+ / CK20- in carcinomas of bile duct (intrahepatic, [Pathol Res Pract 2003;199:65](12747467)), breast ([Ann Diagn Pathol 1999;3:350](10594286)), endocervical and endometrial adenocarcinoma ([Pathol Res Pract 2003;199:65](12747467)), esophagus (distal, [Am J Surg Pathol 2002;26:1213](12218578)), lung (not mucinous bronchioloalveolar, [BMC Cancer 2006;6:31](16451736)), salivary gland ([Pathol Int 2005;55:386](15982212)), thyroid ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2000;8:189](10981870)) 
    * Also mesothelioma ([Cancer 2001;92:2727](11745209))
  
  * CK7- / CK20+ in carcinoma of colon (particularly early stage, [Hum Pathol 2005;36:275](15791572)) 
    * CK20 is less sensitive for poorly differentiated colonic carcinoma ([Chin J Physiol 2006;49:298](17357536)) 
    * Primary mucinous tumors of lower GI tract (79%, [Am J Surg Pathol 2006;30:1130](16931958)) and primary bladder adenocarcinomas (29%, [Am J Surg Pathol 2001;25:1380](11684954))
  
  * CK7- / CK20- in carcinomas of adrenal cortex and prostate ([Mod Pathol 2000;13:962](11007036))   
  
  * To distinguish primary lung carcinoma (CK7+ / CK20-) from metastatic colonic carcinoma to lung (CK7- / CK20+, [BMC Cancer 2006;6:31](16451736))  
  
  * To help distinguish colon carcinoma (80% are CK20+) and poorly differentiated prostatic carcinoma (90% are CK20-) at biopsy ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2007;131:599](17425391))  
  
  * To distinguish Merkel cell carcinoma (CK20+, dot-like, TTF1-) and metastatic small cell carcinoma of lung (CK20-, TTF1+, [Am J Dermatopathol 2006;28:99](16625069))  
  
  * To distinguish anal carcinoma (CK7+ / CK20-) from downward growth of colorectal carcinoma (CK7- / CK20+, [Arch Pathol Lab Med 2001;125:1074](11473461))  
  
  * May confirm Barrett’s mucosa, which has strong diffuse CK7+ surface and crypt epithelium, strong CK20+ surface and superficial crypt staining 
    * Interpretation is affected by fixative ([Hum Pathol 2005;36:58](15712183), but see [Mod Pathol 2002;15:611](12065774)) 
    * Pattern may help distinguish short segment Barrett’s from cardiac intestinal metaplasia ([World J Gastroenterol 2005;11:6360](16419166))
  
  * To distinguish Rathke cleft cysts and pituitary gland pars intermedia (CK8+, CK20+) from craniopharyngioma (CK8-, CK20-, [Arch Pathol Lab Med 2002;126:1174](12296753))  
  
  * To distinguish adenocarcinoma of distal esophagus (CK7+ / CK19+: 90%, CK7+ / CK20-: 74%) from proximal stomach (CK7+ / CK19+: 44%, CK7+ / CK20-: 24%, [Am J Surg Pathol 2002;26:1213](12218578))  
  
  * To detect occult nodal tumor cells in colorectal adenocarcinoma ([Hum Pathol 2006;37:1259](16949928), [Br J Cancer 2006;95:218](16755296))  
  
  * RT-PCR to detect tumor cells of breast and colorectal carcinoma in blood ([J Mol Diagn 2006;8:105](16436641))  
  
  * RT-PCR assessment in peritoneal wash may predict recurrence in gastric carcinoma ([Oncol Rep 2007;17:667](17273749))  
  
**Urothelial carcinoma:**
    * CK20+ p53+ CD44- favors urothelial carcinoma in situ vs. reactive urothelium ([Am J Surg Pathol 2001;25:1074](11474293)) 
    * CIS shows CK20 staining of deep urothelial cells compared with surface cells only in non-neoplastic lesions ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2006;14:260](16932015)) 
    * CK20+ in voided urine by RT-PCR is sensitive and specific for bladder carcinoma ([Clin Biochem 2004;37:803](15329320), [J Egypt Natl Canc Inst 2006;18:82](17237854), but see [J Urol 2003;169:86](12478110)) 
    * In papillary urothelial neoplasms, CK20+ is associated with increasing tumor grade and stage in pTa and pT1 patients ([Mod Pathol 2000;13:1315](11144928))

Urothelial carcinoma, high grade

Positive staining - normal

  * Colon, Merkel cells, small intestine ([Am J Surg Pathol 2004;28:1352](15371952)) and stomach 
  * Urothelium (umbrella cells, [Am J Surg Pathol 2001;25:1074](11474293)) 
  * Uterus 

Positive staining - not malignant

  * Bladder intestinal metaplasia ([Mod Pathol 2006;19:1395](16951671)) 
  * Fibroepithelioma of Pinkus ([Am J Dermatopathol 2007;29:7](17284955)) 
  * GI carcinoid (25%) 
  * Hydatidiform mole (complete-100%, partial-50%, [Gynecol Oncol 2002;87:34](12468339)) 
  * Renal oncocytoma (dot-like pattern, [J Histochem Cytochem 2001;49:919](11410616)) 

Positive staining - malignant

  * Ampullary (variable, [Am J Surg Pathol 2005;29:359](15725805)) 
  * Anal (variable-[Br J Dermatol 2000;142:243](10730755), [Cancer 2001;92:2045](11596018)) 
  * Appendiceal epithelial neoplasms (benign and malignant, 100%, [Hum Pathol 2005;36:1217](16260276)) 
  * Biliary papillary tumors (benign and malignant, [Hepatology 2006;44:1333](17058219)) 
  * Colon (references above) 
  * Gastric (18-31%, [Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2006;14:303](16932021), reduced if high levels of microsatellite instability, [Am J Surg Pathol 2004;28:712](15166663)) 
  * Hepatocellular (20% positive, [Hum Pathol 2005;36:1226](16260277)) 
  * Hepatoid adenocarcinoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2003;27:1302](14508391)) 
  * Lung mucinous bronchioloalveolar ([Hum Pathol 2002;33:915](12378517)) 
  * Merkel cell (dot like, references above) 
  * Ovarian ([Mod Pathol 2006;19:1421](16980943)) 
  * Paget’s disease (secondary only, [Br J Dermatol 2000;142:243](10730755)) 
  * Pancreatic ductal ([Cancer 2006;106:693](16362976)) 
  * Prostatic (variable) 
  * Sinonasal adenocarcinoma (50%, ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2007;131:530](17425380)) 
  * Small cell of major salivary glands ([Am J Surg Pathol 2004;28:762](15166668)), urothelial (references above) 
  * Vulvar Paget’s disease secondary to anorectal or urothelial carcinoma but not primary ([Hum Pathol 2002;33:545](12094381)) 

Negative staining

  * Anal glands ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2001;125:1074](11473461)) 
  * Inverted urothelial papilloma ([Hum Pathol 2004;35:1499](15619209))
