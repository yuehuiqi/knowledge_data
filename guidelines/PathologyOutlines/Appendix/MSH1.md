Definition / general

  * The four key human mismatch repair genes identified to date are [MutL Homologue 1 (MLH1)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsmlh1.html), [MutS Homologue 2 (MSH2)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsmsh2.html), [MutS Homologue 6 (MSH6)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsmsh6.html), and [PostMeiotic Segregation increased 2 (PMS2)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainspms2.html), homologous to the _E. coli_ MMR genes 
  * MSH1 gene is not found in humans 
  * MSH1 encodes a protein homologous to the _Escherichia coli_ Mut S and _Streptococcus pneumonia_ Hex A protein 
  * It is important for mitochondrial DNA repair and stability 
  * It binds to base-base and small insertion-deletion mismatches in mRNA 
  * In plants, its suppression leads to emergence of abnormal plant phenotypes ([Nat Commun 2015;6:6386](25722057)) 
  * In yeast, its loss of function causes a petite phenotype ([Genetics 1992;132:975](1334021))
