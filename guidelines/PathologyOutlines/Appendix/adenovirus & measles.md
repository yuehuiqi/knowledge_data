### Adenovirus & measles

[Adenovirus](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/appendixmeasles.html#adenovirus139913) | [Measles](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/appendixmeasles.html#measles284567) | [Microscopic (histologic) images](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/appendixmeasles.html#microscopichistologicimages139915)

Adenovirus

  * Associated with marked lymphoid hyperplasia leading to intussusception, usually in children 
  * Viral nuclear inclusions: basophilic nuclei with indistinct nuclear membranes (Cowdry type B, smudge cells) and, less commonly, eosinophilic inclusions surrounded by halos and distinct nuclear membranes (Cowdry type-A) 
  * Immunohistochemistry, PCR, and in situ hybridization helpful in diagnosis ([Infect Dis Clin North Am 2010;24:995](20937462)) 

Measles

  * Associated with appendicitis in prodromal measles stage; rash may not appear until after appendix is removed ([Scand J Infect Dis 2001;33:632](11525364)) 
  * Microscopic description: marked lymphoid hyperplasia, multinucleated giant cells (Warthin-Finkeldey type) in germinal centers ([Infect Dis Clin North Am 2010;24:995](20937462))
