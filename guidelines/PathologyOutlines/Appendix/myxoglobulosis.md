### Myxoglobulosis

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/appendixmyxoglobulosis.html#definitiongeneral8964) | [Case reports](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/appendixmyxoglobulosis.html#casereports8966) | [Clinical images](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/appendixmyxoglobulosis.html#clinicalimages8967) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/appendixmyxoglobulosis.html#microscopichistologicdescription8968) | [Microscopic (histologic) images](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/appendixmyxoglobulosis.html#microscopichistologicimages8969)

Definition / general

  * Descriptive term for rare variant of mucocele causing appendiceal dilation due to nonspecific increased intraluminal pressure 
  * Presents either as acute abdomen or as incidental finding at laparotomy or autopsy 

Case reports

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Intraluminal mucinous and pearl-like globules, which may calcify 
  * Globules composed of faintly eosinophilic laminated mucin surrounding an amorphous core
