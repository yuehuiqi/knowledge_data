## Lacrimal duct / gland

### Pleomorphic adenoma

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyelacrimalpleomorphicadenoma.html#definitiongeneral91463) | [Treatment](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyelacrimalpleomorphicadenoma.html#treatment91464) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyelacrimalpleomorphicadenoma.html#microscopichistologicdescription91465) | [Microscopic (histologic) images](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyelacrimalpleomorphicadenoma.html#microscopichistologicimages91466)

Definition / general

  * 50 - 60% of lacrimal gland tumors 
  * Resemble salivary gland tumors of same type 

Treatment

  * Excisional biopsy with negative margins to prevent recurrence and implantation within the orbit 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Usually encapsulated, composed of benign epithelial and myxoid mesenchymal elements 
  * Often prominent component of S100+ hyaline cells (presumed myoepithelial origin) with diffuse eosinophilic staining 
  * May have squamous metaplasia, bone, cartilage or fat
