### Cicatrizing conjunctivitis

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyecicatrizingconj.html#definitiongeneral88965) | [Etiology](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyecicatrizingconj.html#etiology88966) | [Clinical features](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyecicatrizingconj.html#clinicalfeatures88967) | [Diagnosis](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyecicatrizingconj.html#diagnosis88968) | [Treatment](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyecicatrizingconj.html#treatment88969) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyecicatrizingconj.html#microscopichistologicdescription88970)

Definition / general

  * Cicatrizing: causing a scar and possibly affecting vision 
  * Conjunctival fibrosis due to chronic inflammation, leading to alterations of conjunctival architecture 

Etiology

  * Numerous causes ([Curr Opin Ophthalmol 2001;12:250](11507337)) 
  * Atopic keratoconjunctivitis 
  * Burns 
  * Discoid lupus erythematosus ([Ocul Immunol Inflamm 2002;10:287](12854037)) 
  * Epidermolysis bullosa acquisita ([Arch Ophthalmol 2006;124:1615](17102010)) 
  * Lichen planus ([Am J Ophthalmol 2003;136:239](12888044)) 
  * Ocular cicatricial pemphigoid 
  * Ocular rosacea ([Cornea 2004;23:630](15257004)) 
  * Paraneoplastic syndrome associated with lung tumors ([Cornea 2006;25:611](16783152)) 
  * Porphyria cutaneous tarda ([Am J Ophthalmol 2002;134:619](12383830)) 
  * Post-infectious 
  * Sarcoidosis ([Ann Ophthalmol 1989;21:92](2735697)) 
  * Sjögren syndrome 
  * Stevens-Johnson syndrome 
  * Systemic sclerosis 
  * Trachoma 
  * Granulomatosis with polyangiitis (Wegener's) ([Nephrol Dial Transplant 2006;21:3342](16822788)) 

Clinical features

  * Dry eyes 
  * Loss of transparency and vision 

Diagnosis

  * Positive direct immunofluorescence with IgA, IgM, IgG, complement C3, complement C4, and fibrinogen in autoimmune diseases 

Treatment

  * Medical management varies according to specific causes 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Fibrosis and inflammation consisting of neutrophils, lymphocytes, plasma cells 
  * Keratinization of superficial layer, hyperplasia of basal layers
