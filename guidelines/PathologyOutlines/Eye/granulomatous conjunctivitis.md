### Granulomatous conjunctivitis

[Sites](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeconjgranulomatous.html#sites89009) | [Etiology](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeconjgranulomatous.html#etiology89043) | [Clinical features](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeconjgranulomatous.html#clinicalfeatures89044) | [Case reports](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeconjgranulomatous.html#casereports89045) | [Treatment](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeconjgranulomatous.html#treatment89046) | [Gross description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeconjgranulomatous.html#grossdescription89047) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeconjgranulomatous.html#microscopichistologicdescription89048) | [Microscopic (histologic) images](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeconjgranulomatous.html#microscopichistologicimages89010)

Sites

  * Unilateral or bilateral (in systemic diseases) 
  * Palpebral conjunctiva and upper lid more often in tuberculosis 

Etiology

  * Usually associated with systemic disease 
  * Conjunctival granulomata form in response to foreign body, allergen, infectious organism or as part of systemic disease   
  
  * With necrosis: 
    * Tuberculosis ([Int Ophthalmol 2014;34:655](23928944)) 
    * Parinaud oculoglandular syndrome due to cat scratch fever ([Ugeskr Laeger 1995;157:4137](7544512)) or tularemia ([Am J Ophthalmol 2001;131:283](11228320)) 
    * Granulomatosis with polyangiitis (Wegener's) ([Ophthalmology 2003;110:1770](13129876))
  * Without necrosis: 
    * Crohn's disease ([Br J Ophthalmol 1984;68:901](6509011)) 
    * Foreign bodies (ophthalmia nodosa due to hair of caterpillar, surgical talc) ([J AAPOS 2007;11:402](17446101)) 
    * Hodgkin lymphoma ([Am J Ophthalmol 2001;131:796](11384580)) 
    * Sarcoidosis 
    * Syphilis
  * Other: 
    * Acid fast bacilli 
    * Actinic granulomas ([Ophthalmology 2013;120:1786](23601802)) 
    * Churg-Strauss syndrome ([Arch Ophthalmol 2012;130:1228](22965612)) 
    * Drugs (brimonidine-timolol fixed combination) ([Int Ophthalmol 2013;33:557](23224363), [Br J Ophthalmol 2007;91:691](17446509)) 
    * HIV ([Ocul Immunol Inflamm 2006;14:309](17056466)) 
    * Ocular rosacea in children 
    * Parasites ([Ophthalmology 2001;108:1223](11425679)) 
    * Ulcerative colitis ([Eye (Lond) 2013;27:567](23328805)) 

Clinical features

  * Eye discharge, watering, redness and itching 
  * Often regional lymphadenopathy 

Case reports

  * 2 and 5 year old girls with conjunctivitis due to synthetic fibers from teddy bears ([BMC Ophthalmol 2011;11:17](21689446)) 
  * 34 year old male cattle farmer with no history of trauma ([Cornea 2002;21:831](12410047)) 
  * 62 year old woman with bulbar conjunctival sporotrichosis presenting as tumor ([Cornea 2010;29:573](20335807)) 
  * Two women with _Sporothrix schenckii_ ([Cornea 2005;24:491](15829813)) 

Treatment

  * Varies by etiology 
  * Tuberculosis: anti-tuberculosis therapy, topical prednisolone 
  * Excision in systemic conditions 

Gross description

  * Sarcoidosis: ([EyeWiki: Conjunctival Biopsy in the Diagnosis of Ocular Sarcoidosis [Accessed 25 April 2018]](http://eyewiki.aao.org/Conjunctival_Biopsy_in_the_Diagnosis_of_Ocular_Sarcoidosis)) 
    * Small, tan conjunctival nodules
  * Tuberculosis: ulcerative, nodular, hypertrophic granulomatous and pedunculated 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Chronic inflammatory infiltrate with epitheloid histiocytes and giant cells 
  * Elastolysis and granulomatous inflammation in actinic granuloma
