Definition / general

  * See also [steatocystoma simplex](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyesteatocytomasimplex.html)
  * Most are acquired due to surgery ([Ophthalmology 2006;113:1049](16631253)), trauma ([Br J Anaesth 2005;95:825](16199416)), foreign bodies or chronic inflammation 
  * Also due to parasites, vernal keratoconjunctivitis ([Korean J Ophthalmol 2007;21:251](18063892)) 
  * Usually on nasal conjunctiva and lower fornix 
  * Most (65%) conjunctival nevi are also cystic ([Curr Opin Ophthalmol 2007;18:351](17700226)); some tumors such as lymphangioma can be highly cystic 
  * Cyst may be injected with Healon V and trypan blue to facilitate visualization and excision ([Cornea 2005;24:759](16015101))   
  
  * **Dermoid cysts or 'conjunctivoid':**
    * See also [dermoid tumor](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyedermoidtumor.html)
    * Young adults in contrast to skin dermoid 
    * Usually located in nasal or superonasal deep orbit 
    * Lined by stratified squamous epithelium, contains cutaneous adnexae ([Ophthal Plast Reconstr Surg 2006;22:137](16550063)) 
    * Rarely lipogranulomas from cyst rupture 
    * May have oncocytic differentiation ([Ophthal Plast Reconstr Surg 2008;24:69](18209655)) 
  
  * **Inclusion cysts:**
    * One or two cell lining of nonkeratinized epithelium containing goblet cells 
    * May have apocrine snouts resembling apocrine glands 
    * May be associated with Stevens-Johnson syndrome ([Ophthal Plast Reconstr Surg 2006;22:475](17117107))

Case reports
