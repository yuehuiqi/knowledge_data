Definition / general

  * Occurs in up to 80% of chronic graft versus host disease patients with allogeneic hematopoietic transplants 
  * Most commonly affects conjunctiva, lacrimal gland, meibomian glands; also cornea, choroid 
  * Bulbar conjunctiva is biopsied for diagnosis 
  * Conjunctival and lacrimal gland fibrosis may be due to epithelial mesenchymal transition ([Am J Pathol 2009;175:2372](19893038)) 
  * Modified Lerner classification of bulbar GVHD seems to have prognostic value ([Graefes Arch Clin Exp Ophthalmol 2007;245:1001](17120011)) 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Reduced goblet cells ([Eur J Ophthalmol 2006;16:17](16496240)) 
  * Pseudomembrane due to loss of conjunctival epithelium 
  * May have keratinization 
  * Increased ICAM-1 expression ([Eur J Ophthalmol 2006;16:17](16496240)) 
  * Reduced mucosal microvilli ([Bone Marrow Transplant 2012;47:416](21572465))

Cytology description

  * **Conjunctival scrape cytology:**  

    * Atypical epithelial cells 
    * Loss of goblet cells 
    * Epithelial cells with vacuolated or coarse, granular cytoplasm 
    * Squamous metaplasia 
    * Prominent inflammatory background ([Acta Cytol 2010;54:272](20518410)) 
    * Expression of Th1-associated chemokines ([Bone Marrow Transplant 2010;45:1340](20140022))
