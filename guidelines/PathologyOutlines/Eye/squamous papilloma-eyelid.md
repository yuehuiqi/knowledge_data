Definition / general

  * Squamous papillomas are benign exophytic papillary epithelial proliferations that occur on eyelid skin as well as skin elsewhere on the body, usually not associated with human papillomavirus (HPV) ([StatPearls: Eyelid Papilloma [Accessed 16 September 2025]](29262072)) 
  * May occur as single or multiple lesions ([Indian J Ophthalmol 2016;64:177](27146927)) 

Essential features

  * Benign exophytic / papillary squamous proliferation 
  * Branching papillae with central fibrovascular cores covered by hyperplastic squamous epithelium 
  * May be sessile or pedunculated 

Terminology

  * Skin tag, acrochordon, fibroepithelial polyp (FEP), squamous cell papilloma, soft fibroma 

ICD coding

  * ICD-O: [8052/0](https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/f63d7652-1254-47f4-8ab7-3c65ad60d57d/content#page=63) \- squamous cell papilloma, NOS 
  * ICD-10: [D23.10](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/C00-D49/D10-D36/D23-/D23.10) \- other benign neoplasm of skin of unspecified eyelid, including canthus 
  * ICD-11: [2F36.Y & XH50T2](https://icd.who.int/browse/2024-01/mms/en#1269792630%2Fother%20%26%20249060472) \- other specified benign neoplasm of eye or ocular adnexa & squamous cell papilloma, NOS 

Epidemiology

  * Among the most common benign cutaneous lesions ([Indian J Ophthalmol 2016;64:177](27146927)) 
  * Ranging from 2.4% to 22.8% of benign eyelid lesions ([Postepy Dermatol Alergol 2021;38:804](34849127), [Biomed Res Int 2015;2015:453091](26634208)) 
  * Typically, in middle aged or older adults ([Indian J Ophthalmol 2016;64:177](27146927)) 
  * No strong predilection for a particular race or sex ([StatPearls: Eyelid Papilloma [Accessed 16 September 2025]](29262072)) 

Sites

  * Eyelid skin as well as other parts of the body where skin folds rub against each other (i.e., neck, axilla, groin) ([StatPearls: Papillomas - A Multisystem Overview of HPV Associated and HPV Independent Lesions [Accessed 16 September 2025]](32809572)) 

Pathophysiology

  * See [Etiology](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeeyelidsquamouspapilloma.html#eyelidsquamouspapillomaetiology)

Etiology

  * Idiopathic benign proliferation in some studies ([Iran J Basic Med Sci 2012;15:840](23493098), [StatPearls: Eyelid Papilloma [Accessed 16 September 2025]](29262072)) 
  * Usually not associated with HPV; however, HPV DNA detection in a fraction of head and neck papillomas supports the role of HPV in the etiology of this disease, especially types 6 and 11 ([Br J Dermatol 1998;138:649](9640372), [Head Neck 2017;39:254](27618734)) 

Clinical features

  * Smooth or rough 
  * Flesh colored lesion 
  * Solitary or multiple 
  * Pigmented or the same color as the skin 
  * Sessile or pedunculated 
  * Painless 
  * Ulceration due to mechanical irritation 
  * Reference: [Am J Dermatopathol 2009;31:256](19384066)

Diagnosis

  * Characteristic clinical features and histopathology 

Prognostic factors

  * Benign 

Case reports

  * 17 year old girl with recurrent eyelid lesion ([Oman J Ophthalmol 2020;13:105](32792811)) 
  * 52 year old man with pigmented squamous papilloma ([Indian J Ophthalmol 1987;35:158](3507413)) 
  * 54 year old woman with a stye-like nonpigmented eyelid lesion ([Case Rep Ophthalmol Med 2019;2019:5830493](31781447)) 
  * 55 year old man with eyelid squamous papilloma and open comedones consistent with Favre-Racouchot syndrome ([J Biomed Res 2012;26:474](23554787)) 
  * 65 year old man with progressively enlarging ulcerated mass ([J Ophthalmic Vis Res 2019;14:509](31875107)) 

Treatment

  * Asymptomatic lesions: observed 
  * For lesions causing irritation or cosmetically bothersome: shave excision ([StatPearls: Eyelid Papilloma [Accessed 16 September 2025]](29262072))

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Bland squamous epithelium 
  * Variable acanthosis with variable hyperkeratosis 
  * Parakeratosis 
  * Orthokeratosis 
  * Central fibrovascular core ([Indian J Ophthalmol 2016;64:177](27146927)) 
  * No dysplasia 
  * In case of mechanical irritation, ulceration and hyperkeratosis

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/eyeeyelidsquamouspapillomaAhmadian03.jpeg

Which histologic feature is most characteristic of an eyelid squamous papilloma?   
  
  1. Fibrovascular cores covered by hyperplastic squamous epithelium 
  2. Koilocytic change with hypergranulosis 
  3. Nodules of basaloid cells with peripheral palisading 
  4. Sheets of atypical squamous cells with mitotic figures 

Practice answer #1

**A.** Fibrovascular cores covered by hyperplastic squamous epithelium. This is the hallmark microscopic finding of squamous papilloma: branching papillae with central fibrovascular cores lined by bland squamous epithelium, sometimes with acanthosis and hyperkeratosis. Answer C is incorrect because these features are more characteristic of basal cell carcinoma. Answer B is incorrect because these features are more characteristic of verruca vulgaris (HPV related wart), not a classic eyelid squamous papilloma. Answer D is incorrect because these findings suggest squamous cell carcinoma, not a benign papilloma.

Practice question #2

Which of the following best describes the typical clinical features of an eyelid squamous papilloma?   
  
  1. Painful ulcerated lesion with irregular borders 
  2. Pigmented keratin filled cystic lesion with horn pseudocysts 
  3. Rapidly enlarging malignant appearing mass with stromal clefting 
  4. Solitary or multiple flesh colored sessile or pedunculated lesion(s) 

Practice answer #2

**D.** Solitary or multiple flesh colored sessile or pedunculated lesion(s). Eyelid squamous papillomas are single or multiple benign slow growing lesions that present clinically as smooth or rough and flesh colored. Answer A is incorrect because the description of ulceration and irregular borders suggests malignancy rather than a benign papilloma. Answer B is incorrect because these features are more characteristic of seborrheic keratosis, not squamous papilloma. Answer C is incorrect because these features describing a malignant appearance with stromal clefting are more consistent with basal cell carcinoma.
