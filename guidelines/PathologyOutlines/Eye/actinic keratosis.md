Definition / general

  * Also called leukoplakic lesions, ultraviolet light related lesions 
  * Usually limbal conjunctiva at advancing head of pterygium 

Case reports

  * 42 year old man with conjunctival mass ([Indian J Ophthalmol 2004;52:154](http://www.ijo.in/article.asp?issn=0301-4738%3Byear=2004%3Bvolume=52%3Bissue=2%3Bspage=154%3Bepage=5%3Baulast=Jain)) 
  * 61 year old man with bilateral limbus lesions ([J Fr Ophtalmol 1992;15:65](1602107)) 
  * 73 year old man with slowly progressive and whitish limbal conjunctival tumor ([J Fr Ophtalmol 1998;21:458](9759445))

Gross description

  * Thickened conjunctiva with leukoplakia 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Sharply demarcated 
  * Mild atypia of epithelium, acanthosis, parakeratosis, hyperkeratosis but normal polarity, normal maturation 
  * Elastotic degeneration and lymphocytes in substantia propria
