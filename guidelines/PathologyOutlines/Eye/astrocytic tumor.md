# Eye

## Retina

### Astrocytic tumor

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeretinaastrocytictumor.html#definitiongeneral92652) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeretinaastrocytictumor.html#microscopichistologicdescription92653)

Definition / general

  * Associated with tuberous sclerosis (57%), neurofibromatosis (14%), or no syndrome (29%) 
  * Tuberous sclerosis patients usually have multiple, peripheral retinal tumors with giant astrocytes vs. disc based tumors in non tuberous sclerosis patients 
  * Usually benign and extremely rare in retina 
  * References: [Arch Pathol Lab Med 1984;108:160](6421263)

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Interlacing, spindle shaped astrocytes
