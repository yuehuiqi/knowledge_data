# Eye

## Uvea (iris, choroid and ciliary body), limbus and sclera

### Ciliary body adenoma and adenocarcinoma

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeuveafuchsadenoma.html#definitiongeneral93566) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeuveafuchsadenoma.html#microscopichistologicdescription93567)

Definition / general

  * Also called benign ciliary epithelioma, coronal adenoma 
  * Benign tumor of ciliary body 
  * Usually incidental finding in enucleated eye or at autopsy 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Interlacing trabeculae of uniform, nonpigmented ciliary epithelial cells, surrounded by amorphous and PAS+ hyaline material
