# Eye

## Orbit other than optic nerve

### Mucocele-orbit

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeorbitmucocele.html#definitiongeneral92350) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeorbitmucocele.html#microscopichistologicdescription92351)

Definition / general

  * Due to chronic inflammation of frontal or ethmoid sinus 
  * Lesion erodes sinus wall, produces downward and lateral displacement of globe 
  * Insidious onset with slow and asymptomatic enlargement 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Cystic mass lined by ciliated, mucus-secreting columnar epithelium with scarring and inflammation
