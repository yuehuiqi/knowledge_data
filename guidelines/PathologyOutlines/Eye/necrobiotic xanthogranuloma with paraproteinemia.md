### Necrobiotic xanthogranuloma with paraproteinemia

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyenecrobiotic.html#definitiongeneral90357) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyenecrobiotic.html#microscopichistologicdescription90358)

Definition / general

  * Multiple nodules or plaques in periorbital areas and elsewhere in body 
  * Patients always have IgG paraprotein dysproteinemia 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Granulomas with collagen necrobiosis, foamy macrophages, Touton giant cells
