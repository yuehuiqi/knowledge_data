## Retina

### Nodular & massive retinal gliosis

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeretinamassiveretinalgliosis.html#definitiongeneral92774) | [Case reports](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeretinamassiveretinalgliosis.html#casereports92775) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeretinamassiveretinalgliosis.html#microscopichistologicdescription92776) | [Positive stains](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyeretinamassiveretinalgliosis.html#positivestains92777)

Definition / general

  * Relatively uncommon 
  * Large elevated scar near disc and posterior pole after hemorrhage or inflammation 
  * Polyclonal - not a neoplasm 

Case reports

  * 32 year old woman born with bilateral microphthalmia ([Hum Pathol 2005;36:702](16021579)) 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Vitreous body replaced by massively proliferating spindle cells containing fibrillary cytoplasm but no nuclear atypia 

Positive stains
