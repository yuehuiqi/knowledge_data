### Molluscum contagiosum

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyemolluscum.html#definitiongeneral90353) | [Treatment](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyemolluscum.html#treatment90354) | [Gross description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyemolluscum.html#grossdescription90355) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyemolluscum.html#microscopichistologicdescription90356)

Definition / general

  * Poxvirus that produces small nodules on eyelid 
  * See [Skin nontumor](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/skinnontumormolluscum.html) chapter 

Treatment

  * Excision if on eyelid margin to prevent infection of ocular surface 

Gross description

  * Multiple raised skin nodules with umbilicated centers 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Lobules of acanthotic epithelium that surround intracytoplasmic eosinophilic inclusion bodies (molluscum bodies) which become larger as they reach superficial epithelium
