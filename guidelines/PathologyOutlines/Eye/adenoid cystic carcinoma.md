## Lacrimal duct / gland

### Adenoid cystic carcinoma

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyelacrimaladenoidcystic.html#definitiongeneral91255) | [Radiology images](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyelacrimaladenoidcystic.html#radiologyimages91256) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyelacrimaladenoidcystic.html#microscopichistologicdescription91257) | [Microscopic (histologic) images](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/eyelacrimaladenoidcystic.html#microscopichistologicimages91258)

Definition / general

  * 20 - 30% of lacrimal gland tumors 
  * Most common malignant tumor of lacrimal gland 
  * Aggressive, may infiltrate eyelid or brain

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Basaloid growth with cribriform change 
  * Relatively bland cytology 
  * Perineural invasion, focal tumor necrosis
