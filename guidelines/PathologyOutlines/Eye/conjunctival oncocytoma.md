Definition / general

  * Usually caruncle ([Arch Ophthalmol 1977;95:474](843279), [Ophthal Plast Reconstr Surg 2012;28:14](21956543)) 
  * Often elderly (mean age 73), more common in women 
  * Proposed origin from lacrimal and accessory lacrimal glands based on cytokeratin profile ([Acta Ophthalmol 2011;89:263](19843013)) 

Case reports

  * 51 year old woman with tumor of caruncle ([Int Ophthalmol 2004;25:321](16532295)) 
  * 60 year old woman with tumor of caruncle ([Klin Monatsbl Augenheilkd 2005;222:733](16175484)) 
  * 72 year old woman with tumor of bulbar conjunctiva ([Klin Monatsbl Augenheilkd 1996;209:176](8992082)) 
  * 75 year old woman with tumor of caruncle ([Indian J Ophthalmol 2002;50:60](12090093)) 

Gross description

  * Red-orange or yellow-tan mass 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Solid nests and cords of polyhedral cells with abundant, finely granular acidophilic cytoplasm and round / oval paracentral nuclei, usually with one prominent nucleolus 
  * May have microcystic areas with occasional goblet cells ([Am J Dermatopathol 2007;29:279](17519627)), may have malignant histology and behavior
