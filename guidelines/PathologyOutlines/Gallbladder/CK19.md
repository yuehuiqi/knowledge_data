Definition / general

  * Molecular weight is 40 kDa (smallest cytokeratin) 
  * Often coexpressed with CK7 
  * Present in both simple and complex epithelium 
  * Involved in the organization of myofibers; links contractile apparatus to dystrophin at costameres of striated muscle (also CK8, [Mol Biol Cell 2005;16:4280](16000376)) 
  * Polymorphisms of CK19 pseudogene are associated with primary biliary cirrhosis ([Hepatol Res 2003;25:281](12697249)) 

Uses by pathologists

  1. Bladder: possible urine screening test for bladder carcinoma ([J Egypt Natl Canc Inst 2006;18:82](17237854)) 
  2. Bone / soft tissue: distinguish chordoma (CK19+) from parachordoma (CK19-, [Ann Diagn Pathol 1997;1:3](9869820)) 
  3. Breast: presence of CK19+ peripheral blood tumor cells or CK19+ fragments is a poor prognostic factor for breast cancer (predicts CNS relapse, [Breast Cancer Res 2006;8:R36](16846533)) 
  4. Liver: distinguish hepatocellular carcinoma (CK19-) from either hepatoid adenocarcinoma metastatic to liver (CK19+, [Am J Surg Pathol 2003;27:1302](14508391)) or cholangiocarcinoma (CK19+, [J Gastrointestin Liver Dis 2006;15:9](16680226), [Am J Clin Pathol 2006;125:519](16627262)) 
  5. Liver: poor prognostic factor in hepatocellular carcinoma ([Histopathology 2006;49:138](16879391), [Cancer Sci 2003;94:851](14556657)) 
  6. Lung: poor prognostic factor for non small cell lung carcinoma ([Ann N Y Acad Sci 2006;1075:244](17108218), [Cancer 2006;107:2842](17103443)) 
  7. Pancreas: poor prognostic factor in pancreatic endocrine neoplasms ([Am J Surg Pathol 2004;28:1145](15316313), [Am J Surg Pathol 2006;30:1588](17122516)) 
  8. Thyroid: confirm diagnosis of papillary thyroid carcinoma in cytology or equivocal cases ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2003;127:579](12708901), [Mod Pathol 2006;19:1631](16998461)); help distinguish follicular variant of papillary thyroid carcinoma (CK19+) from (a) follicular adenoma (CK19-, [Endocr Pathol 2006;17:213](17308358), [Am J Clin Pathol 2006;126:700](17050067) but see [Am J Clin Pathol 2001;116:696](11710686)), (b) hyalinizing trabecular adenoma (CK19-, [Am J Surg Pathol 2006;30:1269](17001158)), (c) Grave’s disease (weak / negative CK19, [Endocr Pathol 2005;16:63](16000848)), (d) multinodular goiter with papillary areas ([Endocr Pathol 2002;13:207](12446919)); note that CK19 may stain benign thyroid lesions 
  9. Metastases: RT-PCR detects nodal and marrow metastases in various carcinomas - bladder carcinoma (poorer survival, [Clin Cancer Res 2005;11:3773](15897575)), breast ([Anticancer Res 2006;26:3855](17094413), [Jpn J Clin Oncol 2003;33:167](12810830)), gastric ([World J Gastroenterol 2006;12:5219](16937537)), head and neck squamous cell ([Br J Cancer 2006;94:1164](16622440)), skin ([Br J Dermatol 2003;149:998](14632805)); note that pelvic lymph nodes may have false positives ([Int J Cancer 2007;120:1842](17266039)) 
  10. Peripheral blood tumor cells: RT-PCR detects peripheral blood tumor cells in carcinoma of cervix ([Gynecol Oncol 2002;85:148](   11925135)), colon ([Gut 2002;50:530](11889075)), gallbladder ([Rev Med Chil 2004;132:1489](15743160)), pancreas ([World J Gastroenterol 2007;13:257](17226905)); the significance of these tumor cells is unclear ([Ann Oncol 2005;16:1845](15972276)) 

Positive staining - normal

  * Anal transition zone ([Histopathology 1995;26:39](7536178)) 
  * Bile ducts and ductules 
  * Breast (ductal and secretory cells) 
  * Colon 
  * Conjunctiva ([Invest Ophthalmol Vis Sci 2006;47:4780](17065488)) 
  * GI epithelium 
  * Hair follicles ([J Invest Dermatol 1989;92:707](2469734)) 
  * Muscle fibers 
  * Myoepithelium 
  * Nipple epidermis 
  * Pancreatic ducts ([Pancreas 2005;30:158](15714138)) 
  * Salivary gland acini ([Res Commun Mol Pathol Pharmacol 1998;101:115](9821208)) 
  * Squamous epithelium (basal layers, [BMC Cancer 2006;6:10](16412231)) 
  * Sweat glands 
  * Umbilical cord 
  * urothelium 

Positive staining - not malignant

  * Adamantinoma ([Pathol Int 2000;50:801](11107052)) 
  * Ameloblastoma (stellate reticulum-like areas, [Bull Tokyo Dent Coll 2002;43:13](12013821)) 
  * Cholesteatoma ([Histol Histopathol 2007;22:37](17128409)) 
  * Chordoma (references above) 
  * Hepatoblastoma (embryonal subtype, [Pediatr Dev Pathol 2006;9:196](16944967)) 
  * Oral dysplasia ([Zhonghua Kou Qiang Yi Xue Za Zhi 2002;37:187](12419140)) 
  * Posterior polymorphous corneal dystrophy ([Exp Eye Res 2007;84:680](17289024)) 
  * Pulmonary interstitial pneumonia - hyaline membranes (some, [Pathology 2003;35:120](   12745458)) 
  * Synovial sarcoma ([Histopathology 1998;33:501](9870143)) 
  * Syringocystadenoma papilliferum of skin ([Br J Dermatol 2002;147:936](12410704)) 

Positive staining - malignant

  * Anal ([Virchows Arch 2001;439:782](11787851)) 
  * Breast ([Med Mol Morphol 2006;39:8](16575508)) 
  * Cholangiocarcinoma (references above) 
  * Endometrial ([Int J Gynecol Pathol 2011;30:484](21804393)) 
  * Hepatoid adenocarcinoma (references above) 
  * Kidney: medullary, mucinous & tubular spindle cell ([Virchows Arch 2005;447:978](16231179)) medullary, papillary, renal cell (collecting duct-[Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2002;10:332](12613443)), tubulocystic, urothelial ([Am J Surg Pathol 2005;29:747](15897741)) 
  * Lung ([Histopathology 2004;45:125](15279630)) 
  * Paget disease (extramammary, [Histopathology 2006;48:723](16681689)) 
  * Pancreatic ductal (references above) 
  * Squamous cell (various sites-[Histopathology 1993;23:45](7690007)) 
  * Thyroid papillary (references above) 

Negative stains

  * Cornea ([Cornea 2003;22:533](12883346)) 
  * Hepatocytes ([Hepatology 1996;23:476](8617427)) 
  * Hepatocellular carcinoma (usually) 
  * Pancreatic islets 
  * Parachordoma 
  * Thyroid hyalinizing trabecular adenoma 
  * Trichilemmoma ([Br J Dermatol 2003;149:99](12890201))
