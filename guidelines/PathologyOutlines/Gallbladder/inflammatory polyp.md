Definition / general

  * Benign lesion of the gallbladder that arises from an inflammatory process, such as repeated bouts of cholecystitis and biliary colic ([Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:467](31725469)) 
  * Accounts for 10% of all gallbladder polyps ([J Ultrasound 2021;24:131](33548050)) 

Essential features

  * Nonneoplastic lesion that is linked to chronic cholecystitis or gallstones ([Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:467](31725469)) 
  * Typically small (< 10 mm) and asymptomatic, often discovered incidentally during imaging or histopathological examination ([Cureus 2021;13:e16113](34350077)) 
  * Composed of lymphocytes, plasma cells, granulation tissue and fibrous tissue elements ([Euroasian J Hepatogastroenterol 2019;9:40](31988866)) 
  * No malignant potential ([HPB (Oxford) 2020;22:506](31481314)) 

Terminology

  * Pseudopolyp ([Euroasian J Hepatogastroenterol 2019;9:40](31988866)) 

ICD coding

  * ICD-10 
    * [K82.8](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/K00-K95/K80-K87/K82-/K82.8) \- other specified diseases of gallbladder 
    * [K81.1](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/K00-K95/K80-K87/K81-/K81.1) \- chronic cholecystitis 

Epidemiology

  * Associated with chronic cholecystitis and cholelithiasis ([Gut Liver 2012;6:172](22570746)) 
  * Prevalent in older patients ([J Assoc Physicians India 2022;70:11](35062812)) 

Sites

  * Mucosal surface of the gallbladder in any part of the gallbladder wall ([PLoS One 2020;15:e0237979](32915805)) 

Pathophysiology

  * Chronic irritation from gallstones or infection leads to repeated cycles of inflammation and irritation 
  * Inflammatory cell infiltration (lymphocytes, plasma cells, macrophages) results in granulation tissue formation and fibrosis ([Yeungnam Univ J Med 2021;38:1](33045805)) 

Etiology

  * Cholelithiasis 
  * Chronic cholecystitis 
  * Biliary colic 
  * References: [Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:467](31725469), [Cureus 2021;13:e16113](34350077)

Clinical features

  * Usually asymptomatic and discovered incidentally during imaging or cholecystectomy 
  * In symptomatic cases, patients may present with right upper quadrant pain and epigastric pain ([Surg Endosc 2017;31:2776](28039652)) 

Diagnosis

  * Diagnosed on microscopic evaluation of H&E stained slides ([Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:467](31725469)) 

Laboratory

  * May have mild elevations in inflammatory markers or liver enzymes if associated with cholecystitis ([Surg Endosc 2017;31:2776](28039652)) 

Radiology description

  * Ultrasound: sessile or pedunculated polyp characterized by variable echogenicity compared to the liver parenchyma, usually < 10 mm in diameter, protruding into the gallbladder lumen, with absence of postacoustic shadow ([Yeungnam Univ J Med 2021;38:1](33045805)) 

Prognostic factors

  * Benign with no risk of malignancy ([HPB (Oxford) 2020;22:506](31481314)) 

Case reports

  * 69 year old man with inflammatory polyp with features similar to polypoid carcinoma ([Case Rep Gastroenterol 2009;3:255](21103284)) 
  * 72 year old woman diagnosed with inflammatory fibroid polyp of gallbladder ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2013;137:721](23627457)) 
  * 78 year old man diagnosed with gallbladder inflammatory pseudopolyps after presenting with chest pain ([Diagnostics 2022;12:155](35054322)) 

Treatment

  * Observation for polyps < 10 mm in asymptomatic patients with no risk factors 
  * Cholecystectomy for symptomatic patients, polyps > 10 mm in size or when malignancy cannot be excluded ([World J Gastroenterol 2018;24:2844](30018479)) 

Gross description

  * Gallbladder wall thickening, fibrosis and inflammation ([Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:467](31725469))

Cytology description

  * Inflammatory polyps are usually diagnosed histologically rather than through cytology; however, if sampled, cytologic smears may show mixed inflammatory cells, predominately lymphocytes and plasma cells ([Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:467](31725469)) 

Sample pathology report

  * Gallbladder, cholecystectomy: 
    * Chronic cholecystitis with cholelithiasis and inflammatory polyp 

Differential diagnosis

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/gallbladderinflammatorypolypevason02.jpg

A 52 year old woman underwent a cholecystectomy for chronic right upper quadrant pain. Histologic examination of a gallbladder polyp reveals a sessile mucosal lesion composed of granulation tissue, inflammatory cells and fibrous tissue. No dysplasia is noted. What is the most likely diagnosis?   
  
  1. Adenomatous polyp 
  2. Cholesterol polyp 
  3. Inflammatory polyp 
  4. Xanthogranulomatous cholecystitis 

Practice answer #1

**C**. Inflammatory polyp. Inflammatory gallbladder polyps are characterized by prominent inflammation and granulation tissue. Answer A is incorrect because there is no dysplasia noted here. Answer B is incorrect because cholesterol polyps are characterized by foamy macrophages without significant inflammation. Answer D is incorrect because xanthogranulomatous cholecystitis typically has foamy macrophages and is not polypoid.

Practice question #2

Inflammatory gallbladder polyps are most likely to arise in which of the following clinical settings?   
  
  1. Autoimmune hepatitis 
  2. Chronic cholecystitis 
  3. Familial adenomatous polyposis 
  4. Hyperlipidemia 

Practice answer #2

**B**. Chronic cholecystitis. Inflammatory polyps are reactive lesions that arise in the context of chronic inflammation, such as cholecystitis. Answer A is incorrect because autoimmune hepatitis is not associated with gallbladder polyp formation. Answer C is incorrect because familial adenomatous polyposis predisposes to adenomatous gallbladder polyps, not inflammatory polyps. Answer D is incorrect because hyperlipidemia is associated with cholesterol polyps, not inflammatory polyps.
