Definition / general

  * Benign polypoid variant of [cholesterolosis](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/gallbladdercholesterolosis.html)

Essential features

  * Polypoid variant of cholesterolosis 
  * Cauliflower-like architecture with core of foamy lipid laden macrophages 
  * Benign 

ICD coding

  * ICD-10: [K82.4](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/K00-K95/K80-K87/K82-/K82.4) \- cholesterolosis of gallbladder 
  * ICD-11: [DC10.3](https://icd.who.int/browse11/l-m/en#/http%3a%2f%2fid.who.int%2ficd%2fentity%2f839854600) \- polyp of gallbladder 

Epidemiology

  * Cholesterol polyps are the most common type of gallbladder polyp, constituting 60 - 90% of all polyps ([N Am J Med Sci 2012;4:203](22655278)) 
  * Slightly more common in women (F:M = 2.2:1) 
  * Associated with metabolic syndrome, high BMI and young age (40 - 50 years) ([N Am J Med Sci 2012;4:203](22655278), [Lipids Health Dis 2021;20:26](33752687), [Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:467](31725469)) 

Sites

  * Almost always arises in gallbladder 
  * Possible in the common bile duct but very rare 

Pathophysiology

  * Lipids deposit inside macrophages, resulting in the formation of a distinct polyp 
  * Etiologic mechanism is not clear 
  * Suggested etiologies include dyslipidemia, high bile viscosity and increased expression of mucin genes ([Lipids Health Dis 2021;20:26](33752687), [Int J Biol Markers 2016;31:e73](26541419), [BMC Gastroenterol 2020;20:268](32799793), [Gut Liver 2016;10:851](27563024)) 

Clinical features

  * Most are asymptomatic 
  * May detach and behave like gallstones, causing biliary colic, obstruction, nausea, vomiting and rarely pancreatitis ([N Am J Med Sci 2012;4:203](22655278)) 

Diagnosis

  * Incidental finding during abdominal ultrasound or on histopathologic examination following cholecystectomy 
  * Definitive diagnosis requires microscopic examination ([J Ultrasound 2021;24:131](33548050)) 

Laboratory

  * Risk factors for cholesterol polyp formation are closely related to lipid metabolism ([Lipids Health Dis 2021;20:26](33752687)) 

Radiology description

  * Usually multiple, pedunculated, small (< 1 cm) ([J Ultrasound 2021;24:131](33548050)) 
  * Transabdominal ultrasound 
    * Homogeneous, slightly more hyperechoic than liver parenchyma 
    * Immobile / fixed despite positional change 
    * Posterior acoustic shadowing is absent

Prognostic factors

  * Benign with no malignant potential ([Lipids Health Dis 2021;20:26](33752687)) 

Case reports

  * 39 year old man with acute cholecystitis ([J Nippon Med Sch 2001;68:259](11404773)) 
  * 53 year old woman with polyps in the distal common bile duct ([Medicine (Baltimore) 2016;95:e5374](27828866)) 
  * 62 year old man with large cholesterol polyp clinically mimicking carcinoma ([Abdom Imaging 2004;29:100](15160761)) 

Treatment

  * Asymptomatic polyps diagnosed incidentally can be managed by clinical follow up 
  * If symptomatic, cholecystectomy 

Gross description

  * Yellow rounded polyps with smooth contours 
  * Up to 67% of cases are associated with cholesterolosis ([Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:467](31725469))

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Cauliflower-like architecture is a distinctive / pathognomonic feature present in all cases and is generally not seen in other polyps 
  * Connected to the gallbladder via very thin stalks; hence, they may detach from the surface 
  * Foamy lipid laden macrophages generally make up the wide and edematous core of the polyp 
    * 15% of cholesterol polyps may lack these lipid laden macrophages 
  * Lined by a single layer of normal gallbladder epithelium 
  * No epithelial elements in the core of the polyp and no dysplasia 
  * Cholesterol polyps can be found in gallbladders devoid of any significant chronic changes ([Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:467](31725469))

    * Cholesterol polyps (see comment)

Differential diagnosis

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/gallbladdercholesterolpolypBychkov01new.jpg

A gallbladder resection specimen is found to contain a polyp with the histologic appearance shown above. What is the diagnosis?   
  
  1. Adenomyomatosis 
  2. Cholesterol polyp 
  3. Hyperplastic polyp 
  4. Inflammatory polyp 
  5. Intracholecystic papillary neoplasm 

Practice answer #1

**B.** Cholesterol polyp. Cholesterol polyps have cauliflower-like architecture and are made of foamy lipid laden macrophages (both features seen in the image provided above). Answer E is incorrect because the polyp lining epithelium is normal biliary epithelium unlike in intracholecystic papillary neoplasms. Answer D is incorrect because the body of the polyp does not show inflammatory cells as in inflammatory polyps. Answer A is incorrect because the body of the polyp does not show hyperplastic smooth muscle as in adenomyomatosis. Answer C is incorrect because the polyp does not show hyperplastic gallbladder epithelium with elongated villi as in hyperplastic polyps.

Practice question #2

Which of the following is a gross feature of cholesterol polyps of the gallbladder?   
  
  1. Most are > 1 cm 
  2. Most are sessile 
  3. The surface exhibits a gritty texture 
  4. They are usually solitary 
  5. They exhibit a yellowish color 

Practice answer #2

**E.** They exhibit a yellowish color. Answer C is incorrect because cholesterol polyps have a smooth yellowish surface. Answer B is incorrect because most are pedunculated. Answer A is incorrect because most are < 1 cm. Answer D is incorrect because multiple / multifocal cholesterol polyps often coexist.
