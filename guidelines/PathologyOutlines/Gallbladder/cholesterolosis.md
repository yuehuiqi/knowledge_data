Definition / general

  * Accumulation of lipids (triglycerides, cholesterol precursors and cholesterol esters) within subepithelial macrophages in the lamina propria of the gallbladder ([J Ultrasound 2021;24:131](33548050)) 
  * Can be focal, diffuse or polypoid (also known as cholesterol polyps) ([Minerva Gastroenterol Dietol 2003;49:217](16484961)) 
  * Second most common histopathological finding in resected gallbladders (most common being chronic cholecystitis) ([JSLS 2020;24:e2020.00052](33100819), [Rev Col Bras Cir 2020;46:e20192279](31967243)) 

Essential features

  * Due to accumulation of lipids in macrophages in the lamina propria 
  * Demographic: females, high BMI 
  * Focal, diffuse or polypoid (polypoid cholesterolosis = cholesterol polyps) 
  * Gross feature: focal or diffuse flat yellowish dots on the lining of the gallbladder 
    * When diffuse, resembles a strawberry, hence the name strawberry gallbladder 
  * Microscopic features: subepithelial lipid laden macrophages in the lamina propria and inside villi 

Terminology

  * Also known as strawberry gallbladder 

ICD coding

  * ICD-10: [K82.4](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/K00-K95/K80-K87/K82-/K82.4) \- cholesterolosis of gallbladder 
  * ICD-11: [DC10.4](https://icd.who.int/browse11/l-m/en#/http:%2f%2fid.who.int%2ficd%2fentity%2f2109132211) \- cholesterolosis of gallbladder 

Epidemiology

  * Seen in 10 - 30% of cholecystectomies ([Rev Col Bras Cir 2020;46:e20192279](31967243), [Cureus 2020;12:e9627](32923228)) 
  * Occurs in female patients 75% of the time ([Niger J Clin Pract 2019;22:1002](31293268)) 
  * Associated with high BMI ([Niger J Clin Pract 2019;22:1002](31293268)) 

Sites

  * Gallbladder 

Pathophysiology

  * Bile is supersaturated with cholesterol in both cholesterolosis and gallstone disease, perhaps due to excess bile production 
  * Patients who are unable to fully solubilize cholesterol will form cholesterol gallstones 
  * Patients who are able to keep cholesterol fully solubilized may have increased mucosal cholesterol uptake and develop cholesterolosis ([J Clin Pathol 1987;40:524](3584503)) 
    * This likely occurs in patients with increased acyl-CoA cholesterol ester acyltransferase activity in gallbladder mucosa ([Am J Gastroenterol 1998;93:1518](9732935)) 
    * The enzyme causes increased synthesis of cholesterol esters, which accumulate in mucosal macrophages ([Am J Gastroenterol 1998;93:1518](9732935)) 

Clinical features

  * Asymptomatic ([J Ultrasound 2021;24:131](33548050), [Semin Gastrointest Dis 2003;14:178](14719768)) 
  * Noninflammatory (is usually an isolated finding) ([Rev Col Bras Cir 2020;46:e20192279](31967243)) 
  * No malignant potential ([Rev Col Bras Cir 2020;46:e20192279](31967243)) 

Diagnosis

  * Incidentally found during abdominal sonography (appears as a pseudopolyp; see [Radiology description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/gallbladdercholesterolosis.html#gallbladdercholesterolosisradiology) for details) or diagnosed on histopathology of surgical specimens ([J Ultrasound 2021;24:131](33548050)) 
  * Microscopic examination provides the definitive diagnosis 

Laboratory

  * Serum cholesterol may be normal or elevated 

Radiology description

  * Ultrasound cannot reliably detect cholesterolosis ([Semin Gastrointest Dis 2003;14:178](14719768)) 
  * Cholesterolosis may form pseudopolyps that appear as single or multiple hyperechoic parietal foci generating comet tail artifacts ([J Ultrasound 2021;24:131](33548050))

Cholesterolosis

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Foamy lipid laden macrophages expanding the lamina propria 
  * Mucosal villous hyperplasia and hypertrophy ([Semin Gastrointest Dis 2003;14:178](14719768), [Cureus 2020;12:e9627](32923228)) 
  * Usually isolated, with no signs of inflammation 
    * When cholesterolosis is not an isolated finding, most common association would be chronic cholecystitis ([Rev Col Bras Cir 2020;46:e20192279](31967243))

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/gallbladdercholesterolosisalshammashamasha04.jpg

The lesion shown above is observed during microscopic examination of a cholecystectomy specimen. Which of the following is most likely true?  
  
  1. The lesion caused severe clinical symptoms 
  2. The lesion has a high risk of progression to malignancy 
  3. The patient is female 
  4. The patient is very thin 

Practice answer #1

**C.** The patient is female. Cholesterolosis tends to occur in overweight female patients. It is generally asymptomatic and has no risk of progression to malignancy.

Practice question #2

Which of the following is the most likely pathophysiologic cause of gallbladder cholesterolosis?  
  
  1. Increased synthesis and deposition of cholesterol esters 
  2. Infiltration of macrophages due to subclinical infection 
  3. Mucosal irritation due to longstanding inflammation 
  4. Slow degradation and absorption of gallstones 

Practice answer #2

**A.** Increased synthesis and deposition of cholesterol esters. Cholesterolosis appears to occur when excess bile is converted to cholesterol esters via acyl-CoA cholesterol ester acyltransferase. The esters are then stored in mucosal macrophages. Choleliths, inflammation and infection do not play a role in the development of cholesterolosis.
