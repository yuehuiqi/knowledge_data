Definition / general

  * This classification is applicable to tumors that occur in the adrenal gland 

Major updates

  * Nonneoplastic entities including adrenal rests, adrenal cysts and congenital adrenal hyperplasia have been added 
  * Adrenocortical nodular disease has been added 
    * As the nodules may be clonal proliferations, the previous term (hyperplasia) is no longer utilized and has been replaced with the term disease 
    * Sporadic nodules are < 1.0 cm and are nonfunctional, while bilateral disease usually yields Cushing syndrome and can be micronodular (< 1.0 cm) or macronodular (> 1.0 cm) 
  * Paraganglioma has been separated into 2 entities: sympathetic paraganglioma and parasympathetic paraganglioma

**ICD-O note**

  * First 4 digits indicate the specific histological term 
  * Fifth digit after the slash (/) is the behavior code, including 
    * /0 for benign tumors 
    * /1 for unspecified, borderline or uncertain behavior 
    * /2 for carcinoma in situ and grade III intraepithelial neoplasia (not used for adrenal tumors) 
    * /3 for malignant tumors

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/adrenalwhoZynger01.jpg

Which of these tumors arises from within the adrenal gland?   
  
  1. Carotid body paraganglioma 
  2. Jugulotympanic paraganglioma 
  3. Laryngeal paraganglioma 
  4. Pheochromocytoma 
  5. Vagal paraganglioma 

Practice answer #1

**D.** Pheochromocytoma arises from the adrenal gland. Answers A, B, C and E are incorrect because paraganglioma arises in an extra-adrenal location.
