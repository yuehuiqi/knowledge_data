Definition / general

  * Calcium binding protein ([Hum Pathol 2003;34:994](14608532)) 

Essential features

  * Nuclear and cytoplasmic staining 
  * Used primarily in the diagnosis of Hirschsprung disease and to confirm sex cord stromal or mesothelial lineage 
  * Also positive in adrenal cortical lesions, mesonephric carcinoma, female adnexal tumor of probable Wolffian origin (FATWO) and _STK11_ adnexal tumor 

Pathophysiology

  * 29 kilodalton calcium binding protein of the calmodulin superfamily ([Hum Pathol 2003;34:994](14608532)) 
  * Function is not clearly understood but it is thought to act as a buffer or sensor of intracellular calcium ions ([Hum Pathol 2006;37:312](16613326), [Hum Pathol 2003;34:994](14608532)) 

Interpretation

  * Nuclear and cytoplasmic ([Mod Pathol 2007;20:248](17361207)) 

Uses by pathologists

  * Differentiate (as part of a panel) pleural mesothelioma (positive) from lung adenocarcinoma (negative) ([Mod Pathol 2007;20:248](17361207), [Indian J Pathol Microbiol 2021;64:655](34673582), [Am J Surg Pathol 2003;27:150](12548160), [Arch Pathol Lab Med 2018;142:89](28686500)) 
  * Differentiate (as part of a panel) peritoneal mesothelioma (positive) from ovarian serous carcinoma (negative); however, calretinin can stain a significant minority of serous carcinomas ([Am J Surg Pathol 2007;31:1139](17667535), [Am J Surg Pathol 1998;22:1203](9777982)) 
  * Confirm mesothelial origin in pleural or peritoneal specimens ([Hum Pathol 2003;34:994](14608532)) 
  * Diagnosis of Hirschsprung disease (showing an absence of mucosal innervation) ([Pediatr Dev Pathol 2021;24:19](32975470), [Mod Pathol 2009;22:1379](19648883)) 
  * Differentiate (as part of a panel) adrenal cortical lesions (positive) from metastatic renal cell carcinoma (negative) ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:678](21490444)) 
  * Differentiate (as part of a panel) adrenal cortical lesions (positive) from pheochromocytoma (negative) ([Am J Surg Pathol 2010;34:423](20154585)) 
  * Diagnosing (as part of panel) sex cord stromal tumors (ovarian and testis) ([Hum Pathol 2003;34:994](14608532), [Am J Surg Pathol 2021;45:1303](34232606), [Hum Pathol 2005;36:195](15754297)) 
  * Used as part of a panel in diagnosing cervical mesonephric carcinoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2018;42:1596](30148742), [Am J Surg Pathol 2001;25:379](11224609)) 
  * Used as part of a panel in the diagnosis of _STK11_ adnexal tumors and FATWO ([Am J Surg Pathol 2021;45:1061](33534223), [Mod Pathol 2020;33:734](31591497)) 
  * Differentiate schwannoma (positive) from neurofibroma (negative or focal) ([Am J Clin Pathol 2004;122:552](15487453))

Sample pathology report

  * Right pleura, core needle biopsy: 
    * Mesothelioma 
    * Immunohistochemistry performed shows the following staining profile in the lesional cells: 
      * Positive: calretinin, WT1 
      * Negative: TTF1, Claudin4 

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/stainscalretininBaniak04.jpg

A pleural fluid specimen consists of abundant clusters of atypical cells. Pancytokeratin and WT1 stains are positive, while Claudin4 and BerEp4 are negative. The figure above shows a calretinin stain. What is the most appropriate statement?   
  
  1. The biomarker result is noncontributory due to a lack of specificity 
  2. The biomarker result is supportive of a lung adenocarcinoma 
  3. The biomarker result is supportive of a lung squamous cell carcinoma 
  4. The biomarker result is supportive of malignant mesothelioma 

Practice answer #1

**D.** The biomarker result is supportive of malignant mesothelioma
