Definition / general

  * Name is from its solubility in 100% saturated ammonium sulfate at neutral pH 
  * Family of multigenic group of nonubiquitous cytoplasmic homologous intracellular Ca2+ binding proteins with EF hand motifs and significant structural similarities at both protein and genomic levels 
  * Low molecular weights (9 -1 3 kDa) and are able to form heterodimers, homodimers and oligomeric assemblies 
  * S100 is composed of two main subunits, an alpha and a beta chain; most clinical stains use antibodies to the beta chain 
  * S100 protein family has 25 known members solely in vertebrates coded by unique genes 
  * There are 22 proteins (S100A1-A8, trichohylin, filaggrin and repetin) mapped onto chromosome locus 1q21, while the other proteins are located on chromosome loci 4p16 (S100P), 5q14 (S100Z), 21q22(S100B), Xp22(S100G), 5q13(S100Z), 7q22-q3(S100A11P) ([Curr Mol Med 2013;13:24](22834835), [Nat Rev Cancer 2015;15:96](25614008)) 
  * Structurally similar to calmodulin and troponin-C but these proteins are cell specific and dependent upon environmental factors via cytokines, growth factors and toll-like receptors (TLR) ligands ([Curr Mol Med 2013;13:24](22834835), [Nat Rev Cancer 2015;15:96](25614008)) 

Essential features

  * Family of multigenic group of nonubiquitous cytoplasmic homologous intracellular Ca2+ binding proteins with EF hand motifs and significant structural similarities at both protein and genomic levels 
  * Found in melanoma, nerve sheath tumors, clear cell sarcoma of soft tissue, various histiocytic tumors, glial tumors and myoepithelial tumors 
  * Aid in diagnosis of tumors of unknown origin and to show nerve sheath involvement 

Interpretation

  * Cytoplasmic and nuclear staining usually required to call positive 

Uses by pathologists

  * Marker of schwann cells and melanocytes; useful for evaluating nerve sheath tumors and melanoma 
  * Differentiate benign nerve sheath tumors (S100 strong and diffuse) from malignant peripheral nerve sheath tumors (typically weak or negative S100) ([Am J Surg Pathol 2005;29:1042](16006798)) 
  * Help differentiate between schwannoma (stains all cells) and neurofibroma (mixture of positive and negative cells) 
  * Distinguish Langerhans cell histiocytosis from other histiocytosis syndromes ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2015;139:1211](26414464)) 
  * Identifying perineural involvement 
  * Myoepithelial cell marker and identification of tumors with myoepithelial cells 
  * MelanA and MART1 superior to S100 for evaluating sentinel lymph nodes for melanoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2001;25:1039](11474288))

Positive staining - normal

  * Neurons, schwann cells, melanocytes, glial cells 
  * Myoepithelial cells 
  * Adipocytes 
  * Langerhans cells, tissue dendritic cells and interdigitating dendritic cells 
  * Chondrocytes and notochordal cells 

Positive staining - disease

  * Mesenchymal: 
    * Nerve sheath tumors: schwannoma (diffuse), ganglioneuroma (diffuse), dermal nerve sheath myxoma, neurofibroma (predominantly positive with negative nonschwannian component) 
    * Malignant peripheral nerve sheath tumor (30 - 67% show any staining; low grade lesions typically diffuse and conventional high grade examples are weak / patchy) ([J Clin Pathol 2003 Nov;56:826](14600126)) 
    * Adipocytic component of lipogenic tumors 
    * Chondroid tumors: enchondroma, osteochondroma, chondrosarcoma, chondromyxoid fibroma, chondroblastoma and mesenchymal chondrosarcoma (chondrocytic component only) ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2012;136:61](22208489)) 
    * Notochordal tumors: benign notochordal cell tumor, conventional chordoma, chondroid chordoma ([Mod Pathol 2008;21:1461](18820665)) 
    * Clear cell sarcoma of soft tissue ([Am J Surg Pathol 2008;32:452](18300804)) 
    * Granular cell tumor ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2004;128:771](15214825)) 
    * Biphenotypic sinonasal sarcoma ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2018;142:1196](30281368)) 
    * Ossifying fibromyxoid tumor ([Am J Surg Pathol 2008;32:996](18469710)) 
    * Extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma (31%) ([Pathol Int 2005;55:453](15998372)) 
    * Synovial sarcoma (34%) ([Am J Clin Pathol 1999;112:641](10549251)) 
    * Ewing sarcoma (variable) ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2012;20:445](22495377)) 
    * Perivascular epithelioid cell tumors (less commonly than other melanocytic markers; 33%) ([Am J Surg Pathol 2005;29:1558](16327428)) 
    * Mycobacterial spindle cell pseudotumor ([Am J Surg Pathol 1992;16:276](1309172)) 
  * Brain: 
    * Glial tumors: astrocytoma, oligodendroglioma, ependymoma 
    * Choroid plexus papilloma and carcinoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 1986;10:394](3717496)) 
    * Meningioma (Positive in ~70% of fibrous variant) ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2015;23:215](25265429)) 
  * Macrophage lineage: 
    * Interdigitating dendritic cell tumors ([Am J Hematol 2007;82:725](17373675)) 
    * Langerhans cell tumors ([Am J Surg Pathol 2007;31:947](17527085)) 
    * Rosai-Dorfman disease ([Am J Surg Pathol 1992;16:122](1733347)) 
  * Epithelial / myoepithelial: 
    * Melanoma (95%, including desmoplastic and spindle cell tumors); S100- melanomas often had prior S100+ metastases, often are ocular ([Hum Pathol 2005;36:1016](16153466)) 
    * Invasive breast carcinoma (subset; particularly basal-like subtype) ([Contemp Oncol (Pozn) 2016;20:436](28239279)) 
    * Metaplastic breast carcinoma ([Diagn Pathol 2014;9:139](25030022)) 
    * Myoepithelial tumors: myoepithelioma, myoepithelial carcinoma 
    * Salivary gland tumors with myoepithelial differentiation: pleomorphic adenoma, myoepithelioma, basal cell adenoma, adenoid cystic carcinoma, polymorphous adenocarcinoma, epithelial-myoepithelial carcinoma, sialoblastoma ([Acta Histochem Cytochem 2012;45:269](23209336)) 
    * Mammary analogue secretory carcinoma ([Head Neck Pathol 2015;9:85](25078757)) 
  * Others: 
    * Paraganglioma / pheochromocytoma: sustentacular cells 
    * Neuroendocrine tumors (subset, often focal) ([Am J Surg Pathol 2005;29:1194](16096409)) 
    * Sex cord-stromal tumors (variable in any of this category) ([Mod Pathol 1997;10:693](9237180)) 

Negative staining

  * Mesenchymal: 
    * Perineurioma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2005;29:845](15958848)) 
    * Cellular neurothekeoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2007;31:329](17325474)) 
    * Fibroblastic reticular cell tumor ([Am J Surg Pathol 1998;22:1048](9737236)) 
    * Alveolar soft part sarcoma ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2015;139:1459](26516944)) 
    * Rhabdomyoma (may be focally positive) 
    * Sinonasal glomangiopericytoma ([Pathol Res Pract 2019;215:983](30739805)) 
    * Clear cell sarcoma of the kidney ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2019;143:1022](30628851)) 
    * Solitary fibrous tumor ([Iran J Pathol 2016;11:195](27799967)) 
    * Mammary type myofibroblastoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:361](26523539)) 
    * Cellular angiofibroma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2004;28:1426](15489646)) 
    * Gastrointestinal stromal tumor ([Transl Gastroenterol Hepatol 2018;3:27](29971258)) 
    * Benign fibrous histiocytoma ([Am J Dermatopathol 2001;23:104](11285404)) 
    * Atypical fibroxanthoma ([Anticancer Res 2015;35:5717](26503993)) 
  * Macrophage lineage: 
    * Follicular dendritic cell tumors (may show rare positive cases) ([Am J Hematol 2007;82:725](17373675)) 
    * Erdheim-Chester disease ([Blood 2014;124:483](24850756)) 
    * Juvenile xanthogranuloma (rare positive but generally negative) ([Am J Surg Pathol 2005;29:21](15613853)) 
    * Xanthoma 
  * Salivary gland tumors without myoepithelial differentiation: salivary duct carcinoma, mucoepidermoid carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma, oncocytoma / oncocytic carcinoma, Warthin tumor ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2015;139:55](25549144)) 
  * Squamous cell carcinoma 
  * Lymphoma

Practice question #2

A biopsied lung lesion is radiographically suspected to be a metastasis of unknown primary. Which staining profile suggests that the tumor is melanoma?   
  
  1. S100 positive, CK5/6 positive, p63 negative, GFAP negative 
  2. S100 negative, HMB45 positive, TTF1 negative, CK20 negative 
  3. S100 positive, HMB45 positive, CK7 negative, p63 negative 
  4. S100 negative, TTF1 positive, SOX10 positive, AE1/AE3 negative 
  5. S100 positive, CD21 positive, CD35 positive, Clusterin positive 

Practice answer #2

**C**. S100 positive, HMB45 positive, CK7 negative, p63 negative
