Definition / general

  * Insulinoma associated protein 1 
  * Previously abbreviated as IA1 

Essential features

  * Nuclear marker of neuroendocrine differentiation with equivalent or better sensitivity and specificity compared to [synaptophysin](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainssynaptophysin.html), [chromogranin](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainschromogranin.html) and [CD56](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/cdmarkerscd56.html)
  * Expressed in a broad spectrum of neuroendocrine tumors (including small cell carcinoma, large cell neuroendocrine carcinoma and well differentiated neuroendocrine tumor / carcinoid tumor of various sites, paraganglioma / pheochromocytoma, Merkel cell carcinoma, olfactory neuroblastoma and medullary thyroid carcinoma) 
  * Also expressed in normal adult neuroendocrine tissues (adrenal medulla, pineal gland, pituitary gland, gastrointestinal enterochromaffin cells, pancreatic islet cells, thyroid C cells) and developing neurons 

Pathophysiology

  * Zinc finger transcription factor originally isolated from pancreatic insulinomas ([J Biol Chem 1992;267:15252](1634555)) 
  * Transcriptional repressor that regulates entry into cell cycle and controls expression of neuroendocrine phenotype in normal tissues ([Biochem J 2006;397:169](16569215), [J Biol Chem 2009;284:5574](19124461)) 
  * Plays a vital role in development of normal neuroendocrine cells throughout body ([Genes Dev 2006;20:2465](16951258), [Development 2008;135:473](18094025), [J Comp Neurol 2008;507:1497](18205207), [Neural Dev 2011;6:6](21284846)) 
  * Regulates the development of neuroendocrine differentiation in various tumor types ([Am J Pathol 2015;185:3164](26482608)) 

Clinical features

  * Proposed as promoter for targeted gene therapy for [small cell lung carcinoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/lungtumorsmallcell.html) ([Cancer Gene Ther 2006;13:375](16052225)) 

Uses by pathologists

  * Identification of neuroendocrine differentiation in a wide range of tissues and tumor types ([Am J Clin Pathol 2015;144:579](26386079)) 
  * Diagnosis of high grade neuroendocrine carcinoma that is negative for synaptophysin and chromogranin ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:1561](28719469), [Am J Surg Pathol 2018;42:665](29438167)) 
  * Identification of immature neuronal differentiation in brain tumors and developing brain tissue ([J Neuropathol Exp Neurol 2018;77:374](29490065)) 
  * Differentiates carcinoid tumorlet from pulmonary meningothelial-like nodule ([Histopathology 2018;72:1067](29274092)) 
  * Differentiates extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma from most histologic mimics ([Mod Pathol 2018;31:744](29327709)) 

Prognostic factors

  * High expression of INSM1 is associated with increased progression free survival and chemosensitivity in small cell lung carcinoma ([Oncotarget 2017;8:73745](29088741)) 
  * Higher expression in midgut gastrointestinal neuroendocrine carcinomas with metastases compared to those not yet metastasized ([Am J Clin Pathol 2015;144:579](26386079)) 

Interpretation

  * Nuclear stain

Practice question #1

Which of the following tumors should be negative for INSM1?  
  
  1. Extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma 
  2. Middle ear adenoma 
  3. Parathyroid adenoma 
  4. Small cell carcinoma of the uterine cervix 
  5. Well differentiated pancreatic neuroendocrine tumor, grade 1 

Practice answer #1

**C.** Parathyroid adenoma. Although parathyroid neoplasms frequently stain with the common neuroendocrine stain chromogranin, they are consistently negative for INSM1.
