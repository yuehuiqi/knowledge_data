Definition / general

  * Rare, nonhuman papillomavirus (HPV) associated cervical neoplasm that is thought to derive from mesonephric / Wolffian remnants 

Essential features

  * Shows a variety of morphologic patterns, presenting a risk of misdiagnosis 
  * PAX8+, GATA3+, p16-, ER- immunoprofile; TTF1+ in a subset 
  * Frequently harbors _KRAS_ mutations 

Terminology

  * Mesonephric carcinoma of the cervix 
  * Mesonephric carcinosarcoma of the cervix 
  * Mesonephric adenocarcinoma of the cervix 

ICD coding

  * ICD-10: [C53.9](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/C00-D49/C51-C58/C53-/C53.9) \- malignant neoplasm of cervix uteri, unspecified 

Epidemiology

  * Rare, accounts for < 1% of all cervical adenocarcinomas ([Am J Surg Pathol 2018;42:214](29135516)) 
  * Mean age at diagnosis is 52 - 59 ([Gynecol Oncol Rep 2016;17:7](27354991), [Am J Surg Pathol 2021;45:498](33165093)) 
  * Unassociated with [human papillomavirus (HPV)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/cervixHPV.html) ([Am J Pathol 2000;157:1055](11021808), [Histopathology 2010;57:342](20727021)) 

Sites

  * Cervix, more common in lateral walls 

Pathophysiology

  * Unknown at this time 

Etiology

  * Thought to derive from mesonephric duct remnants / [hyperplasia](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/cervixmesonephrichyper.html) ([Mod Pathol 2015;28:1504](26336887)) 
  * Mesonephric remnants are found in the lateral cervical wall in up to 22% of adult uterine cervices ([Int J Gynecol Pathol 2002;21:327](12352182)) 

Clinical features

  * Most common clinical presentation is abnormal vaginal bleeding 
  * Can be detected by abnormal cervical cytology but less frequently than [cervical squamous cell carcinoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/cervixSCC.html) ([BMJ Case Rep 2012;2012:bcr0120125632](23230242), [Am J Surg Pathol 2021;45:498](33165093)) 
  * Can also present as cervical mass or incidentally 

Diagnosis

  * Cervical biopsy, endometrial curettage or hysterectomy specimen 

Radiology description

  * MRI demonstrates cervical mass with extension to the uterus, parametrium and upper vagina

Prognostic factors

  * Worse overall survival, disease specific survival, and progression free survival compared to HPV endocervical adenocarcinoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2021;45:498](33165093)) 
  * Majority present at FIGO stage IB or higher ([Gynecol Oncol Rep 2016;17:7](27354991), [Gynecol Oncol Rep 2020;34:100652](33024807), [Am J Surg Pathol 2021;45:498](33165093)) 
  * Many cases (half in one study) are associated with recurrence, commonly to distant sites ([Am J Surg Pathol 2021;45:498](33165093)) 
  * Stage I: recurrence rate 32%, mean recurrence interval of 24 months, mean overall survival of 50 months ([Gynecol Oncol Rep 2016;17:7](27354991)) 
  * Spindle component may portend a worse outcome ([Gynecol Oncol Rep 2016;17:7](27354991)) 

Case reports

  * 40 year old woman with _FGFR2_ mutation ([Int J Gynecol Pathol 2020;39:452](31335448)) 
  * 46 year old woman who presented with abnormal uterine bleeding and an enlarged cervix and was initially diagnosed with mesonephric hyperplasia ([Iran J Pathol 2021;16:227](33936235)) 
  * 48 year old woman with lung metastasis; tumor showed mutations in _KRAS_ and _CTNNB1_ and gain of 1q ([Diagn Pathol 2019;14:71](31266530)) 
  * 60 year old woman initially diagnosed with endometrial carcinoma ([Rev Bras Ginecol Obstet 2021;43:329](33784759)) 
  * 73 year old woman with _FGFR2_ mutation ([Cureus 2022;14:e25098](35733501)) 

Treatment

  * Mainstay of treatment is hysterectomy with or without bilateral salpingo-oopherectomy and pelvic lymph node dissection 
  * Roles of neoadjuvant or adjuvant chemotherapy and radiation therapy remain unclear 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Many histologic patterns, often varying between tumors and between different microscopic fields of the same tumor ([Am J Surg Pathol 1995;19:1158](7573674), [Int J Gynecol Pathol 2002;21:327](12352182)) 
  * Tubular pattern is most common; consists of small back to back tubules lined by cuboidal cells, containing eosinophilic intraluminal colloid-like secretions that stain positive for PASD and mucicarmine 
  * Ductal (pseudoendometrioid) pattern: glands lined by columnar cells, reminiscent of [endometrioid adenocarcinoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/uterusendometrioid.html)
  * Other reported histologic patterns: retiform, sex cord-like, papillary, hobnail, glomeruloid, solid, sieve-like, spindled 
  * May show a range of cytologic atypia with inconspicuous to prominent nucleoli ([Am J Surg Pathol 1995;19:1158](7573674)) 
  * Mitotic figures can be rare or readily apparent ([Am J Surg Pathol 1990;14:1100](2252101), [Am J Surg Pathol 1995;19:1158](7573674)) 
  * Often deeply infiltrative but may show little desmoplasia ([Am J Surg Pathol 1990;14:1100](2252101)) 
  * Often associated with peripheral mesonephric remnants or [mesonephric hyperplasia](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/cervixmesonephrichyper.html)
  * Mesonephric carcinosarcoma: high grade spindle cell component; heterologous osteosarcomatous and chondrosarcomatous differentiation have been described ([Am J Surg Pathol 1995;19:1158](7573674), [Am J Surg Pathol 2018;42:1596](30148742))

Cytology description

  * Clusters of epithelial cells in tubular, cribriform and sheet-like proliferations ([Cytopathology 2013;24:129](22300170)) 
  * Round to oval, occasionally angulated, small to medium sized nuclei with mild to moderate atypia and increased N:C ratio ([Cytopathology 2013;24:129](22300170)) 
  * Intraluminal eosinophilic material ([Cytopathology 2013;24:129](22300170)) 
  * Infrequently detected by cervical cytology compared to [cervical squamous cell carcinoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/cervixSCC.html) ([BMJ Case Rep 2012;2012:bcr0120125632](23230242)) 

Positive stains

Negative stains

Electron microscopy description

  * One case report demonstrated the presence of cytotelolysomes ([Pathol Res Pract 2008;204:671](18374498)) 
    * A distinctive feature of mesonephric epithelium ([Int J Gynecol Pathol 1985;4:245](2414240)) 

Molecular / cytogenetics description

  * Majority associated with mutations in _KRAS_ and gain in 1q, in contrast to [HPV associated endocervical adenocarcinoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/cervixhpvadenocarcinoma.html) ([Mod Pathol 2015;28:1504](26336887)) 
  * Some mesonephric carcinomas harbor mutation in actionable gene _KRAS_ G12C ([Diagn Pathol 2019;14:71](31266530), [Gynecol Oncol Rep 2020;34:100652](33024807)) 
  * Mutations in _NRAS_ , chromatin remodeling gene mutations (_ARID1A, ARID1B, SMARCA4_), _BCOR, TP53_ have also been described ([Mod Pathol 2015;28:1504](26336887)) 
  * Low tumor mutational burden ([Diagn Pathol 2019;14:71](31266530)) 
Sample pathology report

  * Uterus (with cervix), fallopian tubes and ovaries, total hysterectomy and bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy: 
    * Mesonephric adenocarcinoma (see comment and synoptic report) 
    * Comment: This tumor demonstrates several distinct histologic patterns (tubular, glandular and papillary) with associated peripheral mesonephric remnants. By immunohistochemistry, this tumor shows diffuse and strong expression of GATA3 and PAX8 and no expression of ER. Taken together, these features are diagnostic of mesonephric adenocarcinoma of the cervix.

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/cervixmesonephricadenohoang17.jpg

Which immunohistochemical staining profile would you expect in the cervical neoplasm depicted in this image?  
  
  1. PAX8-, ER-, GATA3+, AMACR+ 
  2. PAX8+, ER-, GATA3+, napsin A- 
  3. PAX8+, ER+, GATA3-, napsin A- 
  4. PAX8+, ER-, napsin A+, AMACR+ 

Practice answer #1

**B.** PAX8+, ER-, GATA3+, napsin A-. Mesonephric carcinomas of the cervix are typically PAX8+, ER-, GATA3+ and napsin A-. Answers A and D are incorrect because they typically show negative to focal staining for napsin A and AMACR.

Practice question #2

Which mutation is frequently harbored by mesonephric adenocarcinomas of the cervix?  
  
  1. _BRAF_
  2. _BRCA_
  3. _KRAS_
  4. _RET_

Practice answer #2

**C.** _KRAS_. Mesonephric adenocarcinomas of the cervix frequently harbor _KRAS_ mutations. Answers A, B and D are incorrect because _BRAF, BRCA_ and _RET_ mutations have not been identified in mesonephric adenocarcinoma of the cervix ([Gynecol Oncol Rep 2020;34:100652](33024807), [Mod Pathol 2021;34:1570](33772212), [Mod Pathol 2015;28:1504](26336887)).
