Definition / general

  * Rare, benign, polypoid lesion of cervix or vagina of young girls to adult women 
  * Treat with local excision; may recur, but good prognosis 

Terminology

  * Mesonephric papilloma may be similar entity ([Geburtshilfe Frauenheilkd 1986;46:654](3770416), [J Pediatr 1963;63:211](14043062))

Case reports

  * 18 month girl with Müllerian papilloma and multiple renal cysts ([Urology 2005;65:388](15708065)) 
  * 4 year old girl with recurrent cervical tumor ([J Pediatr Adolesc Gynecol 1998;11:29](9526823)) 
  * 49 year old woman with borderline malignant change in vaginal tumor ([J Clin Pathol 1998;51:875](10193336)) 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Superficially located, composed of papillary stalks covered by mucinous epithelium with focal squamous metaplasia 
  * Stroma is highly cellular fibrous tissue 
  * No atypia, minimal mitotic activity
