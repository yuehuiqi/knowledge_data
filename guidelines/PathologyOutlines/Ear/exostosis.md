# Ear

## External ear tumors - benign / nonneoplastic

### Exostosis

[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/earexostosis.html#definitiongeneral127582) | [Treatment](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/earexostosis.html#treatment127583) | [Gross description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/earexostosis.html#grossdescription127584) | [Differential diagnosis](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/earexostosis.html#differentialdiagnosis127585) | [Additional references](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/earexostosis.html#additionalreferences127586)

Definition / general

  * Also called surfer's ear 
  * Reactive, localized overgrowth of bone with a broad base 
  * Called osteoma if pedunculated 
  * Arises from wall of external auditory canal 
  * Usually multiple and bilateral; asymptomatic until large enough to block external auditory canal 
  * Highest incidence in cold water swimmers and surfers in Australia and New Zealand ([J Laryngol Otol 2004;118:348](15165308)) 

Treatment

  * Medical (for external otitis, antibiotics and aspiration), transmeatal surgical excision if medical treatment fails and patient is symptomatic ([ORL J Otorhinolaryngol Relat Spec 2003;65:189](14564090)) 

Gross description

  * Broad based, mound like bony proliferation resembling normal cortical bone (however pathologist usually only gets fragments) 
  * No bone marrow spaces 
  * Bone is covered by periosteum with overlying thin skin
