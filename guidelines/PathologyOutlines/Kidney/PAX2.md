Definition / general

  * Paired-box 2; nuclear transcription factor, structurally similar to [PAX8](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainspax8.html) with virtually identical expression in nonneoplastic tissue 
  * Essential in embryonic development of Müllerian organs and hindbrain 
  * Suppressed through promoter methylation at later stages of embryonic development but is reactivated during carcinogenesis ([J Cell Mol Med 2013;17:1048](23890189)) 

Clinical features

  * PAX2 mutations are associated with [kidney hypoplasia](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/kidneycongenital.html#hypoplasia) ([J Am Soc Nephrol 2001;12:1769](11461952)), including renal-coloboma syndrome ([Genet Test Mol Biomarkers 2013;17:786](23756089)) 

Uses by pathologists

  * Nuclear stain 
  * Cervix: help distinguish benign cervical mesonephric and Müllerian glandular lesions (PAX2+) from endocervical adenocarcinoma, including minimal deviation adenocarcinoma (PAX2-, [Am J Surg Pathol 2010;34:137](20061933)) 
  * CNS: differentiate [hemangioblastoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/cnstumorhemangioblastoma.html) (PAX2-, PAX8-, inhibinA+) from metastatic renal cell carcinoma (PAX2+ or PAX8+, inhibin-, [Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:262](21263247)) but hemangioblastoma is rarely PAX2+ ([Int J Clin Exp Pathol 2013;6:1953](24040464)) 
  * GU: determine whether tumors are of renal, Müllerian or Wolffian duct origin 
  * Kidney: renal cell carcinoma - confirm diagnosis in cytology specimens ([Diagn Cytopathol 2012;40:667](22807381)) 
  * Uterus: may be helpful in diagnosis of [EIN](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/uterusendometrialhyperplasiageneral.html) (usually negative, with background normal glands as internal positive control, [Am J Clin Pathol 2012;138:678](23086768))

Bladder / GU: nephrogenic adenoma

Positive staining - normal

  * Fallopian tube 
  * Prostate: epithelium of seminal vesicle and ejaculatory duct ([Hum Pathol 2010;41:1145](20413145)) 
  * Testis: rete testis 
  * Uterus: most epithelial cells of endocervix, endometrium ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1837](21989345)) 

Positive staining - disease

  * Müllerian tumors: variable sensitivity but less than PAX8 ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1837](21989345)) 
  * Cervix: mesonephric remnants and hyperplasia ([Am J Surg Pathol 2010;34:137](20061933)) 
  * CNS: medulloblastoma ([Int J Oncol 2012;41:235](22552444)) 
  * Fallopian tube: endosalpingiosis, paratubal cyst 
  * Kidney: renal cell carcinoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2008;32:1462](18685487)), primary and metastatic renal tumors, although PAX8 is more sensitive ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2012;136:1541](23194047)) 
  * Kidney: mixed epithelial and stromal tumor ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1264](21836481)), Wilm tumor (epithelial and blastema components, [Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1186](21730820)) 
  * Kidney: variable staining of B cell ALL and rhabdomyosarcoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1186](21730820)) 
  * Nephrogenic metaplasia / adenoma of GU tract ([Diagn Cytopathol 2008;36:47](18064687), [Mod Pathol 2006;19:356](16400326), [Mod Pathol 2013;26:792](23328975)) 
  * Urethra: clear cell adenocarcinoma 
  * Uterus: endometrial polyp, endometriosis 

Negative staining

  * Adenomatoid tumor 
  * Bladder: clear cell adenocarcinoma (usually) 
  * Kidney: angiomyolipoma with epithelial cysts and synovial sarcoma are negative but PAX2 and PAX8 often positive in epithelial cysts, likely due to entrapment and dilation of renal tubules ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1264](21836481)) 
  * Kidney: carcinoid 
  * Kidney: Ewing / PNET, neuroblastoma, T ALL 
  * Mesothelioma ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2012;20:272](22498671)) 
  * Testis: normal seminiferous tubules or interstitium; germ cell tumors, Leydig cell tumors, Sertoli cell tumors ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1473](21934480)) 
  * Uterus: usually (71%) loss of PAX2 staining
