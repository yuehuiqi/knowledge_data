Definition / general

  * Protease inhibitor used to treat HIV, strongly associated with direct nephrotoxicity ([Adv Chronic Kidney Dis 2010;17:72](20005491)) 
  * May cause tubulointerstitial nephritis due to intratubular precipitation of indinavir crystals, with damage to tubular epithelial cells and histiocytic reaction 
  * Tolerance may be higher in those from sub Saharan Africa versus US / Europe ([AIDS Res Hum Retroviruses 2007;23:62](17263634)) 
  * May resemble tuberculosis ([Braz J Infect Dis 2008;12:99](18553024)) 

Case reports

  * 49 year old man with HIV and indinavir induced nephrolithiasis 3 years after discontinuing indinavir ([Int Urol Nephrol 2011;43:571](20458537)) 

Treatment

  * Dose reduction ([Eur J Clin Pharmacol 2007;63:901](17690876)), discontinuation, urine acidification, early insertion of bilateral double J ureteral stents ([Int Urol Nephrol 2007;39:743](17180736))
