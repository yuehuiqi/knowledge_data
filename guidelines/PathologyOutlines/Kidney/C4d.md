Definition / general

  * Immunohistochemical (IHC) positivity for C4d in formalin fixed paraffin embedded (FFPE) tissue facilitates diagnosis of antibody mediated allograft rejection, bullous dermatoses, C3 glomerulopathy and placental maternal vascular malperfusion 

Essential features

  * Stable biomarker used by pathologists, mainly to evaluate antibody mediated rejection in solid organs 
  * Expression varies and depends on the organ 
  * C4d is not pathognomonic for antibody mediated rejection and should always be evaluated with clinical parameters 

Terminology

  * Complement component 4d (C4d) 

Pathophysiology

  * C4d is a product of the complement cascade with no known biological function or known receptors 
  * It is a reliable marker for complement activation and antibody mediated tissue damage because it is deposited near the activation site and remains stable, even in FFPE tissue 
  * Serum antibodies, such as donor specific antibodies in sensitized allograft recipients, activate the classical complement pathway by binding to C1q ([Kidney Int 2012;81:628](22297669), [Transplant Rev (Orlando) 2009;23:139](19362461))

Criteria for diagnosis of acute / active AMR

Clinical features

  * Graft dysfunction in association with positive / high serum donor specific antibodies (DSA) ([Hum Pathol 2022;127:67](35728694), [Am J Transplant 2016;16:2816](27273869)) 

Interpretation

  * Variable expression; depends on the disease and organ 
    * Liver: endothelial cells, strong linear staining of capillaries and veins 
    * Kidney: peritubular capillaries, strong linear staining 
    * Pancreas: interacinar capillaries 
    * Heart: capillaries 
    * Placenta: syncytiotrophoblast 
    * Bullous pemphigoid: dermoepidermal junction 
    * Esophageal pemphigus vulgaris: intercellular staining of keratinocytes ([Int J Surg Pathol 2025;33:337](39034277)) 
  * Depends on the methods 
    * Indirect immunofluorescence (IIF) on frozen tissue provides high specificity and sensitivity 
    * Immunohistochemical staining of FFPE tissue blocks provides high specificity but lower sensitivity ([Kidney Int 2012;81:628](22297669)) 

Uses by pathologists

  * Kidney glomerulopathies 
    * Intensity of C4d glomerular deposition is used to predict thrombotic microangiopathy risk in lupus nephritis ([Arthritis Rheum 2008;58:2460](18668574)) 
    * C4d staining was shown to correlate with focal segmental glomerulosclerosis (FSGS), demonstrating good sensitivity ([Ann Diagn Pathol 2024;70:152281](38417352)) 
  * Kidney transplantation 
    * Biomarker for diagnosis of active, chronic or chronic inactive antibody mediated rejection (AMR) 
    * Morphologic evidence of acute or chronic tissue injury, combined with presence of donor specific antibodies (DSA) and C4d positivity (by IHC or IF), supports a diagnosis of AMR ([Am J Transplant 2020;20:2318](32463180)) 
  * Pancreas transplantation 
    * Biomarker for diagnosis of AMR; it is positive in interacinar capillaries ([Am J Transplant 2024;24:362](37871799)) 
  * Heart transplantation 
    * Biomarker for diagnosis of AMR; it is positive in capillaries ([J Heart Lung Transplant 2005;24:1710](16297770)) 
  * Liver transplantation 
    * Biomarker for diagnoses of acute or chronic AMR 
    * Must be associated with circulating DSA and specific histologic findings such as sinusoidal congestion with endothelial swelling, sinusoidal dilatation, leukocyte margination and edema ([Am J Transplant 2016;16:2816](27273869)) 
  * Pregnancy specific dermatoses 
    * C4d positivity distinguishes pemphigoid gestationis (C4d+) from polymorphic eruption of pregnancy (C4d-) ([Clin Cosmet Investig Dermatol 2017;10:441](29184427), [Am J Dermatopathol 2013;35:787](24061402), [Obstet Gynecol 2022;140:679](36075066)) 
  * Placenta 
    * C4d deposition in syncytiotrophoblast is more frequently found in preeclampsia with fetal growth restriction and maternal vascular malperfusion 
    * Associated with higher risk of miscarriage cases ([Hypertension 2012;60:1332](23006730), [Virchows Arch 2015;466:717](25820373), [Front Immunol 2020;10:3098](32010144)) 

Prognostic factors

  * Kidney 
    * C4d+ patients had worse prognosis after kidney transplantation, regardless of AMR status ([Clin J Am Soc Nephrol 2015;10:1435](26071493)) 
  * Placenta 
    * Associated with higher risk of miscarriage and fetal growth restriction ([Hypertension 2012;60:1332](23006730), [Virchows Arch 2015;466:717](25820373)) 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Liver transplantation 
    * In AMR, linear or granular expression of C4d may be observed in the portal vein, sinusoids and endothelial cells of the central vein ([Am J Transplant 2016;16:2816](27273869)) 
  * Kidney transplantation 
    * C4d linear staining in peritubular capillaries (PTCs) or medullary vasa recta ([Am J Transplant 2020;20:2318](32463180)) 
  * Pancreas transplant 
    * C4d positivity in interacinar capillaries ([Am J Transplant 2024;24:362](37871799)) 
  * Heart transplant 
    * C4d positivity in capillaries ([J Heart Lung Transplant 2005;24:1710](16297770))

Diffuse C4d staining of interacinar capillaries

Positive staining - normal

  * Liver 
    * Background staining is described in arterial and perivenular elastic fibers, degenerated hepatocytes and sinusoidal fibrosis ([Am J Transplant 2016;16:2816](27273869)) 

Positive staining - disease

  * Antibody mediated rejection in liver allograft 
    * Strong, linear C4d staining involving > 50% of the circumference of portal veins and capillaries ([Am J Transplant 2016;16:2816](27273869)) 
      * Minimal: involves < 10% of portal tracts with C4d staining > 50% of the circumference of portal vasculature 
      * Focal: involves 10 - 50% of portal tracts with C4d staining > 50% of the circumference of portal vasculature 
      * Diffuse: involves > 50% of portal tracts with C4d staining > 50% of the circumference of portal vasculature 
  * Antibody mediated rejection in renal allograft 
    * Strong linear staining in peritubular capillaries and medullary vasa recta 
    * Indicative of AMR if ([Am J Transplant 2024;24:338](38032300)) 
      * C4d > 2 by immunofluorescence of frozen sections 
      * C4d > 0 by immunohistochemistry on FFPE tissue 
        * C4d0: no staining of peritubular capillaries (0%) 
        * Minimal (C4d1): C4d staining (> 0 but < 10% of peritubular capillaries) 
        * Focal (C4d2): C4d staining (10 - 50% of peritubular capillaries) 
        * Diffuse (C4d3): C4d staining (> 50% of peritubular capillaries) 
  * Antibody mediated rejection in pancreatic allograft ([Am J Transplant 2024;24:362](37871799)) 
    * 3 criteria 
      * C4d positivity in interacinar capillaries (≥ 1% of acinar lobular surface) 
        * Most data based on IHC 
      * Confirmed circulating DSAs 
      * Morphologic evidence of tissue injury (interacinar inflammation, acinar cell damage, vasculitis, thrombosis) 
    * Active AMR if all 3 criteria present 
    * Probable active AMR if 2 of 3 diagnostic criteria preset 
  * Antibody mediated rejection in cardiac allografts ([J Heart Lung Transplant 2005;24:1710](16297770)) 
    * C4d staining in > 50% of capillaries (detected by immunofluorescence or immunoperoxidase) 
      * Accompanied by CD68 positive intraluminal macrophages; may be associated with myocardial infarction 
      * C4d reacts strongly and diffusely with necrotic myocytes in cases of myocardial infarctions, while adjacent histologically normal myocytes are nonreactive, resulting in a clear delineation between damaged and viable myocardium ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2010;134:256](20121615)) 
  * Autoimmune bullous dermatoses 
    * Bullous pemphigoid ([Indian J Dermatol 2023;68:541](38099111), [Indian J Dermatol Venereol Leprol 2024;90:569](38595011)) 
      * Linear C4d staining at dermoepidermal junction with encircling of basal keratinocytes 
      * Direct immunofluorescence is gold standard 
      * Concomitant C3d positivity improves sensitivity in FFPE tissue 
  * Esophageal pemphigus vulgaris 
    * Intercellular C4d staining in basal layer ([Int J Surg Pathol 2025;33:337](39034277)) 
  * Pemphigoid gestationis 
    * With appropriate histology, C4d helps distinguish pemphigoid gestationis (C4d+) from polymorphic eruption of pregnancy (C4d-) ([Clin Cosmet Investig Dermatol 2017;10:441](29184427), [Am J Dermatopathol 2013;35:787](24061402)) 
  * Placenta 
      * Linear staining along syncytiotrophoblast 
      * Correlates with maternal vascular malperfusion 
      * Increased risk of miscarriage ([Virchows Arch 2015;466:717](25820373)) 
  * Pitfalls 
    * Both complement pathways can cause C4d activation and consequent deposition: classical (without antibody binding) or lectin (through carbohydrate molecules linked to various pathogens) ([Kidney Int 2012;81:628](22297669)) 
    * Liver transplantation 
      * Biliary strictures, hepatocyte injury, recurrent immune injury may show diffuse and intense C4d deposition in portal tracts 
      * No circulating DSAs ([Liver Transpl 2011;17:747](21542127)) 
      * Sparse or faint, linear or granular staining 
      * Hepatitis B and C, graft versus host disease (GVHD) and plasma cell hepatitis, especially in pediatric cases ([Transpl Immunol 2010;23:77](20230895)) 

Negative staining

  * Renal transplant 
    * C4d negative AMR (which has been described in chronic but not in acute settings); thus, C4d negativity does not necessarily exclude alloantibody mediated glomerular damage ([Am J Transplant 2007;7:1743](17564636)) 
  * Liver transplant 
    * C4d can be negative in cases of chronic AMR ([Am J Transplant 2016;16:2816](27273869)) 

Sample pathology report

  * Liver, allograft, biopsy: 
    * Acute antibody mediated rejection (AMR) (see comment) 
    * Comment: The liver biopsy shows portal edema, marked microvascular injury and marginated neutrophils. Immunohistochemistry for C4d shows strong and diffuse deposition involving > 50% of the circumference of portal vasculature. These findings, in the context of circulating donor specific antibodies, are compatible with AMR. 

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/stainsC4ddeMello03.jpeg

Immunohistochemical stain for C4d in a liver allograft biopsy showed the above pattern in the majority of portal tracts. Which of the following interpretations is the most appropriate?   
  
  1. C4d deposition in > 50% of the circumference of portal microvasculature 
  2. C4d deposition in < 50% of the circumference of portal microvasculature 
  3. No C4d deposition 
  4. Nonspecific C4d deposition in portal stroma and macrophages 

Practice answer #1

**A**. C4d deposition in > 50% of the circumference of portal microvasculature. There is linear C4d deposition along the entire circumference (100%) of the portal vein, which meets the criterion for > 50% involvement. Answers B and C are incorrect because there is linear C4d staining in the entire circumference of the portal vein. Answer D is incorrect because there is no C4d staining of portal stroma or macrophages.

This is an immunofluorescence for C4d on a kidney biopsy from a patient who underwent renal transplantation 3 weeks earlier. He developed oliguria and was found to have circulating donor specific antibodies (DSA). What does this image show?   
  
  1. C4d0 (extensive positivity in the renal interstitium) 
  2. C4d0 (marked background staining of collagen) 
  3. C4d0 (no staining of peritubular capillaries)

Practice answer #2

**D**. C4d3 (diffuse positivity in > 50% of peritubular capillaries). This immunofluorescence image demonstrates diffuse linear C4d staining involving > 50% of the peritubular capillaries, consistent with C4d3 positivity. Answers A and B are incorrect because the staining is not limited to background or interstitial collagen but clearly outlines peritubular capillaries. Answer C is incorrect because there is clear and extensive staining of the peritubular capillaries rather than no staining.
