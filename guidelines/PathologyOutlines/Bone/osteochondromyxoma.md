## Other chondrogenic tumors

### Osteochondromyxoma

**PubMed search** : [osteochondromyxoma](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/?term=osteochondromyxoma)  
  
[Definition / general](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/boneosteochondromyxoma.html#definitiongeneral26107) | [Treatment](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/boneosteochondromyxoma.html#treatment26108) | [Gross description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/boneosteochondromyxoma.html#grossdescription26109) | [Microscopic (histologic) description](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/boneosteochondromyxoma.html#microscopichistologicdescription26110)

Definition / general

  * Rare congenital bone tumor presenting with painless mass 
  * Associated with Carney complex (familial lentinginous and multiorgan tumor syndrome, [Am J Surg Pathol 2001;25:164](11176065)) 
  * **Sites** : nasal region, tibia, radius 

Treatment

  * Resection, but recurs if incompletely excised 

Gross description

  * Gelatinous, cartilaginous, bony 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Sheets and lobules of bland cells in myxomatous, cartilaginous, osseous, and hyaline fibrous matrix 
  * Low to moderate cellularity 
  * Erodes bone and frequently extends into soft tissue
