Definition / general

  * BCL6 transcriptional corepressor (BCOR) is located at Xp11.4 locus ([Genes Dev 2000;14:1810](10898795), [J Clin Pathol 2020;73:314](32161069)) 
  * Encodes a protein which is involved in transcriptional repression in association with BCL6 and epigenetic silencing through polycomb repressive complex 1 (PRC1) ([Epigenomics 2019;11:835](31150281)) 

Essential features

  * BCOR immunohistochemistry can be used diagnostically to separate tumors with _BCOR_ gene alteration (rearrangement or internal tandem duplication) from their histological mimics (mainly the group of small / ovoid round cell sarcomas) ([Pediatr Blood Cancer 2014;61:2191](25176412), [Histopathology 2020;76:509](31647130)) 

Pathophysiology

  * BCOR is a corepressor when bound to BCL6 ([Genes Dev 2000;14:1810](10898795)) 
  * BCOR forms part of the polycomb repressive complex 1 which ubiquitinates H2AK119 causing epigenetic silencing ([Biochem Soc Trans 2017;45:193](28202673)) 
  * Involved in early embryonic development and the differentiation of embryonic stem cells into ectoderm, mesoderm and hematopoietic lineages ([PLoS One 2008;3:e2814](18795143)) 
  * _BCOR-CCNB3_ fusion results from an X chromosomal paracentric inversion linking the _BCOR_ sequence to exon 5 of _CCNB3_ ([Histopathology 2018;72:320](28833375)) 

Clinical features

  * Associated with cancer of several locations and oculofaciocardiodental syndrome ([Nat Genet 2004;36:411](15004558)) 
  * Soft tissue / bone, kidney, uterus and brain are the most common tumor locations ([Am J Surg Pathol 2018;42:1128](29851702), [Histopathology 2018;72:320](28833375), [Pediatr Blood Cancer 2014;61:2191](25176412), [Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1670](27428733), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:1713](28877060), [Nat Genet 2012;44:461](22387997), [Histopathology 2020;76:509](31647130), [Am J Surg Pathol 2008;32:1228](18580489), [Am J Surg Pathol 2018;42:335](29200103), [Histopathology 2020;76:64](31846533), [Expert Rev Mol Diagn 2020;20:293](31917601)) 
  * _BCOR_ rearranged sarcomas show a striking male predominance and mainly occur in children / adolescents, although the uterine form can occur in the adult population ([Pediatr Blood Cancer 2014;61:2191](25176412), [Mod Pathol 2017;30:1251](28621321)) 

Interpretation

  * Distinct diffuse nuclear staining ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1670](27428733), [Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1009](26945340), [Histopathology 2020;76:509](31647130)) 

Uses by pathologists

  * Specific marker for _BCOR_ associated tumors 
  * Cannot distinguish _BCOR_ rearranged sarcomas from other round cell sarcomas with BCOR overexpression ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1670](27428733), [Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1009](26945340), [Histopathology 2020;76:509](31647130))

Positive staining - normal

  * Spermatogonia ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1670](27428733)) 

Positive staining - disease

  * Undifferentiated small round cell sarcomas with BCOR rearrangements in bone, soft tissue, abdominal / pelvic and visceral location: 
    * BCOR-CCNB3 gene fusion ([Am J Surg Pathol 2018;42:604](29300189)), ([Histopathology 2020;76:509](31647130)) 
    * _BCOR-MAML3_ gene fusion ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1670](27428733)) 
    * _ZC3H7B-BCOR_ gene fusion (chest wall) ([Histopathology 2020;76:509](31647130)) 
    * _BCOR-KMT2D_ gene fusion ([Am J Surg Pathol 2018;42:604](29300189)) 
    * BCOR internal tandem duplication ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1670](27428733), [Am J Surg Pathol 2018;42:1128](29851702), [Histopathology 2018;72:320](28833375), [Nat Commun 2015;6:8891](26573325)) 

Negative staining

  * Desmoplastic small round cell tumor ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1670](27428733)) 
  * Myxofibrosarcoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1670](27428733)) 
  * Ossifying fibromyxoid tumor ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1670](27428733), [Genes Chromosomes Cancer 2014;53:183](24285434)) 
  * Angiosarcoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1670](27428733)) 
  * Chondrosarcoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1670](27428733)) 
  * Low grade fibromyxoid sarcoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1670](27428733))
