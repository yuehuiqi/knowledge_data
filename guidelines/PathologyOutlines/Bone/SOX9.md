Definition / general

  * Sex determining region Y (SRY)-related high mobility groupbox 9 
  * Transcription factor linked to hedgehog pathways, plays a central role in development and differentiation of multiple cell lineages 
  * Downstream molecule of [beta-catenin](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainsbetacatenin.html)
  * Master regulator of chondrogenesis 

Clinical features

  * Poor prognostic factor for colon carcinoma ([Am J Clin Pathol 2008;130:897](19019766), glioma ([Med Oncol 2012;29:3484](22714060)), hepatocellular carcinoma ([Diagn Pathol 2012;7:44](22515642)), non small cell lung cancer ([J Exp Clin Cancer Res 2012;31:18](22385677)) 
  * Cervix: SOX9 methylation is frequently involved in cervical carcinogenesis ([Oncol Rep 2013;29:125](23064448)) 
  * Prostate: acts as critical downstream effector of ERG in TMPRSS2:ERG fusion+ prostatic adenocarcinoma ([J Clin Invest 2013;123:1109](23426182)) 
  * Soft tissue: expressed in Schwann cells from neurofibromatosis 1 tumors ([Hum Pathol 2011;42:434](21193222)) 
  * Testis: Sertoli cell maturation requires functional androgen/AR signaling to repress SOX9 and AMH expression; impairment of androgen/AR signaling promotes SOX9 mediated AMH production, leading to impairment of Sertoli cells in Sertoli cell only azoospermic patients ([PLoS One 2013;8:e76303](24098470)) 

Uses by pathologists

  * Distinguish mesenchymal chondrosarcoma (SOX9+) from small cell osteosarcoma (SOX9-, [Hum Pathol 2010;41:653](20138330))
