Definition / general

  * Homeodomain transcription factor of the NK2 family, playing a role in development and differentiation in the central nervous system and neuroendocrine cells ([Am J Surg Pathol 2012;36:993](22446943), [Mod Pathol 2016;29:370](26847175)) 
  * Downstream target of _EWSR1::FLI1_ ([Am J Surg Pathol 2012;36:993](22446943), [Mod Pathol 2016;29:370](26847175)) 

Essential features

  * Nuclear staining pattern; the majority of positive Ewing sarcoma cases have diffuse, strong staining ([Am J Surg Pathol 2012;36:993](22446943)) 
  * High sensitivity but not entirely specific for Ewing sarcoma ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2019;27:174](28800015), [Head Neck Pathol 2018;12:89](28616785)) 
  * Used as part of an immunohistochemical panel to differentiate Ewing sarcoma from mimickers ([Head Neck Pathol 2018;12:89](28616785)) 

Interpretation

  * Nuclear staining 
  * Staining in at least 5% of cells to be considered positive ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2019;27:174](28800015), [Am J Surg Pathol 2012;36:993](22446943)) 
  * The majority of positive Ewing sarcoma cases have diffuse (> 50%), strong staining ([Am J Surg Pathol 2012;36:993](22446943)) 

Uses by pathologists

  * Distinguish Ewing sarcoma from other small round blue cell tumors as part of a panel ([Mod Pathol 2016;29:370](26847175), [Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2019;27:174](28800015), [Am J Surg Pathol 2012;36:993](22446943))

Practice question #1

NKX2.2 is a downstream target of which gene fusion?   
  
  1. _CIC::DUX4_
  2. _EWSR1::FLI1_
  3. _EWSR1::WT1_
  4. _NAB2::STAT6_

Practice answer #1

**B**. _EWSR1::FLI1_. NKX2.2 is a downstream target of the _EWSR1::FLI1_ gene fusion associated with Ewing sarcoma. Answers A, C and D are incorrect because these fusions do not lead to an upregulation of NKX2.2 and are associated with solitary fibrous tumor (_NAB2::STAT6_), _CIC_ rearranged sarcoma (_CIC::DUX4_) and desmoplastic small round cell tumor (_EWSR1::WT1_).

Practice question #2

[ /stainsNKX2BaniakBRQ2.jpeg)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/stainsNKX2BaniakBRQ2.jpeg)

Which of the following diagnoses typically shows negative staining for NKX2.2?   
  
  1. Alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma 
  2. Ewing sarcoma 
  3. _EWSR1::NFATC2_ translocation associated sarcoma 
  4. Superficial neurocristic _FET::ETS_ fusion tumor 

Practice answer #2

**A**. Alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma is driven by _PAX3::FOXO1_ / _PAX7::FOXO1_ fusion and would be negative for NKX2.2. Answers B, C and D are incorrect because these tumors are diffusely positive for NKX2.2 in the majority of cases.
