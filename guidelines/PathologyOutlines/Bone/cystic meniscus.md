Definition / general

  * Lesion similar to ganglion but in menisci of knee 
  * Either at lateral or medial joint margin ([AJR Am J Roentgenol 2001;177:409](11461872)) 
  * Either confined to meniscus or extends beyond capsule 
  * Almost always associated with meniscal tears; may be due to trauma 
  * Cyst caused by simple extrusion of synovial fluid through meniscal tear 
  * Usually pain and swelling 

Case reports

  * 27 year old man with symptomatic posterior cruciate ganglion cyst ([Knee Surg Relat Res 2012;24:52](22570853)) 
  * 66 year old woman with tumor-like medial meniscal cyst ([Clinics (Sao Paulo) 2008;63:411](18568256))
