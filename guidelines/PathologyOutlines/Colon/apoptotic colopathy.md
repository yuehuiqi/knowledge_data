Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Reduced intracellular mucin, epithelial apoptosis and sloughing, increase in mitotic figures ([Hum Pathol 1998;29:972](9744314), [Cesk Patol 2011;47:130](21887933)) 
  * May have erosion of superficial epithelium, consistent with clinical impression of aphthoid ulcer 
  * Also increased lymphocytes and neutrophils ([Dis Colon Rectum 2006;49:109](16273330)) 
  * Patchy mononuclear infiltration in upper part of lamina propria, increased epithelial cell proliferation of individual crypts, basal cryptitis ([Cesk Patol 2010;46:37](21275224)) 
  * Rarely lamina propria edema or extravasated red blood cells
