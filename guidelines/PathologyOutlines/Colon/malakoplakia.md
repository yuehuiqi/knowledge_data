Definition / general

  * Marked histiocytic infiltrate, more common in [bladder](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/bladdermalakoplakia.html), that may diffusely involve colon 
  * Usually adults, associated with immunosuppression 
  * Due to defective inflammatory response to gram negative bacterial infection 
  * Rarely associated with colorectal carcinoma in elderly men ([Pathology 2002;34:332](12190290)) 

Case reports

  * 2 cases of pediatric malakoplakia of colon ([Pediatr Surg Int 2010;26:323](20101508)) 
  * 4 year old boy with coexisting celiac disease and chronic granulomatous disease ([Indian J Gastroenterol 2006;25:163](16877840)) 
  * 54 year old man with liver transplant ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2004;128:e133](15387686)) 
  * 58 year old woman with liver transplant ([Can J Gastroenterol 2007;21:753](18026580)) 
  * 64 year old man with colonic adenocarcinoma ([World J Gastroenterol 2007;13:6109](18023111)) 
  * 74 year old man with malakoplakia adjacent to adenocarcinoma ([J Clin Pathol 1993;46:959](8227417)) 

Gross description

  * Diffuse mucosal involvement and submucosal thickening; may cause mass 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Marked histiocytic infiltrate containing Michaelis-Gutmann bodies (calcospherites) that may resemble signet ring cells at frozen section
