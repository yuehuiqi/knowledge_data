Definition / general

  * Multisystem inflammatory disorder of unknown origin, with aphthous stomatitis, genital ulcers, relapsing iritis; also arthritis, cutaneous and vascular disease ([eMedicine #1](http://emedicine.medscape.com/article/1006358-overview), [#2](http://emedicine.medscape.com/article/329099-overview)) 
  * GI involvement in 10% (ileum, cecum); causes pain, diarrhea, melena; rarely perforation ([Surg Today 2003;33:383](12734737)) 

Epidemiology

  * More common in region from Mediterranean to eastern Asia 
  * 2 / 3 in males 

Case reports

  * 16 year old girl with colonic Behcet's disease with pyoderma gangrenosum ([World J Gastroenterol 2006;12:979](16521233)) 
  * 17 year old monozygotic male twins with concordant colonic Behçet's disease([J Gastroenterol 2005;40:421](15870978)) 
  * 17 year old boy with colonic perforation in Behcet's syndrome ([World J Gastroenterol 2008;14:6578](19030217)) 
  * 20 year old man with Behçet's disease with multiple colon perforations ([Turk J Gastroenterol 2010;21:324](20931444)) 
  * 21 year old man with multiple perforations along the transverse colon ([Clinics (Sao Paulo) 2009;64:1231](20037714)) 
  * 29 year old woman with severe colitis ([Am J Surg Pathol 1986;10:888](3789253)) 
  * 34 year old man with case resembling inflammatory bowel disease ([Intern Med 2004;43:243](15098609)) 
  * 37 year old woman with case with longitudinal ulcers and granulomas resembling Crohn's ([J Gastroenterol Hepatol 2002;17:105](11895563)) 
  * 77 year old man with colon cancer arising in an ulcer scar due to intestinal Behçet's disease ([Intern Med 2011;50:429](21372452)) 

Treatment

  * Steroids
