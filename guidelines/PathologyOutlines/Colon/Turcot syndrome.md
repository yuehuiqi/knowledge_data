Definition / general

  * Variant familial polyposis syndrome characterized by either [Familial Adenomatous Polyposis](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/colontumorFAP.html) (FAP) or [Lynch Syndrome](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/colontumorlynch.html) (LS), along with central nervous system tumors ([N Engl J Med 1995;332:839](7661930)) 

Essential features

  * Turcot syndrome encompasses either: 
    * Patients with an [APC mutation](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/colontumorAPCgene.html): FAP+ usually [medulloblastoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/cnstumormedulloblastoma.html)
    * Patients with a mismatch repair gene mutation: Lynch Syndrome + usually [glioblastoma multiforme](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/cnstumorglioblastoma.html)

Terminology

  * Pronounced with silent second t (Turcot was French Canadian) 

Clinical features

  * Same as FAP or Lynch Syndrome, but additionally with central nervous system tumor (as above) 

Case reports

  * 11 year old boy with colonic adenocarcinoma and glioblastoma multiforme ([Case Rep Oncol Med 2012;2012:720273](23119205)) 
  * 49 year old man with colon carcinoma and astrocytoma ([Neurol Med Chir (Tokyo) 2004;44:124](15095965)) 

Differential diagnosis

  * Constitutional mismatch repair deficiency syndrome: biallelic mismatch repair gene mutation; patients develop brain tumors, hematopoietic malignancies and Lynch syndrome associated tumors ([Haematologica 2010;95:699](20442441))
