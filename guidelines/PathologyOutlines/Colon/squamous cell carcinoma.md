Definition / general

  * Rare primary malignancy of colorectum, representing 0.1% of cases ([Am J Surg Pathol 1978;2:47](637188)) 

Essential features

  * Diagnosis requires finding no involvement of cloacogenic or squamous lined mucosa, no squamous cell carcinoma elsewhere and thorough sampling to exclude adenosquamous carcinoma ([Surg Today 1994;24:75](8054782)) 
  * Associated with ulcerative colitis, chronic colocutaneous fistula, schistosomiasis and colonic duplication ([Dis Colon Rectum 1988;31:228](3280272), [Cancer 1981;47:602](7226009)) 
  * Aggressive clinical course with frequent metastasis ([Saudi Med J 2006;27:874](16758054)) 

Sites

  * Most common in proximal colon and rectum ([Clin Colorectal Cancer 2001;1:55](12445380)) 

Etiology

  * May arise from squamous metaplasia ([Int J Surg Pathol 2009;17:340](18701516)) 
  * Seemingly not related to HPV infection, except in rare cases ([Eur J Surg Oncol 2002;28:657](12359204), [Gastroenterology 2001;120:988](11231953)) 

Clinical features

  * May cause hypercalcemia ([Dig Surg 2005;22:371](16424667)) 

Prognostic factors

  * Node negative cases have prognosis similar to colorectal adenocarcinoma ([Dis Colon Rectum 2001;44:341](11289278)) 

Case reports

  * 48 year old woman with squamous cell carcinoma arising in duplication of colon ([Cancer 1981;47:602](7226009)) 
  * 54 year old man with squamous cell carcinoma of colon with elevated serum SCC antigen ([Clin Colorectal Cancer 2001;1:55](12445380)) 
  * 59 year old woman with primary acantholytic squamous cell carcinoma of cecum ([Diagn Pathol 2011;6:5](21223553)) 
  * 60 year old woman with squamous cell carcinoma arising from longstanding colocutaneous fistula ([World J Gastroenterol 2005;11:5251](16127766)) 
  * Primary squamous cell carcinoma of colon arising in villous adenoma ([Hum Pathol 1988;19:362](3278968)) 

Treatment

  * Surgery, chemotherapy ([Dis Colon Rectum 1999;42:102](10211528))

Rectal squamous cell carcinoma

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Similar to squamous cell carcinoma in other organs (polygonal eosinophilic cells with intercellular bridges and keratin formation) 
  * Can be poorly differentiated 
  * May be basaloid or acantholytic
