Definition / general

  * Rare (0.1% of colonic carcinomas) WHO recognized epithelial malignancy of colon with glandular and squamous elements ([Am J Surg Pathol 1978;2:47](637188)) 

Essential features

  * Rare primary subtype of colorectal carcinoma 
  * Has higher metastatic rate and worse prognosis than conventional colorectal adenocarcinoma ([Dis Colon Rectum 2012;55:509](22513428), [Dis Colon Rectum 1999;42:258](10211505)) 

Sites

  * Typically arises in right colon ([Dis Colon Rectum 2001;44:341](11289278)) 

Pathophysiology

  * HPV infection does not appear to play a role ([Surg Today 2009;39:619](19562453), [Eur J Surg Oncol 2002;28:657](12359204)) 

Clinical features

  * Tumor stage is often advanced at presentation ([Dis Colon Rectum 1996;39:1265](8918436)) 
  * May cause paraneoplastic hypercalcemia ([Int J Clin Oncol 2005;10:144](15864702), [Am Surg 2001;67:585](11409809)) 
  * Can occur in patients with ulcerative colitis ([Dis Colon Rectum 1988;31:323](3282843)) 

Case reports

  * 71 year old man with adenosquamous carcinoma of sigmoid colon ([Int J Clin Exp Med 2013;6:390](23724159)) 
  * 78 year old woman with associated paraneoplastic hypercalcemia ([Int J Clin Oncol 2005;10:144](15864702)) 
  * Adenosquamous carcinoma of colon ([Am Surg 2006;72:754](16913323)) 
  * Adenosquamous carcinoma of colorectum ([J Exp Clin Cancer Res 2001;20:293](11484990)) 

Treatment

  * Surgery with adjuvant treatment

Sample pathology report

  * Ascending colon, biopsy: 
    * Adenosquamous carcinoma (see comment) 
    * Comment: The malignancy demonstrates both glandular elements and squamous elements. The former is positive for CDX2 by immunohistochemistry and the latter is positive for p40 by immunohistochemistry.
