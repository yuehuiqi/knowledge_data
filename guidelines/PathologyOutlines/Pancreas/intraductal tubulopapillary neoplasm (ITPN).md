Definition / general

  * Represents ~3% of intraductal neoplasms of the pancreas; characterized as an intraductal, grossly solid and cystic, tubule forming epithelial neoplasm with high grade dysplasia and absence of overt mucin production ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1812](21945955), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:313](27984235)) 
  * First described in 2004 by Tajiri et al. under the term intraductal tubular carcinoma; the 2019 WHO classification recognizes it as a distinct type of intraductal neoplasm of the pancreas ([Pancreas 2005;30:115](15714133)) 

Essential features

  * Highly cellular, complex tubular or cribriform growth pattern 
  * Intracellular mucin is typically not detectable or is very minimal 
  * Positive for MUC1 and MUC6; negative for MUC5AC 
  * Lacks genetic alterations commonly seen in intraductal papillary mucinous neoplasm 

Terminology

  * Older term is intraductal tubular carcinoma (not recommended) 

ICD coding

  * ICD-O 
    * [8503/2](https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/f63d7652-1254-47f4-8ab7-3c65ad60d57d/content?sequence=1#page=75) \- intraductal tubulopapillary neoplasm, high grade 
    * [8503/3](https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/f63d7652-1254-47f4-8ab7-3c65ad60d57d/content?sequence=1#page=75) \- intraductal tubulopapillary neoplasm with associated invasive carcinoma 

Epidemiology

  * Mean age: 60 years ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:313](27984235), [Histopathology 2022;81:297](35583805)) 
  * F:M = 17:14 ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:313](27984235), [Histopathology 2022;81:297](35583805)) 
  * Accounts for 3% of intraductal neoplasms of the pancreas ([Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1812](21945955)) 

Sites

  * 50% occur in the pancreatic head, 15% occur in tail and 30% involve entire gland ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:313](27984235), [Int J Clin Exp Pathol 2015;8:9672](26464736), [Histopathology 2022;81:297](35583805)) 
  * Also occurs in bile ducts ([Mod Pathol 2015;28:1249](26111977)) 

Etiology

  * No known etiological factors 

Clinical features

  * Nonspecific symptoms: abdominal pain, weight loss, nausea, vomiting, steatorrhea 
  * Jaundice is not common 
  * ~33% are detected incidentally ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:313](27984235), [Histopathology 2022;81:297](35583805)) 

Diagnosis

  * Surgical resection is needed to make the definite diagnosis 

Radiology description

  * 2 tone duct sign on CT image and cork of wine bottle sign on magnetic resonance cholangiopancreatography (MRCP) are characteristic imaging findings that represent intraductal growth ([J Comput Assist Tomogr 2012;36:710](23192209), [ Medicine (Baltimore) 2019;98:e14426](30732200), [Sci Rep 2022;12:15557](36114217)) 
  * Intraductal solid tumor within dilated main pancreatic duct mimics intraductal papillary mucinous neoplasm ([Diagn Interv Radiol 2019;25:251](31147310)) 
  * Irregular main pancreatic duct dilatation may lead to misdiagnosis of chronic pancreatitis ([Diagn Interv Radiol 2019;25:251](31147310)) 

Prognostic factors

  * Exhibits relatively indolent behavior, even if associated with invasive carcinoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:313](27984235), [Histopathology 2022;81:297](35583805)) 
  * With associated invasive carcinoma, the 5 year survival rate is 70% ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:313](27984235)) 
  * _TP53_ mutational status has been reported as a marker of adverse prognosis ([Mod Pathol 2022;35:1929](27984235)) 

Case reports

  * 40 year old woman who was found to have a pancreatic mass during her annual checkup ([Cureus 2023;15:e43006](37674950)) 
  * 59 year old man with jaundice and lower common bile duct stenosis ([Nagoya J Med Sci 2021;83:869](34916729)) 
  * 70 year old woman with an indistinct mass with small cysts and a dilated pancreatic duct ([Cureus 2023;15:e36932](37131554)) 
  * 76 year old man with weight loss found to have a 3 cm tumor within the main duct of the pancreatic head ([Clin J Gastroenterol 2025;18:376](39910028)) 
  * 85 year old man was with a 20 mm cystic mass at the head of the pancreas ([BMC Gastroenterol 2021;21:162](33849435)) 

Treatment

  * Treated primarily by surgical resection 

Gross description

  * Multinodular, polypoid, solid or fleshy mass within dilated main pancreatic duct ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:313](27984235), [ Acta Cytol 2016;60:267](27459312)) 
  * Cyst formation is less common 
  * Average size: 4 cm ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:313](27984235), [Histopathology 2022;81:297](35583805)) 
  * Multifocality may be seen ([Int J Clin Exp Pathol 2015;8:9672](26464736))

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Circumscribed nodules of back to back tubular glands surrounded by fibrotic stroma; however, the intraductal location of at least some of the nodules is evidenced by continuity of the neoplastic epithelium with nonneoplastic ductal epithelium ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:313](27984235)) 
  * Tubules are lined by cuboidal or columnar epithelium with a modest amount of eosinophilic or amphophilic cytoplasm 
  * Patchy and typically small papillae may also be observed ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:313](27984235)) 
  * Nuclei are small, round to oval and moderately to markedly atypical with readily identifiable mitotic figures 
  * Intracellular mucin production is typically not detectable or is very minimal ([Acta Cytol 2016;60:267](27459312)) 
  * Intraluminal secretions and necrosis, often with comedo-like pattern, are present 
  * Clear cell changes, cartilaginous or osseous metaplasia may be seen ([Diagn Pathol 2014;9:11](24443801), [Pathol Int 2012;62:339](22524663), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:313](27984235), [Pathol Int 2015;65:501](26119268)) 
  * Associated invasive carcinoma is seen in ~60% of cases as individual cells or small angulated nonmucinous glands surrounded by usually desmoplastic stroma but the majority (75%) do not have lymph node metastases ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:313](27984235), [Histopathology 2022;81:297](35583805))

Cytology description

  * Large cribriform and tubular clusters with luminal spaces and scanty intracytoplasmic mucin ([Diagn Cytopathol 2014;42:314](24339429), [Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:366](28055239)) 

Positive stains

Negative stains

Molecular / cytogenetics description

  * Generally lacks _KRAS_ , _GNAS_ and _BRAF_ mutations ([Mod Pathol 2017;30:1760](28776573), [Am J Surg Pathol 2009;33:1164](19440145), [Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1812](21945955), [J Pathol 2013;231:335](23893889)) 
  * _PIK3CA_ , _PIK3CB_ , _INPP4A_ , _PTEN_ may be mutated ([Mod Pathol 2017;30:1760](28776573), [Am J Surg Pathol 2009;33:1164](19440145), [Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:1812](21945955), [J Pathol 2013;231:335](23893889)) 
  * It has been shown that certain chromatin remodeling genes (_MLL1_ , _MLL2_ , _MLL3_ , _BAP1_ , _ARID2_ , _ASXL1_ , _PBRM1_ , etc.) may be mutated and a subset of the cases harbors _FGFR2_ and _STRN::ALK_ fusions ([Mod Pathol 2017;30:1760](28776573), [Mod Pathol 2022;35:1929](36056133)) 
  * Most harbor fusion genes including _FGFR2_ , _BRAF_ , _MAML2_ , _MAML3_ , _NRG1_ and _RET_ genes; some of these fusions might be actionable targets for therapy ([Mod Pathol 2017;30:1760](28776573), [Mod Pathol 2022;35:1929](36056133), [Gastroenterology 2023;164:1310](36801389), [Trends Cancer 2024;10:430](38378317)) 
  * Transcriptome analysis has suggested that most ITPN associated carcinomas display some squamous features ([Mod Pathol 2022;35:1929](36056133)) 

Sample pathology report

  * Pancreas and duodenum, pancreaticoduodenectomy: 
    * Intraductal tubulopapillary neoplasm of pancreas (ITPN) (see comment) 
    * Comment: The pancreas was submitted entirely for microscopic evaluation and no associated invasive carcinoma is identified. The neoplasm involves the main pancreatic duct and measures 4 cm in greatest dimension. Adjacent pancreas reveals atrophy. Surgical margins are free of neoplasm. 15 benign lymph nodes are identified (0/15). 

  * Pancreas and duodenum, pancreaticoduodenectomy: 
    * Intraductal tubulopapillary neoplasm of pancreas (ITPN) associated with an invasive ductal carcinoma (see comment) 
    * Comment: ITPN involves the main pancreatic duct. The entire tumor measures 5 cm in greatest dimension; the invasive carcinoma component measures 0.7 cm in greatest dimension (_suggestion for if multifocal invasion is present_ : the invasive carcinoma accounts for 25% of entire tumor and is estimated to measure 1.25 cm in greatest dimension). Adjacent pancreas reveals atrophy. Surgical margins are free of ITPN and invasive carcinoma. 14 benign lymph nodes are identified (0/14). 

  * Reference: [Ann Surg 2016;263:162](25775066)

Practice question #1

[/pancreasitpn_askanbasturk11.jpg)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/pancreasitpn_askanbasturk11.jpg) [/pancreasitpn_askanbasturk12.jpg)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/pancreasitpn_askanbasturk12.jpg)

Which of the following is true about intraductal tubulopapillary neoplasm (ITPN) of the pancreas?   
  
  1. It commonly has _KRAS_ , _GNAS_ and _BRAF_ mutations 
  2. It has a relatively indolent prognosis even when associated with invasive carcinoma 
  3. It is positive for MUC5AC 
  4. Jaundice is one of the most common symptoms 
  5. Papilla and mucin formation are common histologic features 

Practice answer #1

**B**. It has a relatively indolent prognosis even when associated with invasive carcinoma. ITPN has an excellent prognosis, even when associated with invasive carcinoma. Answer D is incorrect because jaundice is uncommon and nonspecific symptoms, such as abdominal pain, weight loss, nausea, vomiting and steatorrhea, may be present. Answer E is incorrect because intracellular mucin is typically absent or minimal and only focal papillary growth may be observed. Answer C is incorrect because ITPN is positive for MUC1 and MUC6 but negative for MUC5AC. Answer A is incorrect because ITPN lacks genetic alterations commonly seen in intraductal papillary mucinous neoplasm, such as _KRAS_ , _GNAS_ and _BRAF_ mutations.

Practice question #2

Which one of the following stains is negative in intraductal tubulopapillary neoplasm (ITPN) of the pancreas?   
  
  1. CK7 
  2. CK19 
  3. MUC1 
  4. MUC5AC 
  5. MUC6 

Practice answer #2

**D**. MUC5AC. ITPN is negative for MUC5AC. Answers A, B, C and E are incorrect because ITPN is positive for CK7, CK19, MUC1 and MUC6.
