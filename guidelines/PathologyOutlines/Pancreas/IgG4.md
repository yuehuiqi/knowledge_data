Definition / general

  * 6% of total IgG; least abundant of IgG subclasses 
  * IgG4 related sclerosing disease is a syndrome affecting various sites with elevated serum IgG4 and site-related lymphoplasmacytic infiltrates, increased IgG4+ plasma cells and sclerosis 
  * Common sites are head and neck [eosinophilic angiocentric fibrosis (possibly, [Am J Surg Pathol 2011;35:701](21502911)), idiopathic cervical (neck) fibrosis ([Am J Surg Pathol 2010;34:1678](20871392)), salivary glands ([Am J Surg Pathol 2010;34:202)](20061932)], liver ([Mod Pathol 2009;22:1287](19633647)), lung ([Am J Surg Pathol 2009;33:1886](19898222), [Am J Surg Pathol 2009;33:1450](19623032)), pancreatobiliary region (sclerosing pancreatitis, [N Engl J Med 2001;344:732](11236777)) and systemic ([Am J Surg Pathol 2010;34:1812](21107087)) 
  * Other sites: abdominal aortic aneurysms ([Am J Surg Pathol 2008;32:197](18223321)), breast ([Am J Surg Pathol 2009;33:1058](19384187)), CNS ([Am J Surg Pathol 2009;33:1249](19561447)), kidney ([Hum Pathol 2011;43:536](21889187)), lymph nodes ([Am J Surg Pathol 2008;32:671](18344866), [Mod Pathol 2011;25:480](22080064)), ureter ([Hum Pathol 2011;42:1178](21334715)) 
  * In ocular disease, associated lymphomas have numerous IgG4+ monotypic plasma cells ([Am J Surg Pathol 2008;32:1159](18580683)) 

Interpretation

  * Cytoplasmic staining 

Uses by pathologists

  * Diagnose IgG4 related sclerosing diseases, including some cutaneous pseudolymphomas ([Am J Surg Pathol 2009;33:1713](19701072)), although presence of numerous IgG4+ plasma cells by itself is not specific ([Mod Pathol 2011;24:1480](19701072)) 
  * Differentiate IgG4 related sclerosing disease (high number and percentage of IgG4+ plasma cells in tissue ) from inflammatory myofibroblastic tumor (low number and percentage, [Am J Surg Pathol 2009;33:1330](19718789), but see [Mod Pathol 2011;24:606](21297584)) or other lesions ([Am J Surg Pathol 2008;32:1770](18779730), [Hum Pathol 2010;41:643](20149413))
