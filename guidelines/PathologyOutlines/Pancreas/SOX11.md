Definition / general

  * SRY (sex determining region Y) related high mobility group (HMG) box 11 (_SOX11_) is a member of the _SOX_ gene family; specifically a member of the SOXC group of transcription factors 
  * Located at chromosome 2p25.2 ([Semin Cancer Biol 2020;67:3](32574812)) 

Essential features

  * Generally considered to be a marker for mantle cell lymphoma (MCL), although a subset of SOX11 negative MCL cases have also been described 
    * Should be interpreted in the context of histomorphology and other immunohistochemical results ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2014;22:720](25229384)) 
  * Prognostic value is debated 

Pathophysiology

  * Involved in regulating embryonic development and progenitor / stem cell behavior, including neurogenesis and skeletogenesis ([Semin Cancer Biol 2020;67:3](32574812)) 
  * Context dependent posttranscriptional modifications, in addition to interactions with cofactors and microRNAs, may influence its function in disease ([Semin Cancer Biol 2020;67:3](32574812), [Exp Hematol 2018;58:27](29158064), [Am J Cancer Res 2017;7:2305](29218252), [Genes Chromosomes Cancer 2016;55:531](26998831)) 
  * CCND1 and STAT3 may be involved in regulating SOX11 expression ([Blood 2019;133:306](30530749)) 
  * Role of _SOX11_ as an oncogene in MCL is currently an active area of research; proposed mechanisms of functioning include ([Leukemia 2022;36:583](34455421), [Ther Adv Med Oncol 2019;11:1758835919853449](31210798), [Curr Oncol Rep 2017;19:43](28466437)): 
    * Collaboration with cyclin D1 in the pathogenesis of MCL, generally together with genomic instability and the acquisition of secondary alterations ([Curr Opin Hematol 2018;25:299](29738333), [Curr Oncol Rep 2017;19:43](28466437)) 
    * Alteration of terminal B cell differentiation, with _PAX5_ as one of its major targets ([Blood 2013;121:2175](23321250)) 
    * Repression of _BCL6_ transcription to block cell entrance into the germinal center ([Leukemia 2016;30:1596](26710884)) 
    * Regulation of CXCR4 and FAK expression to promote MCL tumor cell homing and invasion ([Blood 2017;130:501](28533307), [Blood 2017;130:389](28751356)) 
    * Promotion of B cell receptor signaling ([Blood 2018;131:2247](29615403)) 
    * Association with an immunosuppressive microenvironment which could promote MCL progression ([Blood 2021;138:2202](34189576)) 
    * Driving proangiogenic signals via interaction with _PDGFA_ ([Blood 2014;124:2235](25092176)) 
    * Via a distant superenhancer ([Leukemia 2022;36:583](34455421)) 
  * In contrast, the role of SOX11 as a tumor suppressor has also been observed in several studies ([Leuk Lymphoma 2020;61:2068](32449421), [Ther Adv Med Oncol 2019;11:1758835919853449](31210798), [Tumour Biol 2015;36:6133](25773392), [Oncogene 2015;34:1231](24681958), [Mol Oncol 2011;5:527](21880559), [Mol Cancer 2010;9:187](20624318), [Cancer Res 2009;69:7953](19808959)) 

Clinical features

  * SOX11 expression by qPCR has been proposed as a molecular marker for measuring minimal residual disease in MCL ([Leuk Res 2019;78:1](30640013), [Med Oncol 2018;35:49](29520657), [Exp Hematol Oncol 2018;7:5](29484276), [Eur J Haematol 2012;89:385](22827557)) 
  * Limited preclinical work has evaluated the possibility of therapeutic targeting of SOX11 in MCL ([Clin Cancer Res 2021;27:4652](34158358)) 
  * Pathogenic variants have been implicated in Coffin-Siris syndrome or the related SWI / SNF related intellectual disability disorders (SSRIDDs) ([Genes (Basel) 2021;12:937](34205270), [Clin Dysmorphol 2021;30:44](33086258)) 

Interpretation

  * Staining in MCL should be nuclear and is often intense ([Blood 2008;111:800](17934069)) 
  * Variation exists in thresholds for positivity ([Haematologica 2015;100:e369](25887497)) 

Uses by pathologists

  * Nuclear expression can aid in the diagnosis of MCL, especially in cyclin D1 negative cases ([Haematologica 2009;94:1555](19880778)) 
  * Can be evaluated on decalcified tissue (formalin or B5 fixed) ([Hum Pathol 2017;59:94](27720733)) 
  * Performance characteristics of SOX11 immunohistochemical staining appear to vary depending on the antibody used, with a generally superior performance noted with a monoclonal antibody ([Am J Surg Pathol 2014;38:86](24145648), [Am J Clin Pathol 2013;140:795](24225745), [BMC Cancer 2012;12:269](22738398)) 
    * Specifically, clone MRQ-58 mouse monoclonal antibody demonstrated robust performance ([PLoS One 2019;14:e0225096](31714947), [Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2014;22:720](25229384)) 

Prognostic factors

  * The prognostic value of SOX11 status in MCL is debated and may vary depending on the clinical presentation (i.e., conventional versus leukemic nonnodal MCL) ([BMC Cancer 2021;21:209](33648463), [Blood 2012;119:4215](22431568)) 
    * In varying MCL populations, higher levels of SOX11 expression have been shown to correlate both with a better and a worse prognosis ([Blood 2018;131:417](29196411), [Oncogene 2015;34:1231](24681958), [Br J Haematol 2014;166:98](24684350), [Histopathology 2019;75:704](31173643), [Cancer Res 2010;70:1408](20124476)) 
    * Absence of SOX11 expression may be characteristic of indolent MCL; however, it is also noted that some SOX11 negative tumors may progress rapidly, corresponding to a transformed phase ([Cancer Res 2012;72:5307](22915760), [Cancer Res 2010;70:1408](20124476), [J Clin Invest 2012;122:3416](23023712)) 
    * Other studies have found no prognostic value for SOX11 in MCL or report loss of significance in multivariate analysis ([BMC Cancer 2021;21:209](33648463), [Blood 2012;119:4215](22431568)) 
    * Furthermore, studies evaluating SOX11 mRNA expression (rather than IHC) have also been performed; several have demonstrated higher SOX11 mRNA expression to be a good prognostic factor ([J BUON 2019;24:1679](31646825), [Leuk Lymphoma 2019;60:1420](30626249), [Eur Rev Med Pharmacol Sci 2018;22:2556](29771407), [Int J Hematol 2017;106:212](28429312)) 
  * Several possible reasons have been suggested for these conflicting results: 
    * Compounding additional morphologic or genetic factors, notably including _TP53_ mutations or 17p deletions ([Virchows Arch 2020;477:259](31975037), [Haematologica 2020;105:754](31296581), [Cancer Res 2012;72:5307](22915760), [Leukemia 2012;26:1895](22425896), [Blood 2012;119:4215](22431568)) 
    * Nonuniform cut off values for SOX11 positivity or variable antibodies used ([BMC Cancer 2021;21:209](33648463), [PLoS One 2019;14:e0225096](31714947), [J BUON 2019;24:1679](31646825), [Haematologica 2015;100:e369](25887497)) 
    * Varying methodologies for evaluating SOX11 expression ([BMC Cancer 2021;21:209](33648463)) 
    * Varying proportions of nodal and nonnodal MCL within studies ([J BUON 2019;24:1679](31646825), [Blood 2018;131:417](29196411)) 
  * Among other hematologic neoplasms, select publications have demonstrated the following associations with SOX11 expression via immunohistochemistry: 
    * Favorable prognosis in B cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia ([Sci Rep 2020;10:2043](32029838)) 
    * No significant impact on prognosis in Burkitt lymphoma, in a small cohort ([Leuk Lymphoma 2017;58:1760](27869516)) 
  * Studies analyzing expression via mRNA in acute myeloid leukemia and chronic lymphocytic leukemia have demonstrated a worse prognosis associated with higher SOX11 expression ([Leuk Res 2018;67:32](29428447), [Tumour Biol 2015;36:4433](25608839)) 
  * Among solid tumors, select publications have demonstrated the following associations with SOX11 expression via immunohistochemistry: 
    * Favorable prognosis in gastric cancer ([Int J Oncol 2014;44:1512](24604109)) 
    * Favorable prognosis in glioblastoma ([Br J Cancer 2013;108:2142](23619925)) 
    * Favorable prognosis in epithelial ovarian carcinoma, including high grade; of note, the first study referenced evaluated both nuclear and cytoplasmic staining ([Eur J Cancer 2009;45:1510](19272768), [BMC Cancer 2011;11:405](21943380)) 
    * Favorable prognosis in breast cancer (via immunohistochemistry) in one study; however, a worse prognosis was seen in another study (via immunohistochemistry), as well as in a third study (via mRNA) ([Asian Pac J Cancer Prev 2014;15:5483](25041022), [J Cell Physiol 2020;235:7295](32043610), [Oncotarget 2016;7:13106](26894864)) 
    * Favorable prognosis in aggressive fibromatosis ([Cancer Med 2014;3:81](24402778)) 
    * Adverse prognosis in endometrial cancer, using mRNA supported by IHC ([Am J Cancer Res 2017;7:2305](29218252)) 
    * Adverse prognosis in malignant melanoma ([J Int Med Res 2013;41:1221](23867449)) 
  * Hypermethylation in _SOX11_ , including promoter hypermethylation, has been demonstrated across various tumor types, with some prognostic implications noted ([Biochimie 2019;162:8](30935961), [Oncol Rep 2019;41:2351](30720133), [J Cancer 2018;9:2275](30026822), [Int J Exp Pathol 2017;98:341](29315911), [Cell Oncol (Dordr) 2015;38:183](25801783), [BMC Cancer 2015;15:273](25880212), [Cancer Cell Int 2013;13:109](24188789), [Cancer Epidemiol Biomarkers Prev 2011;20:1483](21586619), [PLoS One 2011;6:e21382](21738649), [Mol Cancer 2010;9:187](20624318))

MCL of the tonsil

Positive staining - normal

  * Not generally expressed in the nucleus of mature tissues; however, nuclear expression has been noted in Schwann cells, keratinocytes and other squamous or epithelial cells ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780), [Blood 2008;111:800](17934069)) 

Positive staining - disease

  * Mantle cell lymphoma (MCL; cyclin D1 positive and cyclin D1 negative): sensitivity ranged from 78 - 100% and specificity ranged from 72 - 100% in one meta analysis, with substantial heterogeneity in the reported specificities ([PLoS One 2019;14:e0225096](31714947)) 
    * Of note, nodal MCL is generally SOX11 positive, while leukemic nonnodal MCL is generally SOX11 negative; see [Negative staining](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainssox11.html#stainssox11negstain) below ([Am J Hematol 2019;94:710](30963600), [Virchows Arch 2016;469:697](27605053)) 
    * Specific subsets of MCL which have demonstrated SOX11 expression include pleomorphic MCL, secondary cutaneous MCL, MCL with mantle zone growth pattern ([Am J Surg Pathol 2020;44:232](31688139), [J Cutan Pathol 2016;43:354](26762898), [Am J Clin Pathol 2019;152:132](31140550)) 
    * Expression of SOX11 in in situ MCL is variable ([Haematologica 2012;97:270](22058203)) 
    * Expression of SOX11 in CD5 negative MCL ranged from 53 - 67% in the 2 studies referenced, with the latter noting no significant difference from its expression in CD5+ MCL ([Leuk Lymphoma 2022;63:911](34781807), [Am J Surg Pathol 2019;43:1052](31045891)) 
    * 2 cases of SOX11+ MCL with loss of SOX11 expression after ibrutinib treatment have been reported ([Leuk Lymphoma 2020;61:1769](32326800)) 
  * Among other hematopoietic neoplasms, expression is most commonly noted in: 
    * Lymphoblastic leukemia / lymphoma (B or T cell), approximately 33 - 100% with various antibody types used; association with certain genetic subtypes has been identified in the third study listed ([Haematologica 2009;94:1563](19880779), [Haematologica 2009;94:1555](19880778), [Sci Rep 2020;10:2043](32029838)) 
      * Of note, 0% (0/7) cases of T ALL were positive in one study ([Sci Rep 2020;10:2043](32029838)) 
    * Burkitt lymphoma: 20 - 100% with various subpopulations and antibody types used ([Br J Haematol 2022:196:681](34617271), [Ann Diagn Pathol 2013;17:250](23246412), [Haematologica 2009;94:1563](19880779), [Haematologica 2009;94:1555](19880778)) 
    * T cell prolymphocytic leukemia: 66% based on 3 cases ([Haematologica 2009;94:1555](19880778)) 
    * Hairy cell leukemia: 50% in the referenced publications with a correlation with cyclin D1 noted in both studies and weak nuclear staining noted in the latter study ([Mod Pathol 2010;23:105](19801969), [Haematologica 2009;94:1563](19880779)) 
  * Expression has been noted in the following solid tumors (variable thresholds for positivity and scoring systems used in referenced publications): 
    * Solid pseudopapillary neoplasm of the pancreas: 92 - 100% ([Cytopathology 2022;33:216](34816516), [Am J Clin Pathol 2017;149:67](29272888), [Cancer Cytopathol 2017;125:831](29045075)) 
    * Primitive neuroectodermal tumor / Ewing sarcoma: 100% ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Neuroblastoma: 100% ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Esthesioneuroblastoma: 100% ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Central neurocytoma: 100% ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Medulloblastoma: up to 100% but with some heterogeneity reported in the staining pattern of desmoplastic medulloblastomas ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780), [Pathol Res Pract 2016;212:965](27623204), [Neuro Oncol 2008;10:648](18577562), [J Neurooncol 2002;57:201](12125983)) 
    * Testicular germ cell tumor derived undifferentiated primitive neuroectodermal tumor: 100% ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2021;145:953](33290506)) 
    * Immature teratoma: 100% ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Myxoid liposarcoma: 100% ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Salivary duct carcinoma: 97% ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Diffuse astrocytoma: approximately 50 - 98% in the listed publications with differences noted by grade in the former but not the latter ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780), [Br J Cancer 2013;108:2142](23619925)) 
    * Glioblastoma ([Cell Oncol (Dordr) 2018;41:213](29218546)) 
    * Rhabdomyosarcoma: 90% ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Pulmonary small cell carcinoma (68%) and large cell neuroendocrine carcinoma (39%) ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Benign prostatic hyperplasia (approximately 82%) and prostatic carcinoma (approximately 0 - 17%) ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780), [Tumour Biol 2015;36:6133](25773392)) 
    * Malignant melanoma: approximately 50 - 63% ([Life (Basel) 2021;11:281](33801642), [J Int Med Res 2013;41:1221](23867449)) 
      * However, the publication referenced here noted staining in 0% (0/10) of malignant melanomas ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Gastric cancer: approximately 52% ([Int J Oncol 2014;44:1512](24604109)) 
    * Synovial sarcoma: 36% ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Breast cancer: nuclear staining in approximately 17 - 36% ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780), [Asian Pac J Cancer Prev 2014;15:5483](25041022)) 
    * Yolk sac tumor: 25% ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Malignant peripheral nerve sheath tumor: 24% ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Aggressive fibromatosis, although may be heterogeneous ([Cancer Med 2014;3:81](24402778)) 
      * Case report of papillary thyroid carcinoma with fibromatosis / fasciitis-like stroma with SOX11 expression exclusively in the mesenchymal component ([Virchows Arch 2019;475:519](31327063)) 
    * Serous ovarian carcinoma effusions, nuclear expression in any amount was identified in 37%; however, many of these cases demonstrated a limited extent of staining ([Hum Pathol 2015;46:1](25455994)) 
      * Another study demonstrated a mixture of nuclear and cytoplasmic staining in epithelial ovarian carcinoma ([Eur J Cancer 2009;45:1510](19272768)) 

Negative staining

  * Evolving literature suggests the division of MCL into 2 categories, conventional MCL and leukemic nonnodal MCL; the latter generally lacks SOX11 expression and has a more (though not uniformly) indolent disease course ([Hum Pathol 2022;119:59](34767860), [Exp Hematol Oncol 2021;10:41](34256839), [Am J Hematol 2019;94:710](30963600), [Am J Surg Pathol 2019;43:710](30768440), [Genes Chromosomes Cancer 2016;55:531](26998831), [Clin Cancer Res 2013;19:3121](23640973), [Cancer Res 2012;72:5307](22915760), [J Clin Invest 2012;122:3416](23023712), [Cancer Res 2010;70:1408](20124476)) 
    * One proposed explanation for some characteristics of these subtypes involves a functional effect of SOX11 on BCL6 ([Leukemia 2016;30:1596](26710884)) 
  * The following hematologic entities are generally negative, with rare exceptions: 
    * Normal hematopoietic cells ([Blood 2008;111:800](17934069)) 
    * Chronic lymphocytic leukemia / small lymphocytic lymphoma (CLL / SLL), including CLL / SLL with cyclin D1 positive proliferation centers ([Hum Pathol 2022;121:29](35066013), [Am J Clin Pathol 2012;138:132](22706868)) 
    * Marginal zone lymphoma - nodal (0%), extranodal (0%) and splenic (3%) ([Hum Pathol 2022;121:29](35066013)) 
    * Lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma / Waldenström macroglobulinemia (LPL / WM) ([Hum Pathol 2022;121:29](35066013)) 
    * MCL with plasmacytic differentiation, based on 4 cases ([Virchows Arch 2016;468:259](26454445)) 
    * Follicular lymphoma, with 2% of cases noted as positive in the first publication listed ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780), [Haematologica 2009;94:1563](19880779), [Haematologica 2009;94:1555](19880778)) 
    * Diffuse large B cell lymphoma ([Med Oncol 2012;29:1190](21479697), [Mod Pathol 2010;23:105](19801969), [Haematologica 2009;94:1563](19880779), [Haematologica 2009;94:1555](19880778)) 
      * However, the reference listed here noted staining in 46% (14/30) of diffuse large B cell lymphomas ([Ann Diagn Pathol 2013;17:250](23246412)) 
    * Plasma cell myeloma, including with t(11;14)(q13;q32) ([J Clin Exp Hematop 2015;55:137](26763361), [Mod Pathol 2010;23:105](19801969)) 
    * Classic Hodgkin lymphoma, with only weak positivity noted in 3% of cases in the third publication listed ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780), [Haematologica 2009;94:1563](19880779), [Haematologica 2009;94:1555](19880778)) 
    * MALT ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Several T cell lymphoma subtypes, excluding those listed above under [Positive staining](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/stainssox11.html#stainssox11posstain) ([Haematologica 2009;94:1563](19880779), [Haematologica 2009;94:1555](19880778)) 
  * The following solid tumors are noted to generally be negative, with rare exceptions (number of cases evaluated varies by tumor type) ([Histopathology 2019;74:391](30221780)) 
    * Low grade astrocytoma, pulmonary squamous cell carcinoma, pulmonary adenocarcinoma (2%), pulmonary large cell carcinoma (4%), pulmonary typical carcinoid, pulmonary atypical carcinoid (8%), esophageal squamous cell carcinoma, gastrointestinal adenocarcinoma (including stomach, colon, bile duct and pancreas), hepatocellular carcinoma, gastrointestinal neuroendocrine tumors, extrapulmonary small cell carcinoma / large cell neuroendocrine carcinoma (16%), adenocarcinoma of the female genital tract (cervix, uterus and ovary; 3%), cervical squamous carcinoma, certain germ cell tumors (including embryonic carcinoma, seminoma / dysgerminoma, choriocarcinoma and mature teratoma), renal cell carcinoma, urothelial carcinoma, prostatic adenocarcinoma, certain salivary gland tumors (including acinar cell carcinoma, adenoid cystic carcinoma (8%), mucoepidermoid carcinoma, epithelial myoepithelial carcinoma (18%) and myoepithelial carcinoma), nasopharyngeal carcinoma, adrenal cortical adenoma / carcinoma, thymoma / thymic carcinoma, papillary thyroid carcinoma, medullary thyroid carcinoma, parathyroid carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma of the skin / mouth, angiosarcoma (10%), leiomyosarcoma, well differentiated / dedifferentiated liposarcoma, dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans, solitary fibrous tumor, alveolar soft part sarcoma, epithelioid sarcoma, gastrointestinal stromal tumor, chondrosarcoma, osteosarcoma (12%) 
  * There is conflicting information regarding the following entity: 
    * B cell lymphoma, unclassifiable, with features intermediate between diffuse large B cell lymphoma and Burkitt lymphoma (of note, this is a diagnostic entity from the [2008 WHO Classification of Tumours of Haematopoietic and Lymphoid Tissues](https://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/9283224310/pathologyoutl-20), which is included given the publication dates of these studies) 
      * 83% (5/6) ([Ann Diagn Pathol 2013;17:250](23246412)) 
      * 0% (0/6) ([Haematologica 2009;94:1563](19880779))
