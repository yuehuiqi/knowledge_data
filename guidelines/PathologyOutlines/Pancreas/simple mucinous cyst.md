Definition / general

  * Mucinous pancreatic cyst with minimal cytologic atypia, > 1 cm in size and lined by a single layer of gastric type flat epithelium without ovarian type stroma 

Essential features

  * Unilocular or multilocular mucinous cyst (> 1 cm) with a single layer of columnar or cuboidal cells and low grade dysplasia; rarely communicates with pancreatic duct 
  * Paucicellular fibrotic wall with no ovarian type stroma or lymphoid band 
  * High cyst fluid carcinoembryonic antigen (CEA) and low amylase 
  * _KRAS_ , _KMT2C_ , _BRAF_ , _RNF43_ , _CDKN2A_ , _TP53_ and _SMAD4_ mutations in a subset of cases ([Hum Pathol 2020;101:1](32380013)) 
  * Rare progression to high grade dysplasia and even carcinoma ([J Pathol 2025;266:421](40371932)) 

Terminology

  * Also called mucinous nonneoplastic cyst, first proposed by Kosmahl et al. in 2002 ([Mod Pathol 2002;15:154](11850544)) 

ICD coding

  * ICD-10: [K86.2](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/K00-K95/K80-K87/K86-/K86.2) \- cyst of pancreas 

Epidemiology

  * 2 - 3.4% of pancreatic resections, 2.2 - 6.7% of pancreatic cyst resections and 7.6 - 10.3% of pancreatic mucinous cyst resections ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161)) 
  * Mean age: 64 (range: 20 - 88) ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161)) 
  * F:M = 2.8 - 4:1 ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966)) 

Sites

  * Body, tail (53 - 69%) 
  * Head, neck, uncinate process (31 - 47%) ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966)) 

Clinical features

  * Asymptomatic or incidental (41 - 49%), abdominal pain (34 - 44%), jaundice (4 - 7%), back pain (11%) ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966)) 
  * Size: 1 - 12 cm (mean: 2.5 cm) ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161)) 

Diagnosis

  * CT or MRI of abdomen 
  * Endoscopic ultrasound (EUS) with fine needle aspiration (FNA) or biopsy 
  * Cyst fluid analysis 
  * Cytology 
  * Surgical specimen ([StatPearls: Pacreatic Cysts [Accessed 25 August 2025]](30252235)) 

Laboratory

  * Cyst fluid: often elevated CEA, mean of 6,797 ng/mL (range: 11 - 64,000 ng/mL) ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966)) 
  * Low amylase (unless connected to duct) 

Radiology description

  * CT / MRI 
    * Unilocular (55 - 59%), multilocular or septated (41 - 45%) 
    * Rare communication with pancreatic duct (3 - 7%) or mural nodule ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966)) 
    * Upstream main pancreatic duct dilatation ([Abdom Radiol (NY) 2017;42:2827](28567485)) 
    * Low signal intensity of cyst fluid on T1 weighted, high on T2 weighted and low on diffusion weighted 
    * Most with thin cyst wall but can have cyst wall enhancement 
  * EUS 
    * Septate in 26.3%, solid component in 10.5%, possible communication with pancreatic duct in 21% ([Pancreas 2012;41:813](22695095))

Prognostic factors

  * Overall benign behavior with no recurrence or malignant transformation ([Am J Surg Pathol 2015;39:1730](26559377), [Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161)) 

Case reports

  * 50 year old woman with abdominal pain and a pancreatic cystic lesion ([Int J Surg Case Rep 2021;87:106468](34619455)) 
  * 52 year old man with hematochezia and active bleeding from the ampulla ([Korean J Gastroenterol 2017;70:301](29277093)) 
  * 61 year old woman with an incidental large pancreatic body cyst with mural calcifications ([Gastro Hep Advances 2022;1:952](39131253)) 
  * 63 year old man with abdominal pain and cystic lesion in the tail of the pancreas ([Virchows Arch 2021;479:179](33511431)) 
  * 64 year old man with a pancreatic cyst and progressive wall thickening ([Clin J Gastroenterol 2024;17:737](38519754)) 
  * 71 year old woman with a cystic tumor in the body of the pancreas ([World J Gastroenterol 2005;11:2045](15801005)) 
  * 75 year old man with pancreatic cyst ([J Med Case Rep 2019;13:264](31399149)) 

Treatment

  * Often treated with surgical resection due to preoperative imaging that is indistinguishable from other mucinous neoplasms 
  * Surveillance

Gross description

  * Unilocular or multilocular cyst, mean size of 2.5 cm (range: 1.0 - 12 cm) ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966)) 
  * No or rare communication with pancreatic duct ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966)) 
  * Usually clear or serous fluid, ~ 33% with mucinous fluid, rarely serosanguinous or thick, brown-green fluid ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966)) 
  * Smooth cyst lining, no or minimal focal papillary excrescences ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966)) 
  * Submit entire lining (or extensive sampling if large cyst) to rule out high grade dysplasia or invasive carcinoma

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Flat monolayer of columnar mucinous epithelium or attenuated cuboidal epithelial cells, often resembling gastric type mucinous epithelium ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966)) 
  * Low grade dysplasia, which may mimic low grade pancreatic intraepithelial neoplasia (PanIN) 
    * Focal high grade dysplasia in 8% cases in one study ([Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966)) 
    * Carcinoma developed in rare cases ([J Pathol 2025;266:421](40371932)) 
  * No or minimal papillary architecture ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966)) 
  * Paucicellular fibrotic wall with no ovarian type stroma or lymphoid band ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966)) 
  * Degenerative changes in cyst wall common 
    * Hyalinization, myxoid stroma, hemorrhage, calcifications, macrophage clusters, granulation-like tissue ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966))

Columnar epithelium

Cytology description

  * Flat honeycomb sheets / nests of cuboidal or columnar cells without significant atypia (100%) ([Pancreas 2013;42:27](22750967), [Pancreas 2012;41:813](22695095)) 
  * Round to oval nuclei, small to slightly enlarged, 1 - 2 inconspicuous nucleoli, fine granular chromatin, smooth nuclear contour, nuclear grooves (43.5%), nuclear pseudoinclusions (26.1%) ([Pancreas 2013;42:27](22750967)) 
  * Delicate, vacuolated cytoplasm (60.9%), variable in amount ([Pancreas 2013;42:27](22750967)) 
  * May show papillary architecture (10.5%), acini formation (10.5%), 3 dimensional clusters (5.3%), single cell pattern (5.3%), goblet cells (17.4%) ([Pancreas 2013;42:27](22750967)) 
  * Background mucin, macrophages (43.5%), rarely stroma (17.4%) ([Pancreas 2013;42:27](22750967)) 
  * Limitations: may not be representative of entire cyst lining 
  * Unremarkable mucin

Molecular / cytogenetics description

  * _KRAS_ mutations in 13 - 55% ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 2017;141:1330](28968161), [Am J Surg Pathol 2017;41:121](27740966), [J Pathol 2025;266:421](40371932)) 
  * _KMT2C_ (_MLL3_) mutations in 62% ([Hum Pathol 2020;101:1](32380013)) 
  * _BRAF_ , _RNF43_ , _CDKN2A_ , _SMAD4_ mutations in 8% ([Hum Pathol 2020;101:1](32380013)) 
  * _TP53_ mutations in 15% ([Hum Pathol 2020;101:1](32380013), [J Pathol 2025;266:421](40371932)) 
  * Loss of heterozygosity at 10q (_PTEN_) or 17q (ubiquitin E3 ligase ring finger 43 / _RNF43_) reported in 2 cases ([Hum Pathol 2016;55:159](27246174)) 
  * Clonality analysis with _HUMARA_ gene showed polymorphism ([Hum Pathol 2010;41:513](19954814)) 
  * No genetic mutations in 31% ([Hum Pathol 2010;41:513](19954814)) 

Sample pathology report

  * Pancreas, resection: 
    * Simple mucinous cyst without high grade dysplasia or carcinoma (see comment) 
    * Comment: Many of these lesions have _KRAS_ mutation. Although there is no consensus, many authors consider this entity a neoplastic process with rare cases of progression to carcinoma; however, most patients have a benign behavior with no recurrence or malignant transformation after resection. 

Differential diagnosis

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/pancreasmucnoncystBRQ1.jpg

A 62 year old woman presents with an incidental cystic lesion in the head / uncinate process of the pancreas on imaging. The cyst does not communicate with the pancreatic duct. Upon grossing of the Whipple resection specimen, a 4.6 cm multiloculated cyst with smooth lining is present in the head / uncinate. A representative H&E image of the cyst lining is shown. Which of the following is true regarding this entity?   
  
  1. High grade dysplasia is often present 
  2. _KRAS_ mutations have been demonstrated in a subset of cases 
  3. Often communicates with pancreatic duct 
  4. Ovarian type stroma is diagnostic of this entity 
  5. Papillary architecture is common 

Practice answer #1

**B**. _KRAS_ mutations have been demonstrated in a subset of cases. The image shows a pancreatic cyst lined by a single layer of gastric type epithelium and absence of ovarian type stroma. No cytologic atypia is present. The features are that of a simple mucinous cyst. Answer A is incorrect because simple mucinous cysts rarely show high grade dysplasia. Answer C is incorrect because simple mucinous cysts rarely communicate with the pancreatic ducts. Answer D is incorrect because ovarian type stroma is characteristic of mucinous cystic neoplasm. Answer E is incorrect because simple mucinous cysts are often lined by a single layer of epithelium and typically do not exhibit papillary architecture. Papillary features are more common in intraductal pancreatic mucinous neoplasm.

Practice question #2

A 2.3 cm multiloculated cyst with smooth lining located in the head of the pancreas was removed during surgery. It does not connect with the main pancreatic duct. The pathologist observes that the cyst lining is composed of a single layer of columnar mucinous cells with no papillary architecture or high grade dysplasia. The surrounding cyst wall is fibrotic and paucicellular with no ovarian type stroma. No ductal obstruction is present. What is the best diagnosis for this entity?   
  
  1. Intraductal pancreatic mucinous neoplasm 
  2. Mucinous cystic neoplasm 
  3. Pancreatic pseudocyst 
  4. Retention cyst 
  5. Simple mucinous cyst 

Practice answer #2

**E**. Simple mucinous cyst. The description of a pancreatic cyst that is > 1 cm, lined by a single layer of columnar mucinous cells with no papillary architecture or high grade dysplasia and demonstrates an absence of ovarian type stroma is that of a simple mucinous cyst. Answer A is incorrect because intraductal pancreatic mucinous neoplasm communicates with the pancreatic duct and more often exhibits papillary architecture. Answer B is incorrect because mucinous cystic neoplasm is characterized by the presence of ovarian type stroma. Answer C is incorrect because pancreatic pseudocysts do not have an epithelial lining and often show inflammatory changes within the cyst wall. Answer D is incorrect because a retention cyst is a cystic dilation of the pancreatic duct in the setting of ductal obstruction.
