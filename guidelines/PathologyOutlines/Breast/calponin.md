Definition / general

  * Actin filament associated regulatory protein 
  * h1 (basic) isoform is smooth muscle specific, but a late stage smooth muscle marker; described below 
  * h2 isoform is found in smooth muscle and non muscle cells; not described below 

Uses by pathologists

  * Identify myoepithelial cells in breast lesions ([Histopathology 2005;47:202](16045782)) 
  * Help differentiate breast collagenous spherulosis (positive) from adenoid cystic carcinoma (negative, [Mod Pathol 2006;19:1351](16810311)), although adenoid cystic carcinoma is calponin positive in salivary gland tumors ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 1999;123:801](10458827))

Positive staining - normal

  * Smooth muscle, myoepithelial cells (most but not all, [Breast Cancer Res 2003;5:R151](12927046)), myofibroblasts ([Am J Surg Pathol 2003;27:82](12502930), [Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A 1993;90:999](8430113)); also keratinocytes and nerve fibers ([Virchows Arch 2000;437:58](10963381)), choroidal non-vascular smooth muscle cells ([J Anat 2005;207:381](16191166)) 

Positive staining - disease

  * Atypical fibroxanthoma (30%, [Virchows Arch 2002;440:404](11956822)), benign fibrous histiocytoma (65%), collagenous spherulosis ([Mod Pathol 2006;19:1351](16810311)), DFSP (40%), fibromatosis ([Am J Dermatopathol 2006;28:105](16625070)), fibrosarcoma (60%), glomus tumor ([Am J Surg Pathol 2002;26:301](11859201)), leiomyoma, leiomyosarcoma, MFH of bone (47%, [J Clin Pathol 2002;55:853](12401825)), MPNST (40%), myoepithelioma-skin, myofibroblastic lesions, myofibroblasts in desmoplastic stroma of carcinomas, nodular fasciitis, solitary fibrous tumor (70%), synovial sarcoma ([Histopathology 2003;42:588](12786895)) 

Negative staining

  * Adenoid cystic carcinoma
