Definition / general

  * Histologic subtype of metaplastic carcinoma characterized by invasive atypical spindle cells arranged in short fascicles in a storiform pattern or long fascicles in a herringbone pattern 

Essential features

  * Invasive breast carcinoma with intermediate or high grade atypical spindle cell differentiation 
  * Displays evidence of epithelial differentiation by positive immunostaining with high molecular weight cytokeratins or p63 

Terminology

  * Acceptable: spindle cell metaplastic carcinoma 
  * Old terminologies not recommended: sarcomatoid carcinoma, carcinosarcoma, squamous cell carcinoma with pseudosarcomatous stroma ([Hum Pathol 1981;12:550](7275095)) 

ICD coding

  * ICD-O 
    * [8032/3](https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/f63d7652-1254-47f4-8ab7-3c65ad60d57d/content#page=63) \- spindle cell carcinoma, NOS 
    * [8575/3](https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/f63d7652-1254-47f4-8ab7-3c65ad60d57d/content#page=76) \- metaplastic carcinoma, NOS 
  * ICD-11: [2C6Y & XHORD4](https://icd.who.int/browse/2024-01/mms/en#1047754165%2Fother%20%26%20171327834) \- other specified malignant neoplasms of breast & metaplastic carcinoma, NOS 

Epidemiology

  * Rare 
    * 0.02 - 1% of all invasive breast cancers 
    * 0.2 - 1% of all breast cancers diagnosed in the United States 
    * 0.5 - 1.9% across the globe ([Clin Breast Cancer 2023;23:775](37179225)) 
  * F > M 
  * Median age range is 60 - 68 years old (older than [invasive breast carcinoma of no special type [NST]](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/breastmalignantductalNOS.html)) 

Sites

  * Any quadrant of the breast 

Pathophysiology

  * Unknown 
  * In metaplastic breast carcinomas, genetic studies have shown that morphologically distinct components share a clonal origin and demonstrate similar genetic profiles, suggesting that genetic alterations occur early in the tumor formation ([J Pathol 2010;220:562](20099298)) 
  * Others theorized tumor dedifferentiation in the later steps of tumorigenesis ([J Pathol 2011;224:434](21462188)) 
  * Possess abundant mesenchymal stem-like characteristics ([Clin Cancer Res 2022;28:2878](35507014)) 

Etiology

  * Multifactorial similar to triple negative breast cancers 

Clinical features

  * Presents like [triple negative breast carcinomas (TNBC)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/breastmalignanttripleneg.html)
  * Presents with larger tumor size, less nodal metastasis and higher grade than [invasive breast carcinoma of no special type (NST)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/breastmalignantductalNOS.html) ([Ann Surg Oncol 2007;14:166](17066230)) 
  * Calcifications are very rare and can be present in cases with concurrent ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS) or osseous metaplasia 
  * Most patients are stage III or stage IV disease at presentation due to large tumor size ([Ann Surg Oncol 2007;14:166](17066230)) 
  * Most common sites of metastases include lungs, followed by bone, liver and brain ([Cancers (Basel) 2023;15:2954](37296916)) 

Diagnosis

  * By histopathological examination 
  * Epithelial differentiation evidenced by unequivocal positivity for cytokeratins or p63 

Radiology description

  * Mammographic findings: similar to [invasive breast carcinoma of no special type (NST)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/breastmalignantductalNOS.html); oval, partially circumscribed to circumscribed dense mass ([J Clin Imaging Sci 2012;2:21](22616038)) 
  * Sonographic findings: similar to [invasive breast carcinoma of no special type (NST)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/breastmalignantductalNOS.html); variable appearance which can be oval, round, lobular or less frequently irregular, solid hypoechoic mass with circumscribed to noncircumscribed margins and can have posterior acoustic enhancement ([J Belg Soc Radiol 2018;102:46](30039058))

Prognostic factors

  * Associated with a worse prognosis, higher risk of recurrence and shorter disease free and overall survival compared to [invasive breast carcinoma of no special type (NST)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/breastmalignantductalNOS.html) ([Cancers (Basel) 2023;15:1556](36900347)) 
  * 5 year overall survival is lower than [triple negative breast carcinoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/breastmalignanttripleneg.html) ([Breast 2020;49:8](31675684)) 
  * Black women have a worse clinical outcome ([Cancers (Basel) 2023;15:2954](37296916)) 
  * Typically, chemotherapy resistant ([Clin Cancer Res 2022;28:271](34716198)) 
  * Less likely to have pathological complete response (pCR) to neoadjuvant treatment ([Clin Cancer Res 2022;28:2878](35507014)) 

Case reports

  * 24 year old woman with an ulcerated left breast lump ([IJS Open 2020;24:43](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijso.2020.03.005)) 
  * 40 year old man with a 7 cm right retroareolar recurrent breast mass ([J Cancer Res Ther 2023;19:2067](38376321)) 
  * 42 year old woman with a rapidly enlarging right breast mass ([Rare Tumors 2020;12:2036361320977021](33354306)) 
  * 52 year old woman with a rapidly growing breast mass initially diagnosed as malignant phyllodes tumor ([Breast Care (Basel) 2011;6:230](21779230)) 
  * 53 year old woman with a left breast mass with microcalcifications on breast imaging ([J Med Case Rep 2018;12:43](29463294)) 
  * 88 year old woman with a huge bleeding right breast tumor ([J Med Invest 2020;67:365](33148918)) 

Treatment

  * Treated the same as high grade [invasive breast carcinoma of no special type (NST)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/breastmalignantductalNOS.html)
  * Surgery with or without chemotherapy and radiotherapy 
  * Mastectomy or breast conserving surgery with axillary lymph node staging 
  * Poor response rate to nonsurgical treatments 
  * References: [ISRN Oncol 2012;2012:706162](22778998), [Am J Surg 2016;211:716](26830716)

Gross description

  * Large, firm mass with or without associated skin changes, ulcerations, nipple retractions or nipple discharge ([Am J Surg Pathol 2006;30:300](16538049), [Mod Pathol 2003;16:893](13679453)) 
  * Nodular, well circumscribed to infiltrative, white to gray to pink to tan, firm mass ([Mod Pathol 2003;16:893](13679453)) 
  * Necrosis or cystic degeneration may be present ([Jpn J Clin Oncol 1997;27:46](9070341), [Hum Pathol 1981;12:550](7275095)) 
  * Average size ranging from 4.4 - 7.5 cm ([Hum Pathol 1989;20:732](2473024), [Hum Pathol 1984;15:147](6199282))

Frozen section description

  * Fascicles of intermediate to high nuclear grade spindle to epithelioid cells arranged in a storiform pattern 
  * May have concurrent invasive ductal carcinoma, NST or ductal carcinoma in situ 
  * Absence of leaf-like fronds and subepithelial stromal condensation 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Atypical spindled to epithelioid cells in short fascicles forming a storiform pattern or in long fascicles forming a herringbone or interwoven pattern 
  * Intermediate to high nuclear grade with brisk mitosis differentiates it from fibromatosis-like metaplastic carcinoma 
  * Cell nuclei are pleomorphic with dispersed, closely clumped chromatin to vesicular and with prominent nucleoli 
  * Squamous metaplasia can be present 
  * Can be mixed with infiltrating ductal carcinoma of NST, ductal carcinoma in situ (needs thorough sampling) or squamous cell carcinoma 
  * Can be rounded or infiltrative 
  * Can exhibit inflammatory infiltrate including lymphocytes, plasma cells, histiocytes or scattered multinucleated osteoclast-like giant cells 
  * Can show myxoid change 
  * Can be associated with papillomas, complex sclerosing lesions or nipple adenomas 
  * Judicious use of multiple immunostaining to establish epithelial differentiation 
  * References: [Clin Lab Med 2023;43:221](37169444), [Asia Pac J Clin Oncol 2017;13:e72](25483573), [Am J Surg Pathol 2006;30:300](16538049), [Mod Pathol 2003;16:893](13679453)

Cytology description

  * Moderate to high cellularity 
  * Moderately to markedly pleomorphic atypical spindle shaped to epithelioid cells 
  * Arranged in 3 dimensional clusters or scattered singly 
  * Presence of different neoplastic cell types including ductal and squamous cells

Negative stains

Molecular / cytogenetics description

  * Uniformly claudin low gene expression profiles with lower expression of gene involved in epithelial mesenchymal transition ([Curr Issues Mol Biol 2023;45:6040](37504297)) 
  * _EGFR_ overexpression and mutations 
  * _PIK3CA_ / _PIK3R1_ and RAS / MAPK pathway aberrations enriched compared to other triple negative breast carcinomas 
  * Enriched with _TERT_ promoter mutations 
  * Decreased frequency of _TP53_ mutation compared to other subtypes 
  * Mutations in _NF1_ , _HRAS_ genes 
  * Low total mutational burden and were microsatellite stable 
  * PDL1 positivity was observed in some cases 
  * References: [Mod Pathol 2018;31:1661](29946183), [Clin Breast Cancer 2020;20:326](32197944)

Spindle cell carcinoma by Dr. Karah Leigh Roncin

Sample pathology report

  * Breast, left 3 o’clock mass, core needle biopsy: 
    * Spindle cell tumor, favor spindle cell metaplastic carcinoma (see comment) 
    * Comment: H&E section shows cores of tumor tissue composing of fascicles of moderately to markedly pleomorphic spindle cells forming vague storiform pattern. The cells possess oval vesicular nuclei, prominent nucleoli and indistinct abundant eosinophilic cytoplasm. Mitoses is 7 in 10 high power field. There is infiltration into fat present. No definite tubule formation is seen. No epithelial component is noted. Immunostaining shows these cells are positive for AE1 / AE3, p63, SMA and CK5/6. These are negative for desmin, CD34, ER (0), PR (0), cerBr2 (0) and with no nuclear staining for beta catenin. Ki67 index is ~30%. The features are those of a spindle cell tumor; the immunoprofile is more in favor of a spindle cell metaplastic carcinoma.

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/breastspindlecellcarcinomaMarabi13.jpg

A 65 year old woman presented with a 4 cm rapidly enlarging right breast mass. Breast imaging showed an oval circumscribed dense mass. Needle core biopsy reveals an atypical spindle cell proliferation as shown above. Which of the following features favors a spindle cell metaplastic carcinoma?   
  
  1. AE1 / AE3 positive high grade spindle cells with brisk mitoses 
  2. BCL2 positive spindle cells arranged in leaf-like fronds 
  3. Beta catenin positive spindle cells with rare mitoses 
  4. CD34 positive monomorphic spindle cells in a whorled pattern 

Practice answer #1

**A**. AE1 / AE3 positive high grade spindle cells with brisk mitoses. Judicious use of multiple cytokeratin stains and p63 is important as staining can be focal and patchy in spindle cell metaplastic carcinoma. Answer B, C and D are incorrect because these features describe phyllodes tumor, desmoid type fibromatosis and dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans, respectively.

Practice question #2

Which of the following is true of spindle cell metaplastic carcinoma compared to other subtypes of metaplastic carcinoma?   
  
  1. Decreased frequency of _TP53_ mutations 
  2. Enriched in PI3K and MAPK pathway aberration 
  3. Less likely to be triple negative breast cancer 
  4. Uniform claudin low gene expression profile 

Practice answer #2

**A**. Decreased frequency of _TP53_ mutations. Spindle cell carcinoma has a lower frequency of _TP53_ mutation compared to the other subtypes. Answers C is incorrect because spindle cell carcinoma is almost always triple negative (90% of cases). Answer D is incorrect because spindle cell carcinoma exhibits a more claudin low gene expression profile compared to other metaplastic carcinomas. Answer B is incorrect because spindle cell carcinomas more frequently harbor _PIK3CA_ / _PIK3R1_ mutations and RAS / MAPK pathway genetic aberrations than The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) triple negative tumors.
