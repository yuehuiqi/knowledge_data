Definition / general

  * Rare variant pattern of [invasive breast carcinoma of no special type (NST)](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/breastmalignantductalNOS.html), characterized by tumor cells showing clear cytoplasm comprised of abundant glycogen ([Am J Surg Pathol 1995;19:904](7611537)) 

Essential features

Terminology

  * Glycogen rich clear cell pattern 
  * Glycogen rich clear cell carcinoma (GRCC or GRCCC) ([J Clin Med 2019;8:246](30769905), [Arch Pathol Lab Med 2003;127:1629](14632562)) 
  * Glycogen rich carcinoma ([World J Surg Oncol 2008;6:44](18442419)) 
  * Clear cell carcinoma of breast (less specific) 

ICD coding

  * ICD-O: [8315/3](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/96612/9789241548496_eng.pdf?sequence=1#page=70) \- glycogen rich carcinoma 
  * ICD-10: [C50.919](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/C00-D49/C50-C50/C50-/C50.919) \- malignant neoplasm of unspecified site of unspecified female breast 
  * ICD-11 
    * [2C61.0 & XH7KH3](https://icd.who.int/browse/2025-01/mms/en#175963120%20%26%201605887153) \- invasive ductal carcinoma of breast & infiltrating duct carcinoma, NOS 
    * [2C6Y](https://icd.who.int/browse/2024-01/mms/en#1047754165%2Fother) \- other specified malignant neoplasms of breast 

Epidemiology

  * Rare; represents < 1 - 3% of all breast carcinomas ([Exp Ther Med 2021;21:524](33815597), [World J Surg Oncol 2008;6:44](18442419)) 
  * Typically diagnosed in women aged 30 - 60 years ([World J Surg Oncol 2008;6:44](18442419)) 
  * Reference: [Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2020;28:655](32167940)

Sites

  * Breast, accessory breast tissue 

Etiology

  * Hormonal, genetic and environmental factors, such as other breast cancers (see [invasive breast carcinoma of no special type](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/breastmalignantductalNOS.html)) ([World J Surg Oncol 2008;6:44](18442419)) 

Clinical features

  * Presents as palpable or imaging detected breast mass, occasionally with nipple discharge or skin changes ([J Surg Case Rep 2019;2019:rjz150](31110654)) 
  * Similar clinical presentation to [invasive breast carcinoma of no special type](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/breastmalignantductalNOS.html) ([J Surg Case Rep 2019;2019:rjz150](31110654)) 

Diagnosis

  * Histologic examination of tumor demonstrating clear cytoplasm in breast cancer cells ([Am J Surg Pathol 1995;19:904](7611537)) 
  * Positive PAS staining (diastase sensitive) indicating glycogen accumulation ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2020;28:655](32167940)) 

Radiology description

  * Mammography typically shows an irregular or oval mass, with or without calcifications ([Magn Reson Med Sci 2019;18:238](30175804)) 
  * Ultrasonography typically shows a complex cystic or solid hypoechoic mass ([Magn Reson Med Sci 2019;18:238](30175804)) 
  * Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) typically shows an enhancing mass lesion like invasive breast carcinoma of no special type ([Magn Reson Med Sci 2019;18:238](30175804))

Prognostic factors

  * 2019 U.S. Surveillance, Epidemiology and End Results (SEER) study of 155 cases (with a comparison group of 1,251,584 other breast cancers) showed ([J Clin Med 2019;8:246](30769905)) 
    * GRCC was more likely to be high grade, advanced stage and have triple negative biomarker status 
    * After adjustment for age, stage, grade, hormone receptor and HER2, surgery and radiation therapy status, survival remained significantly poorer for GRCC (p = 0.025) 
  * Other studies have suggested prognosis similar to [invasive breast carcinoma of no special type](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/breastmalignantductalNOS.html) when grade and stage matched 
  * Other specific features linked to prognosis of GRCC 
    * Axillary lymph node status as the strongest prognostic indicator ([J Surg Case Rep 2019;2019:rjz150](31110654)) 
    * AJCC stage and molecular subtype influence prognosis ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2020;28:655](32167940)) 

Case reports

  * 49, 57 and 67 year old women with features of GRCC on MRI ([Magn Reson Med Sci 2019;18:238](30175804)) 
  * 69 year old woman with GRCC with next generation sequencing ([Case Rep Oncol 2021;14:500](38352277)) 
  * Premenopausal woman with mixed papillary / GRCC breast carcinoma ([Int J Surg Pathol 2018;26:569](29580112)) 
  * Middle aged woman with GRCC with sentinel lymph node metastasis ([Int J Surg Pathol 2018;26:530](29254403)) 

Treatment

  * Surgical excision, sentinel lymph node excision, possible axillary lymph node dissection ([Breast Cancer 2005;12:189](16110288)) 
  * Radiotherapy is frequently recommended for patients undergoing breast conserving surgery or those with advanced stage ([Magn Reson Med Sci 2019;18:238](30175804)) 
  * Systemic therapy (chemotherapy, hormone therapy, targeted therapy) is primarily based on stage and biomarker status ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2020;28:655](32167940)) 

Gross description

  * Well defined or irregular mass, firm to hard consistency, with or without gritty microcalcifications ([Am J Surg Pathol 1995;19:904](7611537)) 
  * Typically white to tan, may have cystic or hemorrhagic areas ([Am J Surg Pathol 1995;19:904](7611537))

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Predominantly solid, sheeted, corded or nested growth patterns but occasionally tumor cells are arranged in papillary, micropapillary, lobular and tubular patterns ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2020;28:655](32167940)) 
  * Large polygonal epithelial cells with distinct cell borders and clear cytoplasm ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2020;28:655](32167940)) 
  * Cancer cells typically display moderate to marked nuclear pleomorphism with prominent nucleoli ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2020;28:655](32167940)) 
  * Intraductal component is frequently present and typically displays clear, glycogen rich cytoplasm ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2020;28:655](32167940)) 
  * May have scant intracellular mucin ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2020;28:655](32167940))

Cytology description

  * Hypercellular, loosely cohesive groups, some isolated cells ([Acta Cytol 2008;52:65](18323277)) 
  * Abundant finely granular eosinophilic to clear cytoplasm ([Acta Cytol 2008;52:65](18323277)) 
  * Well defined cytoplasmic borders ([Acta Cytol 2008;52:65](18323277)) 
  * Moderate to marked nuclear pleomorphism with prominent nucleoli ([Acta Cytol 2008;52:65](18323277)) 
  * Reference: [Diagn Cytopathol 2005;33:263](16138378)

Positive stains

Negative stains

Electron microscopy description

  * First described ultrastructurally in a study of 10 cases by Hull, et al. in 1981 ([Cancer 1981;48:2003](6271388)) 
  * Nonmembrane bound cytoplasmic glycogen pools ([Am J Surg Pathol 1986;10:553](3017138)) 
  * Tight junctions, immature desmosomes, occasional short microvilli ([Am J Surg Pathol 1986;10:553](3017138)) 

Molecular / cytogenetics description

  * _TP53_ mutations are common ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2020;28:655](32167940)) 
  * Possible PTEN downregulation and PIK3CA pathway alterations ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2020;28:655](32167940)) 
  * No significant _EGFR_ or _ERBB_ mutations ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2020;28:655](32167940)) 
  * Potential for targeted treatment (AR inhibitors, checkpoint inhibitors, epigenetic therapies, PIK3CA inhibitors, radiation) ([Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol 2020;28:655](32167940), [Case Rep Oncol 2021;14:500](38352277)) 

Sample pathology report

  * Right breast, upper outer quadrant, core biopsy: 
    * Invasive breast carcinoma with glycogen rich clear cell features, preliminary modified Bloom-Richardson grade 2 (3+2+2) (see comment) 
    * Comment: Stains show neoplastic cells to be positive (diffuse, strong) for keratin 7, GATA3 and PAS and negative for PASD, supporting the diagnosis. Controls are appropriate. Additional immunohistochemistry shows invasive carcinoma to be 
      * Estrogen receptor (71 - 80%, moderate) positive 
      * Progesterone receptor (< 1%) negative 
      * HER2 / neu (1+) negative for overexpression 
  
  * Left breast, wire localization segmental mastectomy: 
    * Invasive breast carcinoma with glycogen rich clear cell pattern, modified Bloom-Richardson grade 2 (3+2+2), 1.5 x 1.3 x 1.1 cm 
    * Ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS), nuclear grade 2 
    * Margins are negative (> 0.2 cm) 
    * Biopsy site changes 
    * AJCC stage (eighth edition): pT1c

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/breastglycogenrichJorns05.jpg

A histologic section of a breast mass shows a tumor with the pictured morphology. What staining pattern would support this special variant pattern of invasive breast carcinoma?   
  
  1. Keratin 7 positive, PAS positive, PASD positive 
  2. Keratin 7 positive, PAS positive, PASD negative 
  3. Keratin 7 positive, PAS negative, PASD negative 
  4. Keratin 7 negative, PAS positive, PASD positive 

Practice answer #1

**B**. Keratin 7 positive, PAS positive, PASD negative. The pictured tumor is glycogen rich clear cell carcinoma (GRCC) of the breast, which, like other breast carcinomas, is typically keratin 7 positive and contains abundant cytoplasmic glycogen, which stains PAS positive and is diastase sensitive (i.e., PASD negative). Answers A, C and D are incorrect because GRCC has tumor cells with abundant glycogen rich clear cytoplasm that is PAS positive and diastase sensitive or PASD negative. A lipid rich invasive breast carcinoma may have similar morphology but would be negative for both PAS and PASD and positive for lipid markers.
