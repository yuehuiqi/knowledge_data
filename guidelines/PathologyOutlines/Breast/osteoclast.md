Definition / general

  * Part of WHO classification 
  * Osteoclastic giant cells are present in stroma 
  * Presence of giant cells does not alter prognosis 

Case reports

  * 44 year old and 83 year old women ([Diagn Pathol 2010;23:55](20731838)) 
  * 46 year old woman ([Radiographics 2002;22:691](12006697)) 
  * 51 year old woman with pleomorphic carcinoma and prominent osteoclastic giant cells ([Pathol Int 2009;59:91](19154262)) 
  * Cases with neuroendocrine carcinoma ([Pathologica 2008;100:176](18841823)) 

Gross description

  * Often brown due to vascular stroma with hemosiderin 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Giant cells are associated with vascular stroma with extravasated red blood cells and hemosiderin, also chronic inflammatory cells and fibroblasts 
  * Giant cells have variable size and variable numbers of nuclei 
  * Similar histologic features in nodal metastases and recurrences ([Arch Pathol Lab Med 1986;110:636](3013119)) 
  * Carcinoma component may be any type

Cytology description

  * Abundant giant cells and mononucleated stromal cells associated with carcinoma cells ([Diagn Cytopathol 2005;33:246](16138376))
