Definition / general

  * Very rare primary breast carcinoma resembling skin adnexal tumor with sebaceous differentiation, but no evidence of cutaneous derivation (see [J Med Case Rep 2008;2:276](18706083) for cutaneous tumor of breast) 

Clinical features

  * Associated with Muir-Torre syndrome ([Cancer 2005;103:1018](15662714)) 

Case reports

  * 45 year old woman with extensive metastatic disease ([Pathol Int 2000;50:63](10692180)) 
  * 46 year old woman with Muir-Torre syndrome ([AJR Am J Roentgenol 2000;174:541](10658739)) 
  * 50 year old woman ([Pathol Int 2009;59:188](19261098)) 
  * 63 year old woman ([Virchows Arch 2006;449:484](16944238)) 
  * 83 year old woman with tumor of nipple ([J Cutan Pathol 2008;35:608](18466364)) 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Well defined solid sheets or lobules of atypical epithelial cells, including large, pale or clear cells with coarsely vacuolated cytoplasm, containing Oil red O staining lipid and often scalloped nuclei 
  * Often focal squamous morules
