Definition / general

  * Frequent tumor of the salivary glands; rare in breast ([Cancer Med 2023;12:10725](36916425)) 
  * Considered one of the variants of invasive breast carcinoma, specialized type 
  * Staged according to the criteria adopted for other types of breast carcinomas ([Virchows Arch 2004;444:13](14634807), [Histopathology 2019;74:463](30380176)) 

Essential features

  * Slow growing tumor with solid and cystic component, resembling salivary gland counterpart ([Pathology 2017;49:215](28043647)) 
  * Less chance of recurrence and metastasis; good overall survival 
  * Presence of basaloid, epidermoid and mucous cells 
  * Absence of true keratinization 
  * Triple negative and retains the mammary phenotype ([Histopathology 2019;74:463](30380176), [Int J Surg Pathol 2020;28:787](32362174)) 

Terminology

  * Invasive carcinoma breast, specialized type; mucoepidermoid carcinoma 

ICD coding

  * ICD-O: [8430/3](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/96612/9789241548496_eng.pdf?sequence=1#page=72) \- mucoepidermoid carcinoma 
  * ICD-11: [2C60 & XH1J36](https://icd.who.int/browse/2024-01/mms/en#457240160%20%26%20287052516) \- carcinoma of breast, specialized type & mucoepidermoid carcinoma 

Epidemiology

  * Women from 29 to 80 years old ([Breast Care (Basel) 2011;6:293](22135628)) 

Sites

  * Can involve any quadrant of breast, including the retroareolar region ([Pathology 2017;49:215](28043647)) 

Pathophysiology

  * Largely unknown; however, like mucoepidermoid carcinoma of salivary origin, a few hypotheses for mucoepidermoid carcinoma of the breast have been proposed 
  * Squamous metaplasia in columnar epithelium, followed by aberrant overgrowth of squamous and epidermoid cells 
  * _MECT1_ and _MAML2_ fusion genes known in salivary mucoepidermoid carcinoma (MEC) may sometimes be involved in the pathogenesis of breast mucoepidermoid carcinoma ([Cancer Med 2023;12:10725](36916425), [Histopathology 2019;74:463](30380176)) 

Etiology

  * Not well understood but like salivary counterpart, factors which may be implicated include 
    * Radiation exposure 
    * Genetic factors 
    * Chronic inflammation or infection of ducts and lobules 
  * May involve abnormal production of mucin from columnar cells of ducts and lobules 

Clinical features

  * Presents as a lump detected on palpation or screening mammography, similar to invasive breast cancer of no special type 
  * Lesion in retroareolar region may present with nipple discharge ([Pathology 2017;49:215](28043647)) 

Diagnosis

  * Histologic examination of [core biopsy](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/breastcore.html) or excised tissue 

Laboratory

  * Histological evaluation, followed by ancillary techniques for confirmation, such as immunohistochemistry and molecular analysis 

Radiology description

  * Presents as a circumscribed mass lesion on screening mammography, targeted ultrasonography 
  * Ultrasound may show cystic areas with irregular and heterogeneous echogenicity ([Medicine (Baltimore) 2022;101:e29745](35777033), [Case Rep Oncol 2016;9:806](28101030), [Breast J 2017;23:753](28397345)) 
  * Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) shows heterogeneous high signal intensity cystic masses with low signal intensity intramural nodules ([Medicine (Baltimore) 2022;101:e29745](35777033), [Radiology 2022;305:32](35787204))

Staging / staging classifications

  * Breast mucoepidermoid carcinoma is staged according to the criteria adopted for other types of breast carcinomas ([Virchows Arch 2004;444:13](14634807), [Histopathology 2019;74:463](30380176)) 

Prognostic factors

  * Grading is most important histopathological prognostic factor ([Virchows Arch 2004;444:13](14634807), [Histopathology 2019;74:463](30380176), [Breast Care (Basel) 2011;6:293](22135628)) 
  * Cases of low or intermediate grade mucoepidermoid carcinoma generally have favorable survival outcomes 
  * Recurrences presenting as high grade disease or isolated axillary metastases have been associated with survival in some cases ([Breast Care (Basel) 2011;6:293](22135628), [Eur J Gynaecol Oncol 2002;23:423](12440816), [Medicine (Baltimore) 2017;96:e9385](29390541)) 
  * Cases of high grade mucoepidermoid carcinoma with axillary or distant metastases generally exhibit poor survival outcomes ([Breast Care (Basel) 2011;6:293](22135628)) 

Case reports

  * 23 year old woman with left breast palpable mass, harboring _CRTC3::MAML2_ fusion ([Int Cancer Conf J 2024;13:481](39398906)) 
  * 37 year old woman with left breast nodule, 39 year old woman with nodule in outer upper quadrant of right breast and 40 year old woman with nodule in lower quadrant of right breast ([Medicine (Baltimore) 2023;102:e33707](37144989)) 
  * 47 year old premenopausal woman with painless palpable mass in right breast ([Medicine (Baltimore) 2022;101:e29745](35777033)) 
  * 47 year old woman with left breast mass ([Medicine (Baltimore) 2024;103:e37163](38394503)) 
  * Woman in her 50s with adenomyoepithelioma ([BMJ Case Rep 2022;15:e247281](35321911)) 
  * 60 year old postmenopausal woman with right breast nodule and _MAML2_ rearrangement ([Int J Surg Pathol 2020;28:787](32362174)) 
  * 67 year old African American woman with lobulated 3.1 cm left breast mass ([Gland Surg 2024;13:439](38601297)) 
  * 69 year old woman with burn scars ([Breast Care (Basel) 2011;6:293](22135628)) 
  * 71 year old woman with lymphoma ([Case Rep Oncol 2016;9:806](28101030)) 

Treatment

  * Surgical excision by lumpectomy or mastectomy, with or without axillary lymph evaluation via sentinel lymph node excision or node dissection ([Medicine (Baltimore) 2024;103:e37163](38394503), [Gland Surg 2024;13:439](38601297)) 
  * Adjuvant chemotherapy or radiotherapy, according to stage and extent of disease 

Gross description

  * Solid, cystic lesions 
  * Size ranges from 0.6 - 11 cm ([Breast Care (Basel) 2011;6:293](22135628))

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Similar to salivary gland counterpart, mucoepidermoid carcinoma of the breast shows diverse histological features, from low grade to high grade lesions ([Virchows Arch 2004;444:13](14634807), [Pathology 2017;49:215](28043647)) 
  * Glandular and squamoid differentiation, with mucous, intermediate and epidermoid cells 
  * Morphological patterns include columnar, clear cell or oncocytoid features 
  * True keratinization with squamous pearl formation is not seen, in any grade 
  * In situ component may be present ([Virchows Arch 2004;444:13](14634807), [Pol J Pathol 2013;64:210](24166608)) 
  * Either salivary or breast grading system followed (based on architecture, cellular atypia and mitotic count), with similar results ([Virchows Arch 2004;444:13](14634807), [Histopathology 2019;74:463](30380176), [Breast Care (Basel) 2011;6:293](22135628)) 
  * Low grade 
    * Well circumscribed 
    * Predominantly cystic growth pattern with less solid areas 
    * Cystic spaces lined by mucous cells and eosinophilic cells ([Pathology 2017;49:215](28043647)) 
    * Solid areas show neoplastic cell nests with peripheral basaloid cells, merging with epidermoid and mucous cells 
    * 0 - 1 mitotic figures / 10 high power fields (HPF) 
    * No necrosis 
  * Intermediate grade 
    * Only occasionally reported ([Medicine (Baltimore) 2023;102:e33707](37144989)) 
    * Like salivary counterpart, more solid growth pattern 
    * 2 - 3 mitotic figures / 10 high power fields 
    * Well circumscribed or infiltrative 
    * No necrosis 
  * High grade 
    * Any growth pattern but usually solid, with same cell composition as low grade 
    * Cytological atypia with ≥ 4 mitotic figures / 10 high power fields 
    * Usually infiltrative 
    * Necrosis present 
    * May have lymphovascular infiltration ([Breast Care (Basel) 2011;6:293](22135628))

Cytology description

  * Rarely reported in fine needle aspiration (FNA) cytology; cytological features expected to be similar to corresponding salivary gland lesions 
  * Cells dispersed and in small groups, with abundant eosinophilic cytoplasm, intermixed with mucinous and basaloid cells 
  * Mucous material can be present in the background 
  * Diagnosis can be missed in cytology, when salivary gland tumors are not considered in breast lesions ([Pathol Int 2006;56:549](16930336))

Molecular / cytogenetics description

  * Triple negative, lacking ER, PR expression and HER2 amplification ([Histopathology 2019;74:463](30380176), [Int J Surg Pathol 2020;28:787](32362174), [Cancer Med 2023;12:10725](36916425)) 
  * However, ER, PR and AR positivity were recently described in subpopulation of neoplastic cells of low and intermediate grade breast mucoepidermoid carcinoma ([Medicine (Baltimore) 2017;96:e9385](29390541), [Histopathology 2019;74:463](30380176)) 
  * Relatively less data on molecular features of breast mucoepidermoid carcinoma 
  * t(11;19) (q21;p13) translocation, resulting in _CRTC1_ (_MECT1_) and _MAML2_ fusion, found in 80% of salivary gland mucoepidermoid carcinoma cases, has not been detected consistently in breast mucoepidermoid carcinoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1151](27299797), [Cancer Med 2023;12:10725](36916425)) 
  * However, reports of _MAML2_ rearrangement and _CRTC1::MAML2_ fusion are known, while one case had harbored partial deletion of 11q21 (_MAML2_) ([Histopathology 2019;74:463](30380176), [Int J Surg Pathol 2020;28:787](32362174), [Hum Pathol 2009;40:887](19200580))

Practice question #1

https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/imgau/breastmalignantmucoepidermoidTomarMajumdar03.jpg

A 35 year old woman has had a left breast lump for 3 months. Lumpectomy shows the findings above on histopathology. Immunhohistochemistry of biomarkers is triple negative. Mucicarmine is positive on special stain. What is the diagnosis?   
  
  1. Adenomyoepithelioma 
  2. Mucinous carcinoma 
  3. Mucoepidermoid carcinoma 
  4. Secretory carcinoma 

Practice answer #1

**C**. Mucoepidermoid carcinoma. Mucin pools with intermediate cells are seen with mucicarmine positivity. Answer D is incorrect because secretory carcinoma lacks squamoid or epidermoid differentiation. Answer A is incorrect because epithelial and stromal components are not seen in adenomyoepithelioma. Answer B is incorrect because intermediate cells will be absent and mucinous carcinoma are not triple negative.

Practice question #2

Which of the following is correct regarding mucoepidermoid carcinoma of the breast?   
  
  1. _CRTC1::MAML2_ fusion is not consistently demonstrated 
  2. HER2 neu positive 
  3. Increased metastasis as compared with invasive breast cancer of no special type 
  4. Presence of keratin whorls 
  5. Unique morphology different from salivary gland counterpart 

Practice answer #2

**A**. _CRTC1::MAML2_ fusion is not consistently demonstrated. t(11;19) (q21;p13) translocation, resulting in _CRTC1_ (_MECT1_) and _MAML2_ fusion, seen in the majority of salivary gland mucoepidermoid carcinoma cases, has not been detected consistently in breast mucoepidermoid carcinoma ([Am J Surg Pathol 2016;40:1151](27299797)). Answer E is incorrect because the morphology resembles the salivary gland counterpart. Answer B is incorrect because mucoepidermoid carcinoma of the breast is triple negative but preserves mammary phenotype. Answer C is incorrect because there is less chance of metastasis. Answer D is incorrect because true keratinization is absent.
