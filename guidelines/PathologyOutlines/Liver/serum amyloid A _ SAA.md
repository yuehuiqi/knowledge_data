Background

  * Acute phase protein, which has diagnostic, therapeutic and prognostic implications in a wide variety of diseases 
  * First recognized in serum with its cross reactivity with antisera to amyloid AA of secondary amyloid; both proteins have precursor / product relationship 

Structure

  * 104 AA residues 
  * N terminal is usually 76 residues identical to protein AA 
  * Four genes encode SAA isoforms (_SAA1_ , _SAA2_ , _SAA3_ and _SAA4_); it spans 10 kb within the p15.1 region of chromosome 11 
  * Each gene encodes a different protein 
  * Synthesized in liver 
  * In blood, SAA is associated with HDL in acute phase response 
  * Expressed variants of protein and mRNA in different tissues, not only liver, also breast, colon, adipocytes, brain ([Curr Med Chem 2016;23:1725](27087246)) 

Role of SAA in pathogenesis of different diseases

  * Plays a role in inflammation 
  * Role in atherosclerosis: _SAA1_ and _SAA2_ promote early atherogenesis ([Curr Opin Lipidol 2016;27:531](27579547)); one of the early markers of preclinical atherosclerosis ([Vasa 2015;44:247](26314356)) 
  * Increased plasma level of SAA in type II diabetes ([Clin Biochem 1994;27:469](7535207)) 
  * Produced in the brain of patients with Alzheimer disease ([Neurosci Lett 1997;225:73](9147377)) 
  * One of the rheumatoid arthritis biomarkers ([J Med Life 2016;9:144](27453744)); mRNA concentration increases in synovial tissue of RA patients compared to normal ([Arthritis Rheum 2004;50:1788](15188355)) 
  * SAA mRNA is increased in adipocytes in obesity ([J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2005;90:2233](15623807)) 
  * May promote COPD ([Am J Respir Crit Care Med 2013;188:179](23627303)) 
  * Potential biomarker of neonatal sepsis ([Microb Pathog 2017;107:234](28377234)) 
  * Predicts severity of acute pancreatitis ([Crit Rev Clin Lab Sci 2015;52:273](26173077)) 
  * SAA role in cancer 
    * May be involved in pathogenesis of myeloma ([J Cell Biochem 2008;103:994](17849429)) and gastric cancer ([Ann Surg Oncol 2007;14:84](17063306)) 
    * Predicts prognosis of neoplasia ([J Clin Pathol 1986;39:794](3734116)) 
    * Circulating SAA is associated with prognosis in osteosarcoma patients ([Pediatr Blood Cancer 2017 May 24 [Epub ahead of print]](28544777)) 
    * Serum levels of SAA is upregulated in glioblastoma ([J Neurooncol 2017;132:383](28283801)) 

Role in pathology

  * SAA staining is fairly specific for sarcoidosis ([Respir Med 2017;126:1](28427539)) 
  * SAA is associated with earlier stages of chronic hepatitis ([Histopathology 2016;69:937](27302660)) 
  * Can be used in combination with other markers to classify hepatocellular adenoma into groups ([Front Med (Lausanne) 2017;4:10](28280721))
