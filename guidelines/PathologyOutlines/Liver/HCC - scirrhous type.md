Definition / general

  * Subtype of hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) designated by the WHO, with abundant fibrous stroma separating nests and trabecula of tumor cells 

Essential features

  * Unencapsulated, often subcapsular location, may have satellite lesions 
  * Abundant fibrous stroma (> 50% of tumor) but lacks central scar or radiating fibrous bands 
  * May mimic intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma on imaging and gross evaluation 

Terminology

  * Also called sclerosing variant of HCC 

ICD coding

  * ICD-10: [C22.7](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/C00-D49/C15-C26/C22-/C22.7) \- other specified carcinomas of liver 

Epidemiology

  * Incidence ranges from 0.2 - 4.6% of all HCC ([J Gastroenterol Hepatol 2006;21:1470](16911695)) 

Sites

  * Liver, often subcapsular 

Pathophysiology

  * Molecular alterations: _TSC1_ / _TSC2_ mutations ([J Hepatol 2017;67:727](28532995)) 
  * Associated with expression of transforming growth factor beta (TGFβ), epithelial - mesenchymal transition (EMT) and stem cell / progenitor related genes ([J Hepatol 2017;67:727](28532995), [Hepatology 2012;55:1776](22234953)) 

Etiology

  * Similar etiologic factors to [classic HCC](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/livertumorHCC.html)
  * May arise in cirrhotic and noncirrhotic liver ([Dig Dis Sci 2012;57:1698](22327241)) 

Clinical features

  * No differences in patient demographics, presence of cirrhosis or serum alpha fetoprotein (AFP) levels, in comparison with [classic HCC](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/livertumorHCC.html) ([Surg Pathol Clin 2013;6:367](26838979)) 
  * Association with hypercalcemia and hypophosphatemia has been described ([Liver 1981;1:33](6294435)) 

Diagnosis

  * Imaging modalities for diagnosis of HCC: multiphasic computed tomography (CT) or magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) 
  * Tissue biopsy is indicated if imaging not diagnostic of HCC 

Laboratory

  * Elevation in serum alpha fetoprotein (AFP) 

Radiology description

  * Radiographic appearance may be similar to [intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/livertumorcholangiocarcinoma.html)
  * Contrast enhanced CT scan shows peripheral ring enhancement in arterial phase and delayed central enhancement in venous phase ([Eur J Radiol 2009;69:123](17976942))

Prognostic factors

  * Comparison of prognosis with [classic HCC](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/livertumorHCC.html) shows conflicting results ([Surg Pathol Clin 2013;6:367](26838979)) 
  * Prognostic factors for HCC: stage (TNM), single lesion versus multifocal, size, vascular invasion, portal vein thrombosis, severity of underlying liver disease ([Liver Int 2009;29:502](19141028), [J Surg Oncol 2018;117:644](29127719)) 

Case reports

  * 60 year old man with cirrhosis and a 1.5 cm liver nodule ([World J Gastroenterol 2009;15:2296](19437576)) 
  * 67 year old woman with epigastric pain ([Clin Gastroenterol Hepatol 2009;7:A28](19008158)) 
  * 68 year old man with hereditary hemochromatosis ([Semin Diagn Pathol 2017;34:126](28143798)) 

Treatment

  * Surgical resection 
  * Radiofrequency ablation 
  * Transarterial chemoembolization (TACE) 
  * Transplantation (e.g., Milan criteria, modified by some institutions) ([Nat Rev Gastroenterol Hepatol 2017;14:203](28053342)) 

Gross description

  * Unencapsulated, gray-white, firm, lobulated mass, often with serrated border 
  * Often subcapsular, may have satellite nodules 
  * More extensive fibrosis than [fibrolamellar variant](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/livertumorfibrolamellar.html); no radiating fibrous bands, no central scar 
  * Gross appearance may be similar to [intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/livertumorcholangiocarcinoma.html)
  * Reference: [Surg Pathol Clin 2013;6:367](26838979)

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Abundant fibrous stroma separating nests / trabeculae of tumor cells 
  * Criteria for amount of intratumoral fibrosis is > 50% ([Gastroenterol Clin North Am 2017;46:365](28506370)) 
  * Absence of fibrous capsule, no necrosis or hemorrhage, may contain intratumoral portal tracts and prominent tumor infiltrating lymphocytes ([Pathol Int 2005;55:724](16271085), [Hepatogastroenterology 2009;56:1086](19760947)) 
  * Clear cell change, steatotic change and hyaline bodies may be present ([J Gastroenterol Hepatol 2006;21:1470](16911695), [Hum Pathol 2019;86:222](30597153)) 
  * Less frequently poorly differentiated and more likely to have steatosis than nonscirrhous HCC ([Hum Pathol 2019;86:222](30597153))

  * Cells are polygonal with distinct cell membranes, high N:C ratio and round nuclei and may have prominent nucleoli (similar to [moderately differentiated HCC](https://www.pathologyoutlines.com/topic/livertumorhcc.html))

Molecular / cytogenetics description

  * Associated with _TSC1_ / _TSC2_ mutations ([J Hepatol 2017;67:727](28532995)) 

Sample pathology report

  * Liver, mass, partial hepatectomy: 
    * Scirrhous hepatocellular carcinoma (see comment) 
    * Comment: This tumor shows histologic features of scirrhous hepatocellular carcinoma with nests and trabecula of hepatoid cells within a dense fibrous stroma. The tumor cells show positive staining for glypican 3, arginase1 and CK7, as is typical for this variant.
