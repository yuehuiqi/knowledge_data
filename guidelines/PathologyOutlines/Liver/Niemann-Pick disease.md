Definition / general

  * Autosomal recessive lysosomal storage disease clinically characterized by neurodegeneration and liver disease ([Wikipedia: Niemann-Pick Disease [Accessed 29 January 2024]](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niemann–Pick_disease)) 
  * Mutations in the _NPC1_ and _NPC2_ genes cause impaired egress of free cholesterol from lysosomes, leading to accumulation of cholesterol and glycosphingolipids 

Microscopic (histologic) description

  * Foamy Kupffer cells and hepatocytes
